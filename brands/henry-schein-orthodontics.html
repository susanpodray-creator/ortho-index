Henry Schein Orthodontics

Orthodontics business of Henry Schein, Inc.—Carriere Motion bite correctors and SLX 3D self-ligating brackets, Aria twins, Smilers aligners, and the historic Ortho Organizers / Ortho Technology catalogs—now ordered through Henry Schein Dental.

HQ: Savannah, Georgia, USA · Researched 2026-08-26 · Official site

Overview

Henry Schein Orthodontics (HSO) is still the official public name. It is the orthodontics business of Henry Schein, Inc. (Nasdaq: HSIC), not a standalone company and not a Carriere rename. The marketing site remains henryscheinortho.com. June 26, 2026 and April 29, 2026 Henry Schein newsroom releases still open with “Henry Schein Orthodontics (HSO), the orthodontics business of Henry Schein, Inc.” There is no public sale of HSO in 2025–2026 and no announced house-name change to Carriere.

Carriere® is a product system inside HSO, not the parent brand. Official pages title the philosophy the Carriere® System and the Sagittal First® approach: treat the AP dimension first with Carriere® Motion™ appliances (legacy Motion 3D™ plus newer Motion Pro® bite correctors), then finish with Carriere® SLX® 3D self-ligating brackets and Carriere® M-Series™ wires—or with Smilers® aligners. Dr. Luis Carrière is credited as inventor of Motion 3D and SLX 3D; HSO says it develops the Carriere portfolio with him. Searching only “Carriere” will find the hero appliances. It will miss Aria twins, Smilers, Ortho Technology SKUs, and the Henry Schein Dental cart.

The legal manufacturer of record on FDA establishment registration (status 2026) is Ortho Organizers, Inc., business trade name Henry Schein Orthodontics. That matches the history: Henry Schein bought Ortho Organizers (2008), ClassOne (2009), and Ortho Technology (2012), then unified those operations under the HSO name. Ortho Organizers and Ortho Technology still appear as catalog / account labels on the February 2026 ordering FAQs. They are not current house brands on the homepage.

What did change in 2026 is fulfillment, not the brand. HSO’s own FAQs and shop home say that as of February 11, 2026, U.S. direct ortho purchases—including Carriere, Smilers, Ortho Technology, and Ortho Organizers—process through a Henry Schein Dental account. The old “Shop HSO” storefront stopped taking orders that day; Ortho Technology’s site stayed up for convenience into April 2026 but billed through HSD after the cutoff. International orders are still routed separately on the HSO shop page.

Headquarters: the live Contact page lists 800 King George Blvd., Ste 100, Savannah, Georgia. The 2026 FDA listing for Ortho Organizers / HSO uses the same Savannah address. The About page still captions “HSO Headquarters in Carlsbad, CA,” and older directories keep 1822 Aston Avenue, Carlsbad. Treat Savannah as the current official HQ and Carlsbad as a historic / still-mentioned California site. Parent Henry Schein, Inc. remains in Melville, New York.

HSO’s About page attributes 1932 to Henry Schein (the parent), not to the ortho unit. Secondary directories give Ortho Organizers a 1975 or 1976 start. This draft does not invent a founding year for HSO itself.

Who it’s for

  • Specialists who already run Carriere Motion (3D or Pro) for Class II / III AP correction and need a current map of Motion Pro vs Motion 3D, plus SLX 3D finish
  • Offices that want a self-ligating line (SLX 3D) and a featured twin line (Aria / Aria Expressions) from the same vendor, then finish on M-Series wires or InfiNiTi Loops
  • Teams evaluating or already using Smilers / Smilers Expert (Nemotec-powered planning, Smilers Pro app) after HSO’s earlier Reveal aligner sunset
  • Practices that historically ordered Ortho Organizers or Ortho Technology SKUs and now need to buy those parts on a Henry Schein Dental account
  • Clinics adding adjuncts HSO is distributing in 2026: Froggymouth (atypical swallowing) and Eversmile smartIPR (U.S. exclusive)
  • Multi-country offices—Motion Pro Class III, Aria, and smartIPR are flagged as not available everywhere; use the local distributor, not the U.S. shop list

Notable products

Official names below match henryscheinortho.com listings as researched in August 2026. Treatment-time, ROI, and “data on file” claims are manufacturer positioning. Confirm slot, prescription, and country availability before you order. U.S. replenishment is through Henry Schein Dental, not the retired Shop HSO cart.

Carriere Motion bite correctors (Sagittal First)

  • Carriere® Motion Pro® Bite Corrector and Carriere® Motion Pro® Clear Bite Corrector — current-generation, side-specific Class II bite correctors; HSO calls Motion Pro “the new standard in bite correction.” Class III Motion Pro / Motion Pro Clear is not available in all countries
  • Carriere® Motion 3D™ Class II Appliance — legacy Motion family; still sold (silver, colored, multi, and clear Class II SKUs on the shop). Vendor copy: shorter treatment by up to 6 months*
  • Carriere® Motion 3D™ Class III Appliance — still listed for Class III without extractions, orthognathic surgery, or facemasks (vendor intent language)

Sagittal First copy on both Motion hubs: treat AP first, before brackets or aligners, aiming for a Class I platform in an average of 3–7 months*. Built-in stops are described as limiting molar over-rotation and crown distal tip.

Brackets

  • Carriere® SLX® 3D Self-Ligating Bracket System — passive-style slide (reciprocal open/lock), full mesial-distal slot coverage, Adhesive Guard Rail™ (AGR) for flash, compound-contour bases. Narrow/wide bodies by tooth. Carriere SLX 3D Self-Ligating Buccal Tubes are part of the same family; HSO highlights ceramic upper first-molar tubes
  • Aria® Low-Profile Brackets and Aria Expressions Champagne Gold — featured twin / conventional metal line (rhomboid pads, rounded mesial-distal entrance, pylon base). Expressions is the champagne-gold finish. Site note: Aria and Aria Expressions not available in all countries
  • Twin Bracket Systems — still a live shop category alongside SLX 3D. Historic Ortho Organizers / Ortho Technology twins may appear under HSD item numbers; do not assume every legacy name is still a homepage hero

Wires and space closure

  • Carriere® M-Series™ Wires — three-wire sequence plus M-Series Expansion Wires (copper Nitanium, wide/narrow forms) sold as the Sagittal First wire protocol. Includes a .015 Cu Nitanium listing on the wires page
  • Carriere® InfiNiTi Loops® Power Closer — preformed .010” NiTi four-loop closer for upper anterior space (vendor: up to 6 mm; compatible with SL and twin)

Aligners and digital

  • Smilers® and Smilers® Expert — HSO’s current clear-aligner offering (aligners plus treatment-planning software). Expert is positioned as Nemotec-powered virtual planning with optional CBCT root segmentation, trimline and material choices (including Zendura FLX tri-layer), and on-staff setup review. Smilers Pro is the chairside photo / AlgoSmile simulation app. Older Reveal materials were sunset in favor of Smilers; treat Reveal as discontinued, not current
  • Minimum Touch Orthodontics℠ — HSO workflow label (fewer / shorter visits, more virtual touchpoints) built around Sagittal First + SLX 3D / M-Series or Smilers—not a separate SKU

Adjuncts and 2026 distribution adds

  • Froggymouth® — passive myofunctional device for atypical swallowing (manufacturer: Biotech Dental Smilers; U.S. availability announced April 29, 2026). HSO pages spell both Froggymouth and “Froggy Mouth”
  • smartIPR™ — Eversmile / EverBrands oscillating, non-rotating IPR system (handpiece, 0.1–0.4 mm disks, gauge, block; IPRGlide sold separately). Exclusive U.S. distribution through HSO announced June 26, 2026; not available in all countries

The rest of the catalog

Shop categories still include buccal tubes, bands and assemblies, archwires beyond M-Series, elastomerics, instruments, bonding auxiliaries, intraoral and extraoral appliances, and general / patient supplies. That is the old Ortho Organizers + Ortho Technology breadth sitting under one HSD cart.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Carriere Motion (3D and Pro) is a real, widely recognized Class II / III AP toolset with a defined finish path (SLX 3D + M-Series or Smilers)—useful if you already teach Sagittal First
  • Two current bracket stories on the homepage: SLX 3D self-ligating and Aria twins, plus a still-live Twin Bracket Systems shop for mixed inventory
  • Same vendor covers bite correctors, brackets, a simplified wire sequence, a power closer, and an aligner/software stack—rare among “just brackets” houses
  • 2026 adds (Froggymouth, smartIPR) show HSO acting as a specialty distributor, not only a Carriere factory
  • U.S. ordering consolidation onto Henry Schein Dental can mean one cart, one invoice, and Thrive points on ortho SKUs if you already buy dental supplies there
  • Legal entity and FDA listing (Ortho Organizers, Inc.) are public; catalog numbers are supposed to map from old HSO / Ortho Technology part numbers into HSD items

Cons

  • Naming is a stack, not a rename: Henry Schein Orthodontics, Carriere, Smilers, Ortho Organizers, Ortho Technology, and Henry Schein Dental all appear on invoices and boxes. Staff need a cheat sheet
  • Carriere is not the company name. Offices that refile purchasing under “Carriere Motion” will miss Aria, Smilers, and the commodity catalog
  • Shop HSO is gone (Feb 11, 2026). If your team bookmarked the old cart or still emails Ortho Technology, you are on a transition FAQ, not the current checkout
  • HQ copy disagrees with itself (Savannah Contact / FDA vs. Carlsbad About caption). Use Contact + the FDA listing for address work
  • Motion, SLX, and M-Series time-savings, “3–7 months to Class I,” “finish in about 12 months,” and Motion ROI calculator numbers are vendor / data-on-file claims—not independent outcome guarantees
  • Availability footnotes matter: Motion Pro Class III, Aria, and smartIPR are not global. Smilers is a distributed aligner (Biotech Dental manufacturing in older legal copy), not a pure in-house Invisalign-class ecosystem
  • Mega-catalog leftover SKUs from Ortho Technology / Ortho Organizers will tempt a sloppy formulary; HSO has already said redundant SKUs may be phased out

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.
