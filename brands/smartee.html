Smartee Aligners

Clear aligner system and mandibular-repositioning orthodontic platform from Shanghai Smartee Denti-Technology Co., Ltd. (part of Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology Co., Ltd.), headquartered in Pudong, Shanghai, China, and founded in 2004.

HQ: Pudong, Shanghai, China · Founded 2004 · Researched 2026-10-27 · Official site

Overview

Smartee (Smartee Aligners, marketed domestically in China as 正雅) is a doctor-prescribed clear aligner manufacturer and digital orthodontics platform headquartered in Pudong, Shanghai, China (Zhangjiang Hi-Tech Park / China (Shanghai) Pilot Free Trade Zone). The company states that it was founded in 2004 in Shanghai’s Zhangjiang Pharma Valley.

The international website and doctor portal are operated by Shanghai Smartee Denti-Technology Co., Ltd. According to Smartee’s published corporate definitions and Chinese regulatory filings, Shanghai Smartee Denti-Technology Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary of its Jiaxing-based parent and manufacturing entity, Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology Co., Ltd. (also registered in Chinese as 浙江正雅齿科股份有限公司 / Zhejiang Smartee Denti-Technology Co., Ltd., in Nanhu District, Jiaxing, Zhejiang Province), alongside additional manufacturing and regional subsidiaries in Ziyang (Sichuan), Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan, Spain, Germany, France, and the United Kingdom. Smartee is privately held and founder-controlled; in October 2025, the Straumann Group acquired a single-digit minority equity stake as part of a strategic technology and manufacturing partnership under which Smartee also provides clear aligner manufacturing support for Straumann’s ClearCorrect brand in selected EMEA and APAC markets. Smartee is an independent company with no ownership link to Angelalign Technology Inc. (another Shanghai-based clear aligner manufacturer).

Smartee reports operating four research and development centers and four manufacturing bases—in Shanghai, Jiaxing (including a 100,000-square-meter automated facility launched in 2024), Ziyang (Sichuan), and Madrid, Spain (opened in 2024 for the European market). Its clinical positioning centers on combining sequential clear aligners with orthopedic mandibular repositioning mechanics (developed in partnership with a Chinese university hospital orthodontic research team) for skeletal and functional Class II, Class III, and asymmetry cases, alongside classic comprehensive aligners, day-and-night alternating aligners, and pediatric/adolescent early-intervention systems.

FDA status: FDA’s public establishment database shows two active foreign exporter registrations for the group:

  • Shanghai Smartee Denti-Technology Co., Ltd. (registration/FEI 3015342395, owner/operator 10060314, Shanghai) with 9 device listings, covering Custom-made Invisible Aligners (product code NXC, cleared under K203624 and K223454), Clear Retainer (YCL-A-R, YCL-C-R, Class I tooth positioner, code KMY), Dental Sheet (Class I orthodontic band material, code DYO), intraoral scanners (code NOF), and contract/OEM listings referencing third-party clearances (K210320 for ClearCorrect multi-layer aligners and ClearCorrect retainers/engager templates, plus K180346 and K230199 for Byte aligner systems).
  • Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology Co., Ltd. (registration/FEI 3015341209, Jiaxing) with 5 device listings, covering Custom-made Invisible Aligners (K203624 and K223454) and ClearCorrect system aligners, retainers, and engager templates.

In the FDA 510(k) database, the two clearances held directly by the group are K203624 (cleared June 16, 2022) and K223454 (cleared April 17, 2023), both for Custom-made Invisible Aligners (product code NXC, 21 CFR 872.5470) under the applicant name Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology Co., Ltd. Neither clearance appears if you search openFDA 510(k) records strictly by the applicant string “Smartee.” Confirm which specific Smartee product lines and accessories are covered under those 510(k) clearances before prescribing.

U.S. availability: Although Shanghai Smartee Denti-Technology and Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology maintain active FDA export registrations and designate a U.S. regulatory agent in Delaware (Regrek LLC), Smartee’s official corporate entity list (version March 30, 2025) and contact page list offices only in China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan, Spain, Germany, France, and the United Kingdom—with no U.S. subsidiary, U.S. office, or named U.S. distributor. Moreover, the October 2025 Straumann partnership announcement specifies EMEA and APAC manufacturing for ClearCorrect rather than U.S. Smartee distribution. U.S. clinicians should verify direct ordering availability, shipping logistics, and local clinical support with Smartee before planning cases.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and dentists outside or inside territories served by Smartee who want a doctor-prescribed clear aligner lab offering both routine alignment and orthopedic mandibular repositioning protocols
  • Clinicians interested in clear functional and orthopedic mechanics (integrated occlusal blocks, bite planes, splints, and palatal expanders/anchorage devices) for Class II retrusion, Class III concavity, or mandibular deviation
  • Practices treating children and adolescents in primary, mixed, and early permanent dentition that want combined myofunctional trainers, eruption guidance, and phased aligner packages (Smartee Kinder and Smartee Teen)
  • Offices looking for a dual-material day-and-night aligner option (Smartee α) that pairs a flexible daytime tray with a firmer nighttime tray
  • Clinicians who want a cloud case portal (Smartee Doctor Portal, smarteedoctor.com, and SmarteeCheck software) with 3D root/crown visualization and cephalometric tools

Notable products

  • Smartee GS (Mandibular Repositioning Aligners): a two-phase orthopedic and orthodontic clear aligner system designed around facial-profile classification (facial convexity, facial concavity, and mandibular deviation). Phase 1 combines mandibular repositioning with tooth movement; Phase 2 levels the Curve of Spee, coordinates arches, and settles occlusion. Core GS appliances include:
    • S8 Mandibular Repositioning Device for Mandibular Advancement: S8-SGTB (sagittal-guidance twin-block anatomical occlusal blocks integrated into the aligners for Class II cases with deep overbite, deep overjet, and deep Curve of Spee) and S8-SGHB (for Class II cases with deep overjet, shallow overbite, and shallow Curve of Spee)
    • S9 Mandibular Repositioning Device with Bite Plane: integrated anterior bite plane to open vertical space in deep overbite cases, stabilize mandibular position, intrude lower anterior teeth, and level the Curve of Spee
    • S10 Mandibular Repositioning Device with Occlusal Splint: posterior anatomical occlusal splint locking the upper and lower occlusal relationship for mandibular deviation and Class III / facial concavity cases
    • S17 Active Mandibular Positioning Retainer: active retention appliance designed to maintain mandibular position after repositioning and reduce relapse risk
  • Smartee GE (Classic Invisible Dental Aligners): Smartee’s main comprehensive aligner line for mild to complex crowding, spacing, crossbite, open bite, protrusion, extraction, and deep bite cases, integrating the S1–S7 and S15 mechanics modules
  • Smartee α (Alpha Day & Night Aligners): alternating dual-sheet aligner system pairing a flexible Day Aligner for comfortable wear and initial force expression with a stiffer, tear-resistant Night Aligner for rotational, angulation, and root-torque control
  • Smartee Kinder (Aligners for Kids): early-intervention system for primary, early mixed, and middle mixed dentition (featuring Disney-licensed themes in supported markets), combining clear aligners with the K1–K5 pediatric module: K1 child-specific optimized attachments, K3 tongue protrusion / tongue crib features, K4 silicone myofunctional trainer (Joy-Joy), and K5 myofunctional trainers (Smiley-Smiley and Bitie-Bitie)
  • Smartee Teen (Aligners for Teenagers): adolescent 5-year full-cycle management line combining K-series interceptive tools, S8/S9/S10 mandibular repositioning, and S11/S15/S16 maxillary protraction and palatal anchorage mechanics
  • S1–S20 Orthodontic Treatment Solutions: modular biomechanical protocols and auxiliaries across the Smartee portfolio, including S1 Classic Attachments System (bite pads, window, ovoid, rectangular, patented intrusion and precision-control attachments, plus acid-etching templates), S2 Invisible Lip Bumper, S3 Intelligent Traction, S4 Intelligent Power Arm & TAD (mini-screw) extraction-space closure, S5 Intelligent Rotation Center, S6 Classic Tooth Extraction Protocol, S7 / S7 Plus Segmented Aligner for Molar Distalization (two-part segmented aligner to preserve anterior anchorage), S11 Palatal Device (Clear Arch Expander, Clear Nance, and Clear TPA), S12 Hybrid Therapy, S15 Integrated Traction Devices (Smartee Hook and Smartee Button rated up to 3,000 g tractive force), S16 Invisible Forward Traction Device (paired with a facemask and S11), S18 Clear Appliances for Perioperative Orthognathic Treatment, S19 Clear Appliance for Obstructive Sleep Apnea Syndrome (OSAS), and S20 Early Correction of Facial Retrognathism
  • Smartee Platinum & Dental Thermoplastic Sheets: proprietary multi-layer composite thermoplastic sheets (building on earlier Crystal PETG and Diamond TPU sheet lines) featuring a textured inner layer designed for diffuse light reflection, tear resistance, and sustained stress retention, plus Clear Retainers (YCL-A-R, YCL-C-R)
  • Smartee Digital ecosystem: Smartee Doctor Portal (smarteedoctor.com), SmarteeCheck 3D treatment-planning software, Smartee Doctor App and Smartee Assistant App, iFast (one-click 5-minute smile simulation), Root Visualization (AI-assisted 3D crown, root, and bone modeling), CEPH Measurement (SNA, SNB, ANB, FMA, and bone-age diagrams), and Outcome Pre-refinement Evaluator

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Differentiated biomechanical catalog: Goes well beyond standard single-layer trays with integrated twin-block/bite-plane/splint mandibular repositioning (S8/S9/S10), segmented molar-distalization trays (S7 Plus), clear palatal expanders/Nance/TPA (S11), and pre-crafted traction hooks/buttons (S15)
  • Large-scale automated manufacturing: Four production bases across China and Spain—including a 100,000 m² modular automated facility in Jiaxing—validated by Straumann’s 2025 decision to partner with Smartee for EMEA/APAC ClearCorrect production
  • Active FDA registration and 510(k) clearances: Both Shanghai Smartee Denti-Technology (FEI 3015342395) and parent Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology (FEI 3015341209) hold active FDA registrations, with two 510(k) clearances (K203624 and K223454) on file for custom-made invisible aligners
  • Broad age and case coverage: Dedicated lines for primary/mixed dentition (Kinder + K1–K5 trainers), adolescents (Teen), dual-sheet day/night wear (Smartee α), adult comprehensive cases (GE), and skeletal/functional discrepancies (GS)

Cons

  • No confirmed U.S. commercial footprint: Despite FDA export listings, Smartee lists corporate entities and offices only in Asia and Europe; there is no published U.S. office, U.S. clinical support team, or named U.S. distributor on its official site
  • Split corporate naming in regulatory databases: FDA 510(k) clearances K203624 and K223454 are filed under the parent name Zhejiang Yinchili Medical Technology Co., Ltd. rather than “Smartee,” and it is not publicly itemized in openFDA which S1–S20 or GS/Kinder specialty appliances fall under those sequential-aligner clearances in the U.S.
  • No public doctor fee schedule: Per-case lab pricing, refinement policies, and shipping timelines are gated behind registered doctor accounts
  • Marketing claims require clinical scrutiny: Website statements describing Smartee GS as “the world’s only clear aligner solution that effectively addresses severe malocclusions” or claiming non-extraction/non-surgical skeletal correction and “space-level precision” are manufacturer promotional claims; skeletal remodeling in adults and complex jaw repositioning depend heavily on case selection, patient compliance, and independent clinical evidence

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

In markets where Smartee operates directly (such as China, Europe via its Madrid facility and European subsidiaries, Japan, Singapore, and the Middle East), clinicians register on the Smartee Doctor Portal (smarteedoctor.com) or Smartee Doctor App to upload intraoral scans, photographs, panoramic radiographs, and lateral cephalograms. For Smartee GS mandibular-repositioning cases, the workflow includes classifying the facial profile, taking a construction wax bite in the repositioned mandibular posture, scanning the arches with the bite registration, and reviewing the two-phase 3D setup in SmarteeCheck (with optional CBCT root visualization and cephalometric tracing) before manufacturing. If your practice is in the United States, treat Smartee primarily as a global benchmark or OEM manufacturer to be aware of—and if you wish to order directly, contact the company first to confirm U.S. account eligibility, which specific appliances are cleared for U.S. import under K203624/K223454, turnaround times from overseas facilities, and local support terms.
