Ormco

Long-standing orthodontic manufacturer known for Damon brackets, Spark aligners, and a broad wire and appliance lineup for specialty practices.

HQ: Brea, California, USA · Founded 1960 · Researched 2026-08-23 · Official site

Overview

Ormco is one of the best-known names in specialty orthodontics. The company sits under Envista and sells brackets, wires, auxiliaries, and clear aligners aimed at orthodontists rather than general dentistry alone. Practices usually encounter Ormco through the Damon self-ligating platform or through Spark aligners.

Public materials emphasize clinical education, case libraries, and a dense catalog of metal and ceramic appliances. Like most large manufacturers, product availability and regional packaging can differ by country, so offices should confirm SKUs with their rep.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic specialists evaluating self-ligating bracket systems
  • Offices comparing clear aligner options alongside fixed appliances
  • Multi-doctor groups that want a single vendor for brackets, wires, and auxiliaries
  • Practices already invested in Damon education and inventory

Notable products

  • Damon — self-ligating bracket family with metal and ceramic options
  • Spark — clear aligner system marketed to orthodontists
  • Titanium and stainless wires — archwires and auxiliaries across common prescriptions
  • Hermetic / other appliance lines — additional bracket and instrument offerings vary by market

Exact SKU names change over time; check Ormco’s current catalog before ordering.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Deep specialty catalog with long clinical familiarity in many markets
  • Strong education ecosystem around Damon and Spark
  • Aligners and fixed appliances under one corporate umbrella

Cons

  • Large-vendor pricing and contracts can feel less flexible for small startups
  • Switching off Damon mid-panel can be inventory- and training-heavy
  • Aligners compete in a crowded field; fit depends on your case mix, not brand loyalty
