Invisalign (Align Technology)

Market-leading clear aligner platform from Align Technology, paired with iTero scanning and a large doctor network.

HQ: Tempe, Arizona, USA · Founded 1997 · Researched 2026-08-23 · Official site

Overview

Invisalign is Align Technology’s clear aligner brand and the most widely recognized name in orthodontic aligners. Align also sells the iTero intraoral scanner ecosystem, which many offices use to submit cases digitally. The platform includes consumer branding, doctor portals, and tiered product lines that change over time.

This page covers the brand/product platform for practices, not patient marketing claims. Case selection, compliance, and finishing still sit with the treating doctor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and qualified dentists offering clear aligner therapy
  • Offices already standardized on iTero or evaluating scanner + aligner bundles
  • Practices that want a high-recognition consumer brand for case acceptance conversations
  • Multi-location groups needing consistent digital workflows

Notable products

  • Invisalign clear aligners — multiple clinical packages/tiers (names evolve; check Align’s doctor materials)
  • iTero intraoral scanners — digital impression and visualization workflow tightly linked to Align submissions
  • Doctor education and software tools — ClinCheck-style planning and related portals (confirm current product names with Align)

Always verify eligibility, territory rules, and current SKU naming with Align directly.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Highest consumer recognition in clear aligners in many markets
  • Mature digital submission and scanner ecosystem
  • Large peer community and continuing education volume

Cons

  • Pricing and lab fees can be higher than challenger aligner brands
  • Brand strength does not replace case selection or finishing skill
  • Contract terms, volume commitments, and scanner financing need careful review

Warm and direct: Invisalign is a toolset, not a promise. Ortho Index does not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.
