AcceleDent (OrthoAccel Technologies)

Legacy vibration-based orthodontic accessory brand (AcceleDent, AcceleDent Aura, AcceleDent Optima) from OrthoAccel Technologies of Texas. The company is reported closed, its brand site is a parked domain, and the AcceleDent trademark is now recorded to Dentsply LLC.

HQ: Historically Bellaire (Houston area), Texas, USA (company reported closed) · Researched 2026-11-03 · Official site

Get a quote Claim this brand

Overview

AcceleDent was a line of at-home vibration devices worn during orthodontic treatment, made by OrthoAccel Technologies, Inc. In a 2019 press release the company described itself as privately owned, based in Houston, Texas, with an office in Essen, Germany. openFDA lists its address as Bellaire, Texas (earlier, Houston). The company marketed its “SoftPulse Technology” and claimed the device could accelerate tooth movement and reduce discomfort; those are the company’s claims, not established findings (see Pros / cons).

Ownership and status: We could not find a current official AcceleDent or OrthoAccel website. acceledent.com now serves only a parked-domain redirect page. The Canadian trademark record for ACCELEDENT shows the mark assigned from OrthoAccel Technologies, Inc. to OrthoAccel Holdings, LLC (Ossining, New York) in April 2020, and then to Dentsply LLC (York, Pennsylvania) effective June 1, 2021. That date matches Dentsply Sirona’s acquisition of Propel Orthodontics’ assets, but Dentsply Sirona’s Propel press release does not mention AcceleDent, and we found no Dentsply Sirona announcement about it. We could not confirm whether a bankruptcy or asset sale took place, whether Dentsply sells or supports AcceleDent devices today, or whether replacement parts and support remain available. Third-party company databases list OrthoAccel as closed; we treat AcceleDent as a discontinued legacy brand.

FDA status (openFDA): OrthoAccel holds four 510(k) clearances under product code OYH (orthodontic vibratory accessory; Class II):

  • K110661 (2011): AcceleDent
  • K130643 (2013) and K153048 (2016): AcceleDent Aura
  • K170093 (2017): AcceleDent Optima

The K170093 summary states that AcceleDent Optima “is intended for use during orthodontic treatment. It is used in conjunction with brackets and wires or aligners and helps facilitate minor anterior tooth movement.”

Who it’s for

Historically, orthodontic offices that dispensed a patient-use vibration accessory alongside braces or aligners. Today the page is mainly a reference for clinicians and staff who encounter patients with existing AcceleDent devices or who are researching the history of vibration accessories.

Notable products

  • AcceleDent (original): first-generation device, cleared in 2011 (K110661).
  • AcceleDent Aura: second generation, a mouthpiece plus activator used for a short daily session (K130643, K153048).
  • AcceleDent Optima: third generation, with changes to design, materials and software versus Aura, cleared in 2017 (K170093) for use with brackets and wires or aligners.

We could not confirm that any of these models are still sold.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • FDA 510(k) clearances for three device generations are on public record, with published summaries
  • Simple at-home routine with no extra in-office procedure
  • The trademark record suggests the brand passed to a large dental company (Dentsply LLC) rather than being abandoned

Cons

  • The company said AcceleDent could accelerate tooth movement “by as much as 50 percent.” A 2015 Cochrane review of non-surgical ways to speed up tooth movement, which covered vibration, rated the available evidence very low quality and said it was not possible to determine whether these interventions have a positive effect. Treat speed claims as unproven.
  • The maker is reported closed and the brand website is parked; current availability, warranty and support are unconfirmed
  • No public information on parts, replacement mouthpieces or service for existing devices
  • Pricing isn’t published
  • Patients have to use it daily

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

AcceleDent was an add-on to fixed-appliance or aligner treatment, not a treatment system on its own. Because the brand appears to be discontinued, practices should not plan around new supply. If you have patients with existing devices, confirm with Dentsply Sirona whether any support or IFU documentation is still available, and weigh the company’s historical claims against independent evidence.
