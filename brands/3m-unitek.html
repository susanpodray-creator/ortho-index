Solventum Orthodontic Solutions

Former 3M Unitek / 3M Oral Care fixed-appliance and bonding catalog—Clarity ceramic brackets, Victory Series and SmartClip metal lines, APC precoat, plus Clarity Aligners—now marketed by Solventum after the 2024 3M Health Care spin.

HQ: Eagan, Minnesota, USA · Researched 2026-08-25 · Official site

Overview

The catalog most offices still call 3M Unitek is no longer marketed under that house name. On April 1, 2024, 3M completed the spin-off of its health care business as Solventum Corporation (NYSE: SOLV). Official Solventum pages now sell the historic Oral Care orthodontic line—brackets, APC precoat, Transbond adhesives, buccal tubes and bands, and Clarity digital / aligner tools—under Solventum Oral Care. Packaging and on-box branding are mid-transition: many SKUs still carry a 3M™ prefix on the website while copy states they are moving to Solventum™ names. Solventum’s own rebrand FAQ says catalog numbers and trademarked product names stay the same; the company name and packaging change. That work is scheduled through March 31, 2028, so mixed 3M and Solventum boxes on the same shelf are expected.

Unitek is a historic line, not the current corporate brand. It still appears as a trademark filter on Solventum’s brackets and adhesives pages (for example, 3M™ Unitek™ Miniature Twin Metal Brackets, Unitek Multi-Cure glass ionomer band cement). The Miniature Twin listing itself says it is transitioning to Solventum™ Miniature Twin Metal Brackets, dropping Unitek from that SKU title. Do not treat “Solventum Unitek” as an official rename—Solventum has not published that as a house brand.

Clarity™ is the name Solventum leads with. Rebrand materials give Clarity a special designation and its own look, “complementary to and endorsed by Solventum.” The Clarity family now spans ceramic brackets, digital bonding, a cloud portal, aligners, retainers, and attachment products. Metal workhorses remain Victory Series™ and SmartClip™. APC™ (II, PLUS, Flash-Free) is the adhesive-precoat system sold on compatible brackets.

Corporate headquarters is listed by Solventum Investor Relations and the Contact page at 1750 Yankee Doodle Road, Eagan, Minnesota. Solventum’s orthodontic hub claims more than 75 years of appliance innovation (consistent with the old Unitek specialty line inside 3M), but the company does not publish a single founding year on these pages, so this draft does not invent one. 3M’s April 1, 2024 release is the firm date for the current owner.

Who it’s for

  • Specialists who already stock Clarity ceramic, Victory Series, or SmartClip and need a current name / ordering map after the 3M → Solventum spin
  • Offices that want APC-precoated metal or ceramic brackets (APC II, APC PLUS, or APC Flash-Free) plus Transbond primers and adhesives from the same vendor
  • Practices comparing ceramic twin (Clarity Advanced) and ceramic self-ligating (Clarity Ultra) against metal twin and clip/door self-ligating options in one catalog
  • Teams using or evaluating Clarity Digital Bonding (full-arch tray placement with APC Flash-Free) and Clarity Portal for treatment design
  • Offices that may add Clarity Aligners (Flex / Force), Precision Grip Attachments, and Clarity Retainers without leaving the same digital workflow
  • Multi-country practices ordering through Shop Ortho or authorized distributors during the mixed-packaging years

Notable products

Official names below match Solventum listings as researched in August 2026. Many pages still title SKUs with a 3M™ prefix and a one-line “transitioning to Solventum™ …” note. Confirm slot, prescription, hook pattern, and precoat before you order.

Ceramic / aesthetic brackets

  • Clarity™ Advanced Ceramic Brackets — low-profile translucent ceramic; Solventum markets a Stress Concentrator base for predictable debond; available uncoated or APC II / APC PLUS / APC Flash-Free; packaging copy says it is moving to the new company brand
  • Clarity™ Ultra Self-Ligating Brackets — translucent ceramic self-ligating; same Stress Concentrator language; APC Flash-Free kits are heavily listed

Metal brackets (current official families)

  • Victory Series™ Brackets — conventional metal twins; site copy: transitioning to Solventum™ Victory Series™ Metal Brackets; identification marks and deep tie-wing undercuts called out
  • Victory Series™ Low Profile Brackets — lower-profile metal twin; transitioning to Solventum™ Victory Series™ Low Profile Metal Brackets
  • Victory Series™ Active Self-Ligating Brackets — metal active SL with a full-slot door; transitioning to Solventum™ Victory Series™ Active Self-Ligating Metal Brackets; Roth* and APC II options on current cards
  • SmartClip™ SL3 Self-Ligating Brackets — metal self-ligating with an integral nickel-titanium clip; transitioning to Solventum™ SmartClip™ SL3 APC™ Metal Brackets; offered uncoated and with APC II / PLUS / Flash-Free
  • Mini Uni-Twin™ Brackets — transitioning to Solventum™ Mini Uni-Twin Metal Brackets
  • Unitek™ Miniature Twin Metal Brackets — smaller twin; transitioning to Solventum™ Miniature Twin Metal Brackets (Unitek dropped from the destination name)

Bracket filters on the official page also list prescriptions including MBT™, Roth*, High Torque, ZeroBase™, Andrews*, Sondhi, Hilgers*, Bench, Samson*, Alexander*, Ricketts*, Level Anchorage™, Dischinger*, and Standard Edgewise. Availability is SKU-specific.

APC precoat and chairside bonding

  • APC™ Flash-Free — resin-soaked nonwoven mat on the bracket base; marketed as eliminating flash cleanup; featured with Clarity Digital Bonding
  • APC™ II and APC™ PLUS — older precoat generations still on current kits (PLUS is the color-change variant in catalog materials)
  • Transbond™ family — Plus Self Etching Primer, XT Light Cure Adhesive Primer, Plus Color Change Adhesive, Supreme LV, Plus Light Cure Band Adhesive, and related kits
  • Sondhi™ Rapid-Set indirect bonding adhesive
  • Unitek™ Multi-Cure Glass Ionomer Band Cement — still listed under the Unitek trademark
  • Clarity™ Attachment Material — syringe attachment composite positioned for any clear aligner system

Digital, aligners, ordering

  • Clarity™ Digital Bonding — digitally designed full-arch trays; official compatible brackets include Clarity Advanced, Victory Series Low Profile, and SmartClip SL3 with APC Flash-Free
  • Clarity™ Portal — cloud treatment-design platform (clarity.solventum.com) for aligners, retainers, Precision Grip, and digital bonding
  • Clarity™ Aligners — Flex (0.625 mm) and Force (0.75 mm) multi-layer films; Clarity™ Precision Grip Attachments (3D-printed, pre-loaded, Clarity Aligners only); Clarity™ Retainers
  • Shop Ortho — Solventum’s practice ordering portal (shoportho.solventum.com)
  • Buccal tubes, bands, auxiliaries, and instruments sit in adjacent Solventum Oral Care categories

Vendor time-savings and bond-failure percentages on Digital Bonding and APC pages are manufacturer positioning. Read them against IFUs and your own failure log.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Full fixed-appliance stack still in one place: ceramic (Clarity), metal twin (Victory Series, Miniature Twin, Mini Uni-Twin), and two self-ligating mechanics (SmartClip clip and Victory active door)
  • APC precoat (especially Flash-Free) plus Transbond primers is a real workflow differentiator if your team already runs precoated inventory
  • Clarity is an actual brand system now—not only a ceramic bracket—covering portal, digital bonding, aligners, attachments, and retainers
  • Catalog numbers are supposed to stay stable through the rebrand, which helps purchasing during mixed 3M / Solventum packaging
  • Deep prescription list on the brackets hub; Shop Ortho plus distributor channels for replenishment
  • Clarity Digital Bonding names compatible metal and ceramic APC Flash-Free brackets, so hybrid aligner/fixed offices can stay inside one digital vendor

Cons

  • Naming is messy in 2026: boxes, invoices, and web titles still say 3M™, Unitek™, and Solventum™ depending on SKU and warehouse vintage. Staff will need a cheat sheet, not a new “Solventum Unitek” brand story
  • Unitek as a house name is fading (Miniature Twin destination name drops it). Searching only “3M Unitek” will miss current Solventum pages
  • Mega-vendor complexity—hundreds of slot/prescription/precoat combinations—punishes a loose formulary
  • Aligners exist (Clarity Flex/Force) but this is not a single-purpose aligner company; compare that workflow on its own merits against dedicated aligner brands
  • Efficiency, comfort, stain, and “lower bond failure” claims are vendor copy (some citing older surveys or internal data). They are not outcome guarantees
  • Rebrand runs into 2028; expect leftover 3M IFUs, SDS headers, and training decks beside new green Solventum art

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.
