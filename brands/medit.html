Medit

Intraoral and lab 3D scanners (i900 series, iE, i700 series, i600, T-Series), Medit Link cloud platform, and orthodontic/CAD software apps (Medit Orthodontic Suite, Medit Design, Medit Model Builder, Medit Splints, Medit ClinicCAD, Medit AuraVue) from Medit Corp., headquartered in Seoul, South Korea, and founded in 2000.

HQ: Seoul, South Korea · Founded 2000 · Researched 2026-10-28 · Official site

Overview

Medit is a South Korean 3D dental scanning and digital dentistry software company. The legal entity is Medit Corp. (Korean business registration 114-81-97458), headquartered at 8, Yangpyeong-ro 25-gil (9F, 10F, 13F, 14F, 16F), Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul, 07207, Republic of Korea. According to Medit’s company history and anniversary releases, the company was founded in 2000 in Seoul as an industrial 3D metrology scanner manufacturer for quality inspection and reverse engineering, entered digital dentistry in 2013 with its first blue-light dental tabletop scanner (Identica Blue, followed by Identica Hybrid in 2015 and the T-Series), and launched its first intraoral scanner, the Medit i500, in 2018.

Ownership and corporate history: Medit is privately held and majority-owned by the North Asia private equity firm MBK Partners (through its MBK Partners V fund). In late December 2022, MBK Partners signed an agreement to acquire a 99.5% stake in Medit Corp. from previous majority owner Unison Capital Korea (which had acquired a controlling stake in 2019) in a transaction valued at approximately KRW 2.4–2.45 trillion ($1.9–2.0 billion). MBK Partners records the investment date as March 2023; under the transaction structure reported in the Korean financial press, the company’s founder and related shareholders reinvested a portion of their proceeds to retain an approximate 30% minority stake alongside MBK Partners’ 70% majority stake.

In May 2026, Medit Corp. announced that its U.S. subsidiary, Medit USA, Inc., had acquired Progressive Orthodontics (an orthodontic continuing-education and software provider headquartered in Aliso Viejo, California) and established Medit’s Global Orthodontic Business Division, headquartered at Medit USA’s office in Newport Beach, California. That division oversees the Medit Orthodontic Suite (MOS) treatment-planning platform, Medit Aligners ordering workflow, and SmileStream bracket/coaching integration.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic and general dental practices that want an open-format intraoral scanner (native STL, PLY, and OBJ export alongside .meditMesh) that connects to third-party clear aligner portals, custom bracket labs, and orthodontic planning software on both Windows and macOS
  • High-traffic orthodontic clinics or multi-chair bays evaluating ultra-lightweight wired wands (i900, i900 classic, and iE at 165 g including tip) or wireless chair-to-chair scanning (i900 Mobility with iPad-native Medit Link Express over Wi-Fi 6E, or i700 wireless with multi-hub Switch & Scan)
  • In-house orthodontic labs and 3D-printing practices that rely on Medit Model Builder (ABO, plate, and plateless base creation), Medit Design (3D superimposition, deviation colormaps, and mesh editing), and Medit Splints (occlusal splints, retainers, and anterior bite planes)
  • Clinicians in supported regions (including the U.S. and Europe) looking for chairside orthodontic simulation, cephalometric overlay, aligner staging, or direct aligner/bracket ordering inside Medit Orthodontic Suite
  • Dental laboratories and orthodontic appliance labs seeking high-speed tabletop model and impression scanners (Medit T-Series: T710, T510, T310)

Notable products

Specifications and pricing structures below reflect Medit’s product pages and Medit Help Center documentation checked on October 28, 2026. Confirm regional availability, hardware bundle contents, and current app subscription tiers before purchasing.

Intraoral scanners (i900 series, iE, i700 series, i600)

  • Medit i900: flagship wired touch-interface intraoral scanner featuring a 3rd-generation 10-bit optical engine, 12–30 mm scan depth, 165 g weight (including medium tip), 223.4 × 36.7 × 35.3 mm unibody dimensions, a 360° Touch Band (double-tap to start/stop scanning from any grip angle) plus a Touch Pad and menu button, single-cable USB 3.1 Gen 1 Power Delivery connection, UV-C LED internal disinfection, haptic vibration feedback, a 90-day calibration cycle, and Realtime Intelligence (real-time scan and prep verification) included at no extra cost. Supports three reusable PPSU tip sizes—Large (18.0 × 15.0 mm FOV), Medium (14.0 × 11.5 mm FOV), and Small (10.0 × 8.0 mm FOV; sold separately)—autoclavable up to 150 cycles.
  • Medit i900 classic: shares the i900’s 165 g unibody housing, 10-bit 3rd-generation optical engine, 12–30 mm scan depth, 90-day calibration period, Large/Medium/Small PPSU tips (up to 18 × 15 mm FOV), UV-C LED disinfection, and included Realtime Intelligence, but replaces the touch band and touchpad with a traditional tactile Scan Button interface.
  • Medit i900 Mobility (i900M): wireless intraoral scanner (319 g including medium tip and battery; 290.2 × 42.0 × 42.0 mm) built on the 3rd-generation 10-bit optical engine (12–30 mm scan depth, 90-day calibration, Large/Medium/Small tips compatible with i900/i900 classic) with physical Scan and Control buttons and built-in Wi-Fi 6E connectivity. Connects either directly over a Wi-Fi router to an iPad running Medit Link Express (recommended: iPad Pro M4) or to a laptop running Medit Link, or via an included wireless hub. Smart battery provides up to 1 hour of continuous scanning (30+ cases per charge) with thermal and overcharge monitoring.
  • Medit iE (“Start with Medit, Evolve with Medit”): entry-level wired scanner introduced in October 2026 sharing the 165 g unibody form factor, 10-bit 3rd-generation optical engine, 12–30 mm scan depth, 18 × 15 mm Large and 14 × 11.5 mm Medium tips (autoclavable up to 150 cycles), single-button control, UV-C LED disinfection, and 90-day calibration period. Positioned as a lower-cost “Scan & Send” entry unit: unlike the i900 series and i700/i600, Medit iE does not include free access to the Essential App Bundle or Medit Model Builder (standard app pricing applies unless another qualifying Medit scanner is registered to the same clinic group), and Realtime Intelligence is a paid add-on rather than bundled.
  • Medit i700: wired scanner (245 g including tip; 248.0 × 44.0 × 47.4 mm) with up to 70 FPS scanning, 8-bit color depth, 12–23 mm scan depth, 14-day calibration period, 180° reversible PSU tips (Standard 15.0 × 13.0 mm and Small 11.2 × 9.7 mm, autoclavable up to 150 cycles), UV-C LED disinfection, remote control button, and single-cable USB-C Power Delivery connectivity.
  • Medit i700 wireless: wireless version of the i700 (328 g including tip and battery; 313.0 × 44.0 × 47.4 mm) using 60 GHz wireless transmission to a USB-connected hub, with Switch & Scan multi-hub chair switching. Ships with three batteries (up to 1 hour continuous scanning / 8 hours standby / ~30 cases per battery) plus a corded extension-cable battery for uninterrupted backup use.
  • Medit i600: wired 35 FPS scanner sharing the i700’s 245 g weight, 12–23 mm scan depth, 14-day calibration cycle, 15.0 × 13.0 mm reversible tips, and stated full-arch accuracy (10.9 μm ± 0.98), with one-button operation and USB-C plug-and-scan connectivity (omits UV-C LED disinfection and remote control button by default; selected features available via optional Feature Upgrade).
  • Medit i500: Medit’s first-generation intraoral scanner (launched 2018; 280 g, 8-bit, 12–21 mm scan depth, USB 3.0 hub connection, tips autoclavable up to 50 cycles). Still supported in Medit Link and covered for eligible app bundles, though no longer eligible for warranty extensions.

Tabletop lab scanners (Medit T-Series)

  • Medit T710, T510, and T310 (succeeding earlier T500, T300, T200, and Identica Blue/Hybrid/Light models): blue-light desktop lab scanners for dental and orthodontic labs. The flagship T710 uses four 5.0 MP cameras, automatic model elevation (eliminating manual jig stacking), an 8-second full-arch scan time, < 4 μm accuracy (ISO 12836), KAS and AM articulator jigs (supporting KaVo, Artex, SAM, MARK330, and BIOART A7+), and open STL export.

Cloud platform and scanning software

  • Medit Link (medit.com/medit-link): desktop (Windows and macOS) and web workflow platform connecting clinics and dental labs, with case management, Medit Scan for Clinics / Medit Scan for Labs acquisition software, patient communication views, lab order tracking, and open export (STL, PLY, OBJ, and .meditMesh). Includes 2 GB of free cloud storage, with an optional 10 TB Medit Cloud Storage Plan at $9.99/month.
  • Medit Link Express: standalone iPad-native scanning and consultation app for Medit i900 Mobility (and supported on Mac for the i900 series), featuring an Express Scan mode for immediate chairside consultation without prior patient registration and automatic cloud sync back to desktop Medit Link.

Orthodontic and clinical software apps (Medit Apps)

As of 2026, Medit divides its software ecosystem into the Essential App Bundle and Individually Paid Apps:

  • Medit Orthodontic Suite (MOS) (meditortho.com; individually paid app, available in the U.S., EU, UK, Switzerland, and South Africa): launched in February 2026 as the official successor to Medit Ortho Simulation (which was discontinued in the U.S. and EU on May 4, 2026) and anchored by Medit’s May 2026 acquisition of Progressive Orthodontics (K241153). MOS provides a 9-step chairside workflow—Data Editing (with automatic bracket/attachment removal), Occlusal Table alignment, Tooth Identification, optional AI-assisted Ceph Tracing (13 landmarks; requires NVIDIA CUDA on Windows or Apple Silicon on macOS) and Ceph Overlay, Arch Form Selection, Bite Classification, Simulations Preview (generating 3 to 9 treatment scenarios with Bolton ratio and traffic-light difficulty scoring), and Output Overview with layer-by-layer staging, IPR, and attachment controls. Clinicians choose between:
    1. In-House Production workflow: export staged aligner files for in-office 3D printing—priced at $199.99/month or $1.50/arch one-time export for registered Medit scanner owners (including iE, i900 series, i700 series, i600, i500, and T-Series), or $499.99/month / $5.00/arch standard price.
    2. Direct Manufacturing Orders & SmileStream integration: order fabricated Medit Aligners at $18/unit (or $20/unit including the in-app Design Service; Medit’s privacy policy names K Line Europe GmbH in Germany as the contracted aligner fabrication processor), or send bracket/wire/torque prescriptions directly to SmileStream for fixed-appliance orders and clinical coaching.
  • Medit Model Builder (individually paid app; free for i900 series, i700 series, i600, i500, and T-Series owners; $99.99/month or $2.50 single arch / $5.00 dual arch per export for standard/iE users): creates 3D-printable orthodontic and prosthodontic models from scan data with ABO, Plate, and Plateless bases, hollow or infill interiors, drain holes, articulators (plus optional $19.99/month xSNAP 3D articulator module), dies, and embossed/engraved case labels.
  • Essential App Bundle (free for i900 series, i700 series, i600, i500, and T-Series owners; $99.99/month for non-Medit scanner users and Medit iE-only accounts):
    • Medit Design (formerly Medit Compare): 3D mesh alignment, multi-timepoint orthodontic superimposition, deviation colormaps (Avg/RMS), distance/area/angle/section measurements, boolean operations, offset, sculpting, smoothing, and surface reversal across .STL, .PLY, .OBJ, and .meditMesh files.
    • Medit Splints: automated or guided design of printable Michigan, flat plane, and anterior bite splints, retainers, and deprogrammers with numerical millimeter controls for bite opening, inner offset, undercut blockout, and labeling.
    • Medit Smile Design: 2D consultation mockups on patient smile photos, including customizable tooth shapes/shades and metal and ceramic braces templates.
    • Medit Occlusion Analyzer: automated occlusal interference colormaps, section views, bite realignment, and mandibular movement replay.
    • Medit Checkpoint: visual consultation notes from ortho scan views exported as patient PDFs or QR codes.
    • Medit Crown Fit, Medit Margin Lines, and Medit DCM Converter (converts 3Shape .dcm files to Medit format).
    • Medit Ortho Simulation: remains in the bundle only outside regions where it was retired (discontinued in the U.S. and EU on May 4, 2026, and in the UK, Switzerland, and South Africa on November 19, 2026).
  • Medit ClinicCAD (individually paid app; $29.99/month or $3/tooth preferred price for all Medit scanner owners including iE; $59.99/month or $5/tooth standard): chairside restorative CAD software (replacing Medit Temporaries) for crowns, bridges, veneers, inlays/onlays, cervical inlays, and eggshell temporaries, with direct SprintRay RayWare Cloud printing integration.
  • Medit AuraVue (U.S. only; $99.99/month Basic for up to 50 X-rays/month or $249.99/month Pro unlimited; free for existing Overjet subscribers): multimodal web consultation app displaying Medit 3D intraoral scans side-by-side with Overjet FDA-cleared radiographic AI annotations on 2D X-rays.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Native Windows, macOS, and iPad support: Medit Link and its core apps run natively on both Windows (Intel/NVIDIA) and Apple Silicon macOS (M1/M2 or higher), and the i900 Mobility adds iPad-native wireless scanning via Medit Link Express
  • Ultra-lightweight current hardware and open file exports: The i900, i900 classic, and iE weigh 165 g (including tip) with a single USB-C Power Delivery cable, 90-day calibration intervals, and 150-cycle autoclavable tips, while Medit Link exports open STL, PLY, and OBJ files without per-scan click fees
  • Strong orthodontic utility in core apps: Medit Model Builder (with ABO base presets), Medit Design (timepoint superimposition and deviation mapping), Medit Splints, and Medit Smile Design (with bracket preview templates) are included at no monthly software fee for owners of the i900 series, i700 series, i600, i500, and T-Series
  • End-to-end orthodontic expansion: The 2026 launch of Medit Orthodontic Suite (backed by FDA 510(k) K241153 via the acquired Progressive Orthodontics platform) adds cephalometric tracing/overlay, multi-scenario simulation, staged aligner export ($1.50/arch preferred price), direct aligner ordering, and SmileStream bracket prescription handoffs
  • Established U.S. subsidiary and warranty options: Medit USA, Inc. in Newport Beach, California, serves as the FDA-registered U.S. importer and orthodontic division hub, and the optional Medit Shield Program offers accidental-drop replacement coverage

Cons

  • Shift from “all apps free” to tiered paid software: Medit has retired Medit Ortho Simulation in the U.S. and EU (effective May 4, 2026) in favor of the paid Medit Orthodontic Suite ($199.99/month or $1.50/arch export for scanner owners), and newer apps like ClinicCAD and AuraVue carry separate per-month or per-export fees
  • Medit iE excludes bundled app entitlements: Although the entry-level Medit iE shares the i900’s 165 g body and 10-bit optics, an iE-only account does not qualify for free access to the Essential App Bundle ($99.99/month) or Medit Model Builder ($99.99/month or $2.50/arch), and Realtime Intelligence and Medit Link Express are excluded or paid extras
  • Contract-manufactured aligner fulfillment: When ordering physical Medit Aligners through Medit Orthodontic Suite ($18–$20/unit), Medit’s privacy policy discloses that patient STL data is transmitted to a third-party processor in Germany (K Line Europe GmbH) for fabrication; U.S. practices should verify shipping lead times, specific 510(k) coverage for fabricated trays, and patient consent requirements
  • High workstation and network specs: Desktop Medit Link and Medit Orthodontic Suite require dedicated NVIDIA GPUs (with CUDA installed for cephalometric AI tracing on Windows) or Apple M-series chips with at least 16 GB RAM, and wireless i900 Mobility performance depends on a stable Wi-Fi 6 / Wi-Fi 6E router environment

How it fits in a practice

In an orthodontic practice, Medit typically serves as the chairside digital impression and in-house 3D printing hub. Assistants can capture full-arch scans using wired wands (i900, i900 classic, i700, i600) connected by a single USB-C cable to a Windows PC or MacBook, or carry an i900 Mobility with an iPad Pro from chair to chair using Medit Link Express for quick new-patient consult scans that automatically sync to the clinic’s Medit Link account. From Medit Link, practices sending cases to external aligner or custom-bracket vendors can export open STL/PLY/OBJ files or connect through Medit’s 150+ partner integrations. Practices running an in-house 3D printing lab can prepare ABO-base or hollow printable arches in Medit Model Builder, design occlusal splints or retainers in Medit Splints, superimpose progress scans in Medit Design, or use Medit Orthodontic Suite to trace lateral cephalograms, compare treatment simulations chairside, and export staged aligner models ($1.50/arch for scanner owners) for in-office thermoforming or direct printing. When comparing scanner quotes, factor in whether you are buying an i900 / i700 / i600 (which unlock the Essential App Bundle and Model Builder for free) versus the entry Medit iE (where those design and model-building apps require paid subscriptions or per-arch export fees).
