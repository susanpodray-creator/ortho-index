DEXIS

Envista’s dental imaging brand (Dental Imaging Technologies Corporation, Quakertown, PA) covering OP 3D and i-CAT CBCT, DEXIS sensors, NOMAD and FOCUS intraoral X-ray, Imprevo/IS scanners, and DTX Studio Clinic software under one connected ecosystem.

HQ: Quakertown, Pennsylvania, USA · Researched 2026-10-02 · Official site

Overview

DEXIS is a dental imaging and digital-workflow brand owned within Envista Holdings Corporation. The live U.S. brand site is dexis.com/en-us. Site footers and imprint pages list the operating legal entity as Dental Imaging Technologies Corporation (DITC) at 450 Commerce Drive, Quakertown, Pennsylvania 18951, so this page uses Quakertown, Pennsylvania, USA for hq. DEXIS careers materials place the brand in Envista’s Equipment & Consumables segment, alongside sibling Envista brands such as Ormco, Nobel Biocare, and Kerr.

Naming and ownership, checked against live official sources (not invented renames):

  • The public brand today is DEXIS. Product families still carry historic trademarks that now sit under that umbrella: ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH / OP 3D, i-CAT, NOMAD, FOCUS, DIGORA, Gendex (as heritage for IXS), and DTX Studio.
  • On March 29, 2022, Envista announced that its KaVo imaging business would be re-branded as DEXIS, bringing CBCT, handheld X-ray, sensors, scanners, and navigated surgical solutions under one imaging name, and combining historic lines including i-CAT, Gendex, Instrumentarium, SOREDEX, and NOMAD with the longstanding DEXIS sensor brand.
  • On April 20, 2022, Envista completed its acquisition of Carestream Dental’s intraoral-scanner business, said that business would be rebranded as DEXIS, and explicitly excluded Carestream Dental’s imaging-equipment and practice-management businesses. Former Carestream CS scanner names (CS 3600 / 3700 / 3800) therefore belong in current DEXIS scanner conversations, not as current Carestream Dental hardware.
  • Footer copyright blocks on current product pages still name Dental Imaging Technologies Corporation and list trademarks held by DITC, PaloDEx Group Oy (ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH, OP, OP 3D, FOCUS, Scan eXam), and Nobel Biocare Services AG (DTX Studio). That matches a multi-lineage portfolio under one commercial brand, not a single corporate rename of every historic company.

Founding date is not a clean single year, so this page omits founded rather than pick one:

  • The About page says DEXIS was “founded over 70 years ago,” tying that language to panoramic heritage (ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH lineage; DEXIS’s own innovation-day article cites Paatero’s work in the late 1940s–1950s and a first commercial panoramic unit in 1964 at the Tuusula, Finland factory).
  • The DEXIS sensor brand itself is described on that same innovation timeline as emerging in the 1990s (digital intraoral sensing / portable digital radiography), with ADA Seal acceptance cited for 2001.
  • The unified Envista imaging brand named DEXIS dates to the 2022 KaVo-imaging rebrand.

Those are different clocks. Treat “70 years,” “20+ years of DEXIS sensors,” and “2022 brand unification” as separate facts, not one founding year.

What this page covers: the parts an orthodontic office is most likely to meet—extraoral CBCT / pan / ceph platforms, intraoral sensors and generators, intraoral scanners, and the DTX Studio / IS ScanFlow software stack. It is informational, not a purchasing recommendation. Availability, configurations, software modules, and regulatory labeling differ by country; DEXIS repeatedly notes products are not available in all regions.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic offices comparing a compact 2D/3D OP 3D platform (EX or LX) with a large-FOV i-CAT FLX system for full maxillofacial / airway / ortho analysis.
  • Practices that want optional cephalometric imaging on an OP 3D unit (ORTHOceph Plus language on OP 3D EX; scalable ceph modality on OP 3D LX).
  • Offices standardizing on DEXIS sensors (Ti2 or IXS) and deciding between wall-mounted FOCUS and handheld NOMAD generation.
  • Teams moving from impressions to digital models with Imprevo / IS 3800 / IS 3800W and wanting scan data to land in DTX Studio Clinic alongside 2D and 3D radiographs.
  • Existing i-CAT sites that still use Tx Studio (Anatomage-powered) planning, or mixed sites evaluating migration toward DTX Studio Clinic.
  • Buyers prepared to evaluate the full implementation: room height and shielding, DICOM/export handoffs, workstation requirements, software modules and renewals, service plans (DEXIS Complete / Connect Pro), training, and local handheld-X-ray rules—not just the cabinet price.

Notable products

Names below match live U.S. manufacturer pages and Envista releases researched October 2, 2026. Availability, FOV licenses, cephalometric arms, scanner SKUs, AI module entitlements, and service tiers vary by country and quote. Performance and “AI-powered” language is manufacturer positioning unless otherwise noted.

Extraoral 3D / CBCT and panoramic

  • ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH OP 3D LX — multimodality 2D/3D platform DEXIS positions as the largest FOV option on its OP 3D family to date. Listed FOVs run from 5 × 5 cm through an optional 15 × 20 cm maxillofacial volume, with stitch-free single-pass capture, upgradeable FOV, optional cephalometric modality, Automatic Dose Control (ADC), MAR / ICE filters, Motion Correction, and DTX Studio Clinic workflow features (focal trough detection, positioning correction, mandibular nerve canal annotation). The product page explicitly calls out orthodontic uses (impacted canines, root resorption, skeletal discrepancies) and a head-support design aimed at soft-tissue profile for ortho and surgical applications.
  • ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH OP 3D EX — complete 2D/3D platform aimed at practices expanding into 3D. Preset FOVs from 5 × 5 cm to 10 × 10 / 10 × 11 cm, with optional 10 × 15 cm; DEXIS lists 66 customizable volume sizes. Upgradable to cephalometric imaging via ORTHOceph Plus. Includes HD pan mode, ADC, MAR / ICE / EPNR filters, Motion Correction, and DTX Studio Clinic automation. Specs published on the page include IGZO TFT detectors, CBCT voxels 80–400 μm, and Windows 10/11 Pro workstation requirements.
  • ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH OP 3D (base family) — still listed on DEXIS extraoral overview materials as an upgradeable pan/CBCT platform with MAR across FOV sizes. Confirm whether a quote is for OP 3D, OP 3D EX, or OP 3D LX; the family name alone is not a configuration.
  • i-CAT FLX V-Series — seated CBCT family with three maximum FOV tiers: V8 (up to 8 × 8 cm), V10 (up to 16 × 10 cm), and V17 (up to 23 × 17 cm). DEXIS says V17 is used most often by orthodontists, OMS, and oral radiologists. Shared traits called out on the product page: full 24.2 × 19.3 cm sensor, PureScan (no offset/stitch half-beam scanning per DEXIS), traditional 2D panoramic on the same sensor, QuickScan / QuickScan+ low-dose protocols, open DICOM, in-office FOV upgrades via software, and Tx Studio planning (powered by Anatomage), including 3D cephalometric and orthodontic modules.

Intraoral sensors and X-ray generation

  • DEXIS Ti2 Sensor — current flagship CMOS intraoral sensor succeeding Titanium. Published specs include 1623 × 1324 px, 19.5 μm pixels, theoretical 25.6 lp/mm (visible 20+), PerfectSize one-size housing, CleanCapture processing, USB 2.0/3.0, and recommended software DTX Studio Clinic 3.4.11.4+ or DEXIS 10.4.1+. DEXIS markets integrated 2D AI findings through DTX Studio Clinic / DEXIS Imaging Software, IoT health monitoring via Connect Pro, and a first-year Premier / Complete service story (confirm exact entitlement on the quote).
  • DEXIS IXS Sensor — positioned as the “new and improved Gendex,” built on the GXS-700 lineage, with size 1 and 2 RiteSize options, open direct integration into many imaging/PMS platforms without a bridge (per DEXIS), on-sensor calibration files, SimplySmart IoT updates, and durability claims versus GXS-700. Size-2 published active area 25.2 × 37.0 mm / 1026 × 1539 px.
  • NOMAD Pro 3 — current handheld intraoral X-ray highlighted on the U.S. intraoral category page and in DEXIS’s September 2025 portfolio announcement (clearer images, enhanced protection, increased power versus prior NOMAD generations). Handheld use is jurisdiction-specific; confirm state registration, training, storage, and shielding rules before purchase.
  • FOCUS — wall-mounted / room-mounted high-frequency intraoral generator with Anti-Drift Mechanism, selectable 60/70 kV, 7 mA, three arm lengths, and DAP dose display.
  • DIGORA Optime — imaging-plate system still listed on the current U.S. intraoral category page. Confirm plate sizes, network vs chairside configuration, and whether plates remain the right path versus sensors for the office’s workflow.

Intraoral scanners

  • DEXIS Imprevo — wireless scanner announced September 16, 2025, powered by IS ScanFlow 2.0. Official materials list dual-sensor imaging, 40 FPS, ~25 mm depth of field, STL/PLY/OBJ and lab-order exports, Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), and DTX Studio Clinic integration. Positioned as the next step beyond the IS 3800 family (slimmer handpiece, faster capture).
  • DEXIS IS 3800W — wireless IS 3800-family scanner still featured on the current scanners page.
  • DEXIS IS 3800 — wired compact scanner in the same family.
  • DEXIS IS Voyager — portable / mobile scanning station listed alongside the handpieces.
  • Historic CS 3600 / 3700 / 3800 units may still appear in installed bases and older Carestream literature; for new purchases, use the current DEXIS IS / Imprevo lineup and ownership story above.

Software

  • DTX Studio Clinic — central AI-assisted imaging hub DEXIS markets for consolidating 2D, 3D, photography, and intraoral scans; diagnostics, patient education, treatment planning, and surgical-guide workflows. Footers note AI functionality lives in this software and that products/modules are not universal by region.
  • DEXIS Imaging Suite — 2D radiograph and clinical-photo hub with integrated AI diagnostics language on the software category page.
  • Tx Studio — i-CAT-oriented treatment-planning application (implant, orthodontic, and surgical tools; Anatomage-powered per i-CAT pages). Do not assume Tx Studio and DTX Studio Clinic are interchangeable licenses.
  • IS ScanFlow — acquisition and review software for DEXIS intraoral scanners; IS Model for digital models; IS Ortho for orthodontic analysis/planning from scans.
  • DEXIS Complete / Connect Pro — service, monitoring, and protection plans referenced across sensors and CBCT pages. Treat these as quote-level entitlements, not free defaults.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Broad imaging ladder under one brand. Sensors, handheld and wall-mounted generators, pan/CBCT, scanners, and planning software can be sourced as one Envista imaging conversation rather than four unrelated vendors.
  • Orthodontics is visible in the catalog. OP 3D LX/EX pages call out ortho indications and ceph upgrades; i-CAT V17 is explicitly positioned for orthodontists; IS Ortho and Tx Studio orthodontic modules appear in the software list.
  • Two CBCT lineages with different strengths. OP 3D EX/LX for modular pan/CBCT/ceph workflows tied to DTX Studio Clinic; i-CAT FLX for seated large-FOV ortho/OMS/airway work and Tx Studio. That choice is useful if the office knows which software path it wants.
  • Sensor depth and install base. DEXIS leans hard on sensor heritage (Ti2 / IXS / former Gendex), open IXS software integration language, and published durability/service packaging—relevant for multi-chair ortho offices that abuse sensors daily.
  • Clear post-2022 ownership map. KaVo imaging → DEXIS brand, plus Carestream scanner business → DEXIS, with Carestream retaining its own CBCT/PMS stack. Buyers can stop mixing those catalogs once the boundary is understood.
  • Active 2025–2026 refresh. Imprevo (Sept 2025), NOMAD Pro 3 and Motion Correction portfolio push (Sept 2025), and continued OP 3D EX/LX promotion show the brand is not frozen on legacy SKUs alone.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Many historic names under one roof. DEXIS, OP 3D, i-CAT, NOMAD, Gendex, Instrumentarium, SOREDEX, KaVo imaging, and former Carestream scanner SKUs all appear in the wild. Match the written quote to the exact current model, not the family story.
  • Two software centers of gravity. DTX Studio Clinic is the “one hub” message on most new pages; Tx Studio remains central to i-CAT materials; older DEXIS Imaging Suite installs still exist. Ask which license, which version, and which migration path you are buying.
  • Options and plans drive real cost. FOV upgrades, ceph arms, Motion Correction availability, AI findings, IS Ortho, surgical-guide modules, Connect Pro, and Complete coverage may be separate. Get an itemized entitlement and renewal schedule.
  • Handheld X-ray is not plug-and-play everywhere. NOMAD Pro 3 may be attractive chairside, but state and local rules for handheld operation, training, and storage can block or complicate deployment. Confirm before the PO.
  • Marketing claims need context. “#1 sensor,” radiation-leakage comparisons, scan-time records, AI automation, and dose-comparable-to-pan language come from DEXIS or cited manufacturer data. Evaluate in a demo with your own protocols; they are not universal performance guarantees.
  • Ecosystem pull vs open files. IXS open-integration language and i-CAT open DICOM help, but the tightest AI and guide workflows are strongest inside DTX Studio Clinic / IS ScanFlow. Test real handoffs to your aligner, referral, and archive tools.
  • Regional and catalog lag. Footers say products are not available in all regions; older OP 3D or scanner pages can linger next to newer EX/LX/Imprevo SKUs. A live page is not proof every configuration is orderable in your country today.

Evaluation notes

  • Decide the required record set first: sensors only, pan ± ceph, limited-FOV CBCT, large-FOV ortho/airway CBCT, scanners, or a full stack.
  • For CBCT, compare OP 3D EX vs LX vs i-CAT V8/V10/V17 on maximum FOV, ceph option, seated vs standing, software (DTX vs Tx Studio), Motion Correction, and service plan—not on brand familiarity alone.
  • For sensors, decide Ti2 vs IXS (one-size PerfectSize vs size 1/2; DTX-centric AI path vs open PMS integration claims) and confirm holder compatibility and warranty replacement terms in writing.
  • If adding a scanner, validate Imprevo vs IS 3800W against tip autoclave cycle limits, export formats, IS Ortho needs, and the exact IS ScanFlow / DTX Studio versions on the proposal.
  • Verify DICOM / STL / PLY / JPEG export and a real round-trip into your orthodontic planning and referral workflow before go-live.
  • For handheld generators, get written confirmation of local regulatory allowance and training requirements before paying a deposit.
  • Ask which proposal claims are included hardware, which are licensed software, and which require DEXIS Complete or Connect Pro.

Bottom line

DEXIS today is Envista’s unified dental imaging brand—sensors and NOMAD heritage, ORTHOPANTOMOGRAPH / OP 3D and i-CAT CBCT lines, FOCUS and DIGORA extras, plus the Imprevo/IS scanner family that absorbed the former Carestream scanner business—tied together mainly through DTX Studio Clinic (and Tx Studio on the i-CAT side). For an orthodontic office, the practical shortlist is usually Ti2 or IXS sensors, OP 3D EX/LX or i-CAT FLX for extraoral imaging, and Imprevo or IS 3800W if digital impressions matter.

The purchasing traps are naming and software. Get the exact OP 3D or i-CAT configuration, ceph/FOV options, and module list in writing, and do not treat old KaVo, Gendex, or Carestream CS scanner labels as current catalog truth without checking the live DEXIS quote. Legal entity on the site is Dental Imaging Technologies Corporation in Quakertown; parent company is Envista.
