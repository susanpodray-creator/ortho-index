Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics

Specialty orthodontics unit of Dentsply Sirona (Nasdaq: XRAY). After the 2020 exit from traditional GAC brackets, bands, tubes, and wires, the public catalog is SureSmile digital appliances plus Essix plastics and IPR—not a GAC or SureSmile rename.

HQ: Sarasota, Florida, USA · Researched 2026-08-27 · Official site

Overview

Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics is still the official public name for the specialty ortho unit of DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc. (Nasdaq: XRAY). It is not a standalone company, not a GAC rename, and not a SureSmile rename. The live U.S. hub is the Orthodontics category on dentsplysirona.com. The legal manufacturer on DS location pages and the 2025 catalog footer is Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics Inc., 7290 26th Court East, Sarasota, Florida. Parent DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc. remains in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Do not invent a house-name change. Searching only “GAC,” “In-Ovation,” or “Raintree Essix” will find historic catalogs and leftover PDFs. It will miss what DS actually sells in 2026.

How the names stack (verified, not a rebrand story):

  • GAC International — DENTSPLY’s 1990s ortho acquisition (official History page). After the February 29, 2016 DENTSPLY–Sirona merger, trade press reported GAC and Raintree Essix folded under the new Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics division (2017). In-Ovation (R, C, L, mini, X), MicroArch / OmniArch / PLUS twins, Sentalloy, and BioForce were GAC-era lines. They are not homepage brands now.
  • Raintree Essix — the historic thermoforming-plastics company. The current public name on the ortho hub and shop is Essix® (PLUS, ACE, C+, TRAY-RITE, Nightguard Laminate, Dual Laminate, A+). Essix survived the 2020 hardware exit.
  • SureSmile® — the current clinical system, acquired with OraMetrix in March 2018. It belongs to DS. A full SureSmile page is queued separately; treat this as the parent-division map, not that product brief.
  • Byte — DS bought the DTC aligner in January 2021. Sales and marketing of Byte Aligners and Impression Kits were voluntarily suspended October 24, 2024; on January 14, 2025 DS said it would not reinstate those kits for new patients. Current 10-K language: Orthodontics “previously included” Byte. Not a current buy.

The 2020 hardware exit is the fact that matters for a brackets-category page. Contemporaneous coverage of DS’s Q2 2020 results, plus later trade notes (RMO’s September 2020 buy-back offer; distributor statements), describe an official exit from traditional brackets, bands, tubes, and wires. The August 2025 Orthodontic Product Catalog posted on the live U.S. hub has no In-Ovation, MicroArch, OmniArch, or GAC twin SKUs. What remains of “fixed appliances” is digital: SureSmile® IDB trays and SureSmile® robotically bent wires, used with brackets you source elsewhere. DS’s later annual-report wording matches that: the Orthodontics category is the SureSmile brand—cloud software to prescribe clear aligners, robotically bent wires, and digital IDB trays—plus retainers and whitening.

Parent founding years (Dentists’ Supply Company 1899; Sirona lineage 1877; merger 2016) are not a founding year for this division. This draft does not invent one.

Product lines

Official names below match the live U.S. Orthodontics hub and the August 2025 global catalog as researched in August 2026. Availability varies by market. Refinement rates, treatment-time cuts, “practically invisible,” and VPro comfort/acceleration language are manufacturer positioning. Confirm country clearance and IFUs before you order.

SureSmile® (brief — full page later)

  • SureSmile® Software — browser-based, clinician-controlled planning. Catalog copy: upload digital impressions, photos, optional X-rays/CBCT; Digital Lab review on full-service cases.
  • SureSmile® Full-Service Aligners — lab-made aligners. Package names in the 2025 catalog: COMPLETE (flat-rate, refinements up to three years from production, Patient Kit, VPro, U.S. Whitening Kit) and SELECT (pay-per-aligner; Patient Kit and Whitening Kit; VPro optional). Vendor claim: no refinements on 3 of 4 cases (data on file / JCO citation in catalog).
  • SureSmile® In-Office Clear Aligner Design — same Ortho platform; you or the Digital Lab stage, then print models in-office or order printed models.
  • SureSmile® Retainers — ordered by arch from a finished SureSmile stage or a new scan; also listed for patients not treated with SureSmile. Catalog: designed to wear up to 12 months when cared for as directed.
  • SureSmile® Robotically Bent Wires — custom Shape Memory Alloy wires from a 3D target setup; each wire inspected, tracked, shipped. This is the remaining DS wire SKU, not a Sentalloy / BioForce catalog.
  • SureSmile® IDB — digitally designed indirect-bonding trays (lab-printed or STL export). Placement toolset, not a DS bracket line. SureSmile doctor pages say you pick a supported bracket set (including third-party “For IDB” libraries).
  • SureSmile™ Simulator (DS Core) — 3D smile visualization before a prescribed plan. Catalog/history footnotes: requires a DS Core subscription and compatible IOS (often Primescan); simulation is not a treatment plan; outcomes may differ; not in all markets.
  • SureSmile® VPro™ — high-frequency vibration device, mouthpiece, and app. Bundled on COMPLETE; optional on SELECT. Vendor may-help language on comfort and anterior movement.

Primescan / Primescan Connect and Axeos sit next door as DS imaging, not as Orthodontics SKUs. The hub’s “Orthodontic Treatments with Primescan” line is: send scans to a partner, or stay inside DS with SureSmile.

Essix® plastics (current consumable brand)

Still a homepage brand. 2025 selection chart (intended uses per 510(k) K062828 / design controls):

  • Essix® PLUS™ — retainers and aligners; accepts acrylics/bonding agents; temporary bridges
  • Essix® ACE® — retainers and aligners; temporary anterior bridges; shop copy: not made with natural rubber latex or BPA
  • Essix® C+® — aligners and retainers positioned for bruxers
  • Essix® TRAY-RITE® — whitening trays (catalog: not a medical device in most markets)
  • Essix® Nightguard Laminate and Essix® Dual Laminate — nightguards / TMJ-style splints (medical-device material not CE marked per catalog footnotes)
  • Essix® A+® — bite planes and related appliance uses on the chart

IPR and in-office fabrication

  • SpaceFile® IPR — Swiss-made diamond files (hand-held, removable grip; historic air-driven handpiece not assumed current in every market). Still the named IPR brand on the U.S. hub.
  • Also in the 2025 catalog: Flexview® strips, Diamond IPR Strips, Qwikstrip™ (not a DS trademark; catalog notes not CE marked), serrated strips, IPR gauge set.
  • Thermoforming: Drufomat® Scan pressure machine plus trimming/finishing kits. Attachment bonding pages reuse DS restorative composites (TPH / Ceram.x / Neo Spectra ST) rather than an ortho-specific adhesive line.

Not current DS Orthodontics products

In-Ovation® (R, C, L, mini, X), MicroArch® / OmniArch® / PLUS, Ovation ceramic, Resolve tubes, Snap-Fit bands, Sentalloy®, Neo Sentalloy, BioForce®, Accu-Force, and the old GAC instrument/commodity catalog. Leftover PDFs on dentsplysirona.com and third-party shops do not make them live house brands. Byte aligner systems and impression kits are not offered to new patients.

Who it is for

  • Specialists and GP-ortho offices that want a clinician-directed digital system (aligners, custom wires, IDB) from the same DS account that already runs Primescan / DS Core
  • Practices that still thermoform Essix retainers, aligners, or nightguards in-house and need current sheet names—not a Raintree catalog reprint
  • Teams doing aligner IPR who already stock SpaceFile or the adjacent strip SKUs
  • Offices leaving GAC / In-Ovation inventory that need a current map: DS is not your bracket replenishment vendor anymore; SureSmile IDB + robotically bent wires are the only DS-branded fixed-appliance path
  • Multi-country groups that must check local clearance—catalog footnotes flag CE/non-device items, VPro/Whitening kits, and Simulator availability

Pros

  • Naming on the live hub is simpler than the 2017 stack: Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics (division) + SureSmile (system) + Essix (plastics) + SpaceFile (IPR)
  • One digital planning surface can prescribe aligners, IDB trays, and robotically bent wires—useful if you already mix modalities and do not want a second cloud login
  • Essix remains a widely recognized in-office plastic line with a published use chart and shop SKUs
  • Legal entity and Sarasota address are public on DS’s own locations page and catalog footer
  • Honest about what it is not: after 2020, this is not a mega-catalog twin/SL bracket house competing with AO, Ormco, or RMO on metal kits

Cons / watch-outs

  • This is a brackets-category page for a company that exited brackets. If you need In-Ovation or MicroArch today, you are shopping equivalents or leftover dealer stock—not DS current production
  • Naming leftovers will still trip purchasing: GAC, Raintree Essix, OraMetrix, Byte, and old Islandia / “Dentsply GAC International” addresses appear on PDFs and invoices from the last decade
  • SureSmile is a system (software + Digital Lab + appliances), not a box of brackets. IDB assumes a supported third-party bracket library; confirm that list before you promise a tray workflow
  • Byte’s 2024–2025 shutdown is a compliance and brand-trust event sitting next to the clinician-directed SureSmile story. Staff will get asked. It is not a current product
  • Refinement-rate, 30% shorter treatment (wires), VPro, and Simulator claims are vendor / study-cited marketing. Catalog itself footnotes that Simulator output is not a prescribed plan
  • Mega-vendor complexity: DS Core subscription, SureSmile registration, Primescan validation, and country-specific kits (Whitening, VPro, CE flags) are easy to mis-order if you treat this like a commodity cart
  • Parent HQ (Charlotte) and ortho entity (Sarasota) are both “official.” Use Sarasota for Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics Inc.; Charlotte for DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc.

Buying notes

  • Start at the U.S. Orthodontics hub and the 2025 Orthodontic Product Catalog (linked there as of December 1, 2025). The old /products/orthodontics.html path 404s
  • U.S. shop and SureSmile customer care are account-gated. Essix ACE shop copy can require a registered SureSmile customer for some SKUs—confirm with your DS / SureSmile rep, not a bookmark from 2018
  • SureSmile doctor site: suresmile.com. Phone listed on historic SureSmile materials: 1-888-672-6387 (also on current Essix shop “Become a SureSmile Customer” copy)
  • Do not write POs to “GAC,” “Dentsply GAC,” or “Raintree Essix.” The current legal line is Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics Inc.
  • Product availability varies by market. Catalog: returns need an RMA; SKUs change

Bottom line

Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics is the real current name. GAC is history; SureSmile is the product system, not the division rename; Essix is the plastics that stayed. Use this page to stop ordering dead bracket SKUs and to point the digital/aligner conversation at SureSmile—then wait for the dedicated SureSmile brief for workflow depth.
