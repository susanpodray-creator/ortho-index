American Orthodontics

Specialty orthodontic manufacturer offering brackets, wires, instruments, and practice support for orthodontists worldwide.

HQ: Sheboygan, Wisconsin, USA · Founded 1968 · Researched 2026-08-23 · Official site

Overview

American Orthodontics (AO) is a specialty manufacturer based in Sheboygan, Wisconsin. The company focuses on orthodontists with brackets (metal and aesthetic), wires, auxiliaries, and instruments. Many startup and established offices know AO for competitive catalog breadth and direct specialty sales support.

AO is not primarily an aligner company; practices evaluating clear aligners will usually look elsewhere or use AO alongside an aligner partner.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic specialists building or refreshing bracket and wire inventory
  • New practices comparing Independence-style starter packages and catalogs
  • Offices that want metal twin, self-ligating, and ceramic options from one specialty vendor
  • Teams that value U.S.-based specialty manufacturing and education events

Notable products

  • Empower — self-ligating bracket platform (verify current generations on AO’s site)
  • Iconix / aesthetic brackets — ceramic and cosmetic options
  • Metal twin brackets — high-volume workhorse lines
  • Wires and auxiliaries — NiTi, stainless, and related inventory
  • Instruments — pliers and chairside tools sold into specialty practices

Product names and package contents change; treat catalog PDFs and your rep as source of truth for ordering.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Orthodontist-focused catalog and sales model
  • Broad mix of bracket styles and wires
  • Strong recognition among U.S. specialty practices

Cons

  • Clear aligner story is not the core of the brand
  • Large catalogs mean you still need disciplined SKU selection for startups
  • Pricing and promotions vary by relationship and volume—compare apples to apples
