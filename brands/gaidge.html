Gaidge

U.S. orthodontic practice analytics software from Gaidge, LLC, now a PracticeTek brand. It pulls data nightly from a practice's management system into KPI dashboards and regional benchmarks, and adds new-patient conversion, digital intake forms, financial planning tools, and a consulting arm.

HQ: Georgia, USA (parent PracticeTek, San Diego) · Researched 2026-11-20 · Official site

Get a quote Claim this brand

Overview

Gaidge is cloud-based business analytics software built for orthodontic practices. It is sold by Gaidge, LLC, the entity named in its terms of service and privacy policy. Instead of staff running and compiling end-of-month reports by hand, Gaidge pulls data from the practice’s management system (PMS) every night. It turns that data into dashboards, goal tracking and comparisons against other Gaidge practices.

The product line has grown beyond analytics. It now includes a new-patient conversion module (Consult Manager), digital intake forms, financial planning tools sold as an “Executive Membership,” and a consulting team branded Gaidge 360 Consulting.

Ownership. In June 2022, trade press reported that Gaidge, topsOrtho, EasyRx, Oasys Crossfire and OrthoMinds had “joined forces under PracticeTek,” a group of healthcare software and payments companies, and would remain independently operated. In March 2023, Orthodontic Products described “the Gaidge and impact360 Consulting acquisition by PracticeTek,” with the consulting firm impact360 renamed Gaidge 360 Consulting. PracticeTek’s own website lists Gaidge among its brands. PracticeTek has not published deal terms, and we did not find a dated official announcement of the acquisition itself, so treat the exact timing as reported rather than confirmed.

Location. Gaidge’s website does not publish a street address. Its 2020 and 2021 press releases were datelined Gainesville, Georgia, and a 2023 release was datelined Alpharetta, Georgia. Its customer terms are governed by Georgia law. The parent company, PracticeTek, lists its headquarters in San Diego, California. Ortho Index lists Gaidge as Georgia-based on the strength of its own datelines and terms; this has not been confirmed by the company.

Gaidge’s own website does not state a founding year, and third-party figures disagree, so we have left it out.

Gaidge is not a medical device, and we found no FDA clearance associated with it. None would be expected for business analytics software.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic practice owners and managers who want monthly and daily KPIs without assembling reports from the PMS by hand.
  • Treatment coordinators and front-desk teams managing new-patient exams, observation recalls and pending cases (Consult Manager).
  • Multi-location practices and groups, as well as consultants, study clubs and financial advisers, that compare performance across offices. The company says Gaidge supports multi-practice tracking.
  • AAO members. In 2025 the American Association of Orthodontists added Gaidge Analytics to its AAO Endorsed Programs, and says it negotiated $1,000 off onboarding for members. An endorsement is a membership benefit, not a clinical or technical evaluation by Ortho Index.

Notable products

  • Gaidge Analytics: The core dashboard product. The company says it offers 80+ practice metrics and 35+ reporting dashboards, grouped by area:

    • production and collections (net production vs. net collection, average contract amount, discounts and write-offs, production per doctor or staff hour)
    • new-patient exams and starts (case acceptance, origin of starts, marketing referrals)
    • observation patients
    • A/R and delinquency
    • schedule statistics (for example, patients past their estimated completion date, active patients with no appointment)
    • treatment efficiency

    It also offers benchmarks against other Gaidge offices. The company says these draw on “over 2,000 offices, across 8 regions,” based on AAO constituency regions.

  • Gaidge Consult Manager: Launched in May 2023. It’s a dashboard for the new-patient process: tasks such as insurance verification, forms and confirmations; digital intake forms; treatment and payment presentation with a payment “slider”; remote contract acceptance by email; and pending and observation follow-up and reporting. Fees can be collected at presentation through Gaidge Pay, which the company says supports card payments and text-based payment links. In April 2024, PracticeTek announced that Consult Manager was available for practices using Tops.

  • Gaidge Forms: Digital health-history, consent, e-signature and release forms with practice branding, website embedding, text or email delivery and completion reminders. The company describes the forms as “HIPAA-compliant.”

  • Gaidge Executive Membership: A higher tier that adds the Overhead Expense Tracker, which the company says syncs nightly with QuickBooks and Xero, and Practice Projections, a forecasting tool for working backward from production goals to the starts, exams and new patients needed.

  • Gaidge 360 Consulting: Practice-management consulting on operations, scheduling, HR and systems change, offered alongside the software.

  • Gaidge Mobile: The FAQ describes a mobile app that uses the same login and role-based permissions as the web dashboard. Administrative settings still require the desktop version.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Built specifically around orthodontic metrics (starts, observation, case acceptance, contract values) rather than generic dental KPIs.
  • Automated nightly import from several major ortho PMS platforms. The FAQ names Carestream (OrthoTrac), Cloud 9, Dolphin, Ortho2 (ViewPoint and Edge) and Tops, and the AAO announcement adds OrthoFi.
  • Peer benchmarks by region, which practices can’t easily build on their own.
  • One vendor for analytics, new-patient workflow, forms and consulting, which may reduce the number of separate tools.
  • AAO endorsement, which the AAO says follows its own vetting process.

Cons

  • Performance figures on the website (for example, “12% more starts,” “10% increased collections” and “25% larger” practices in the first year) are company claims based on its own user data, not independent studies. A practice that chooses analytics software may differ from one that doesn’t.
  • Benchmarks only reflect Gaidge subscribers, who may not be representative of all orthodontic practices.
  • Pricing isn’t published apart from the AAO onboarding discount. Analytics, Consult Manager, Forms and Executive Membership appear to be priced separately, so get a written quote.
  • Ownership changed in 2022–2023, and the parent owns other ortho software brands, including Tops and EasyRx. Ask about the product roadmap and how integrations with competing PMS vendors will be maintained.
  • Consult Manager, Forms and Gaidge Pay handle patient information and payments, so data-protection and business associate terms matter. The FAQ says analytics data transfers use anonymous identifiers. The privacy policy says patient data, which “has historically included” protected health information, is handled under service agreements with clinics. The terms include a business associate clause “if applicable.” These are the company’s statements and we have not verified them.
  • Only practices on supported PMS platforms get the automated import.

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

Gaidge sits on top of the practice-management system rather than replacing it. The PMS (for example Ortho2, Cloud 9, Dolphin or OrthoTrac from Carestream Dental) remains the system of record. Gaidge reads from it nightly to report and benchmark. Consult Manager and Forms reach further into the front-office workflow and may overlap with tools a practice already has, such as PMS-native forms, OrthoFi treatment presentation, or other payment and intake systems.

When evaluating it, ask:

  • Whether your exact PMS version is supported for nightly import, and which metrics depend on how your team codes appointments and statuses in the PMS.
  • What patient-level data leaves the PMS for each module (Analytics vs. Consult Manager vs. Forms vs. Gaidge Pay), where it is hosted, and whether a business associate agreement is signed.
  • How benchmark peer groups are formed and how many practices are in your comparison group.
  • Pricing per location for each module, onboarding fees, and contract term.
  • Who processes Gaidge Pay transactions, and the fees.
  • How you would export your data and history if you cancelled.

These are diligence questions, not a recommendation to buy.
