DynaFlex

Privately held U.S. orthodontic supplier, full-service appliance laboratory, clear aligner manufacturer, and dental sleep device maker (Ortho Solutions, LC d/b/a DynaFlex) in Lake St. Louis, Missouri, founded in 1965.

HQ: Lake St. Louis, Missouri, USA · Founded 1965 · Researched 2026-10-26 · Official site

Overview

DynaFlex is a privately held U.S. orthodontic product supplier, custom appliance laboratory, clear aligner manufacturer, and dental sleep medicine lab based in Lake St. Louis, Missouri, west of St. Louis. The company states that it began in 1965 as an orthodontic laboratory and relocated in 2021 from St. Ann, Missouri, to a newly built 70,000-square-foot headquarters and continuing-education facility at 8050 Hawk Ridge Trail in Lake St. Louis.

The operating legal entity is Ortho Solutions, LC (written as Ortho Solutions, LLC in its terms of sale) doing business as DynaFlex. You will also see Dyna Flex in older FDA 510(k) submissions and Dyna Flex of Missouri, L.P. in legacy federal vendor URLs, all tied to the same Missouri business. It is privately owned and led by its chief executive officer/owner, with no parent conglomerate.

DynaFlex organizes its business around five dental markets: Orthodontic Products, Orthodontic Laboratory, SmileShare™ Clear Aligners, Digital Office Solutions (3D.Ortho® and DynaFusion®), and Dental Sleep Medicine. In May 2023, DynaFlex expanded its sleep division by acquiring WholeYou/Respire Medical and moving Respire device production to its Lake St. Louis facility.

In the U.S.: FDA’s public database shows an active domestic establishment registration (registration number 1937100) for Ortho Solutions LC at the Lake St. Louis address, with 60 device listings across orthodontic supplies, lab appliances, clear aligners, and oral sleep devices. Four 510(k) clearances are on file under the applicant name Dyna Flex: two for sequential clear aligners (K121396 in 2012 and K190583 in 2019, listed in FDA registration data under the proprietary name EZ Align), one for anti-snoring oral appliances (K103076 in 2011, covering the Dorsal Appliance and Adjustable Herbst), and one for ceramic brackets and buttons (K230225 in 2023, listed as the Norris Clear System). The registration also lists seven 510(k) clearances originally cleared under Respire Medical (including K111207, K131138, K150572, K152292, K170692, K192127, and K214096) for the Respire Blue and Pink sleep series, plus third-party clearances for ceramic brackets and resins. Confirm the regulatory status of specific items you plan to use.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic practices that want a single vendor for catalog chairside supplies (brackets, tubes, wires, elastics, instruments) and custom lab fabrication
  • Offices looking for digital fixed appliances, including 3D metal-printed bands and semi-custom or CBCT-guided fully custom MARPE expanders
  • Clinicians who use chairside or lab-integrated Class II and Class III non-compliance correctors (the CS®5 system and Series 2000® lab appliances)
  • Practices seeking a lab-fabricated clear aligner system (SmileShare™) or thermoforming sheet materials (Glacier® LTR and TMP) for in-office retainer and aligner production
  • Dental and orthodontic practices that treat snoring and obstructive sleep apnea and need milled, acrylic, or Medicare-verified (E0486) oral appliances

Notable products

  • The Norris 20/26™ Bracket System: passive self-ligating brackets with a reduced .020" x .026" slot designed to pair with .019" x .025" wires, available in cobalt-chromium metal and clear ceramic (cleared under K230225), alongside Norris Extra Broad archwires and opening tools
  • CS®5 Class II & Class III Corrector: chairside non-compliance inter-arch NiTi coil spring system (7 mm and 10 mm springs) with Twist-Lock, Self-Ligating, Traditional, and Bondable pivot options and Nylok-coated hybrid screws
  • Catalog orthodontic supplies: conventional brackets (including Atlas Mini and Gold’n Braces), buccal tubes, archwires (including Infinity aesthetic wires), bands, elastomerics, Precision Aligner Buttons (G2 metal and clear buttons shaped for aligner cutouts), bonding supplies, hand instruments, mirrors, and IPR supplies
  • Glacier® thermoforming sheets: Glacier LTR (Long-Term Retention PTEG sheets in .035" / 0.89 mm and .040" / 1.0 mm) and Glacier TMP (Tooth Movement Pro .030" / 0.76 mm aligner material)
  • Orthodontic Laboratory (fixed and removable): 3D Metal Printed Bands (direct metal printed from intraoral scans without separators), Semi-Custom and Fully Custom MARPE (including CBCT-guided TAD planning), Series 2000® spring-loaded non-compliance expanders and sagittal appliances (including the CS2000 Class III setup), Herbst, MARA, Wilson 3D, and removable Hawley, Frankel, and Bionator appliances
  • SmileShare™ Clear Aligners: lab-made aligners offered in tiered packages—Individual (up to 5 aligners), Fast 5 (up to 10), Perfect 10 (up to 20), Supreme 15 (up to 30), Terrific 20 (up to 40), and Unlimited—with straight-cut, scalloped, or modified scalloped laser trimlines, plus hybrid protocols combining Series 2000 appliances with aligners
  • Digital workflows (DynaFusion® and 3D.Ortho®): DynaFusion is a free cloud portal for digital prescriptions, STL uploads, custom appliance templates, and SmileShare setup reviews; 3D.Ortho handles digital file transfer, 3D model printing, and indefinite digital model storage
  • Dental Sleep Medicine devices: CAD/CAM 5-axis milled and traditional oral appliances including the DynaFlex Dorsal®, Adjustable Herbst®, dreamTAP / TAP 3, EMA, Respire series (Blue+, Blue+ EF, Pink AT), and nylon/temporary devices (White Night™ and DynaFlex Gateway), including four devices with Medicare E0486 verification

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Long operating history (since 1965) with U.S.-based manufacturing and laboratory fabrication in Missouri
  • Unusually broad scope under one account: catalog supplies, digital and conventional ortho lab work, clear aligners, thermoforming plastics, and sleep apnea devices
  • Single login via DynaFusion covers digital Rx submission, scan uploads from major intraoral scanners, aligner setup review, and supply ordering
  • Modern digital lab options—such as 3D metal-printed bands that skip separator appointments and CBCT-planned MARPE designs—sit alongside traditional wire-and-acrylic appliances
  • Verifiable FDA establishment registration with 60 device listings and 510(k) clearances across aligners, ceramic brackets, and sleep devices

Cons

  • Product and lab pricing is gated behind a registered doctor account (/order/login/); public pages do not show list prices or per-case aligner fees
  • Marketing pages include strong performance claims (such as percentage efficiency gains for the Norris 20/26 bracket or crack-resistance multiples for Glacier LTR) that are the company’s own rather than independent head-to-head trials
  • Lab terms state standard turnaround of about 7–10 days for orthodontic appliances and three weeks for sleep devices, plus a $100 fee after the second case cancellation in a year
  • Supplies have a 60-day warranty (30 days for a full-refund return, 31–60 days with a 20% restocking fee), and orthodontic lab fit is warranted for 90 days
  • Corporate naming varies across records (Ortho Solutions, LC / LLC, DynaFlex, and older Dyna Flex filings, plus EZ Align vs. SmileShare in FDA aligner listings), which takes care when cross-checking regulatory documents

How it fits in a practice

Practices typically use DynaFlex either as an all-in-one lab and supply partner or for specific clinical niches—such as 3D metal-printed banded expanders, CS5 Class II/III springs, Norris 20/26 self-ligating brackets, or dorsal/Respire sleep appliances. Because DynaFusion accepts open STL files and direct connections from major intraoral scanners (iTero, 3Shape TRIOS, CEREC, Carestream, Planmeca, and others), a digital office can submit lab scripts, review SmileShare aligner or custom MARPE setups, and reorder chairside supplies from one portal. Practices that thermoform in-house can also buy Glacier LTR and TMP sheets directly. Before committing volume, set up an account to review current catalog and lab fee schedules, verify scanner integration, check turnaround times to your location, and confirm the specific FDA listing or 510(k) for each device you plan to prescribe.
