Forestadent

German specialty manufacturer (FORESTADENT Bernhard Förster GmbH) of brackets, wires, expansion screws, TADs, and appliances—BioQuick, 2D Lingual, Sprint, OrthoEasy, SARA, plus FAS aligners—made in Pforzheim and sold through a U.S. subsidiary.

HQ: Pforzheim, Germany · Founded 1907 · Researched 2026-08-28 · Official site

Overview

FORESTADENT is the public brand of FORESTADENT Bernhard Förster GmbH, a family-owned specialty manufacturer based at Westliche Karl-Friedrich-Str. 151, Pforzheim, Germany. The live English hub is forestadent.com/en. Managing directors named on the About page are Bernhard and Stefan Förster. Official copy still says fourth-generation family ownership and production in the Black Forest (“Made in Black Forest”).

How the names stack (verified, not a rebrand story):

  • Bernhard Förster GmbH (1907) — jewelry hardware. Official History: Bernhard Förster (1879–1963) founded the company at 27 making chain fasteners. About Us: spring rings and chain fasteners.
  • Foresta watches (1934–1972) — same company, different trade. History: Foresta was the watch brand; the last Foresta watch shipped in 1972. Do not treat Foresta as the current ortho name.
  • FORESTADENT (1974–) — ortho entry. History: Rolf Förster sold the first expansion screw in 1974 and used existing precision machinery for orthodontic parts. About Us: that shift “mark[ed] the evolution of FORESTA into FORESTADENT.” The brand is more than 50 years in orthodontics; the legal company is older.
  • FORESTADENT USA (1995–) — wholly owned U.S. subsidiary. The live Contact page lists 1868 Craigshire Road, St. Louis, MO 63145, phones 1-314-878-5985 and 1-800-721-4940, and orders@forestadentusa.com. Older flyers and directories still show 2315 Weldon Parkway (63146) or Earth City / Rider Trail addresses. Use the Contact page for current U.S. paper, not a 2010 PDF.

The 1950 Pforzheim HQ address is still the production site (History: rebuilt after WWII destruction; 2012 expansion/modernization with a stated investment of over €13 million). About Us also lists subsidiaries in France (Strasbourg), Spain (Madrid), and the USA, plus a dealer network (Contact: FORESTADENT Ltd in Milton Keynes for the UK/Ireland; Canada through independent dealers). Vendor stats on the homepage and History page—118 or 120 countries, 70% export, “well over 10,000 items”—are company figures, not independently audited here.

This is a fixed-appliance specialty house that also sells aligners, not an aligner-platform company with a leftover bracket SKU. FAS® is the digital/aligner lane; BioQuick® / Sprint® / 2D® Lingual remain the chairside core.

Who it’s for

  • Specialists who want German-made metal, ceramic, self-ligating, and lingual brackets from one catalog rather than a mega-vendor aligner stack
  • Offices that still run expansion screws, lab wires, and removable appliances and care that Forestadent’s ortho story started there in 1974
  • Teams adding skeletal anchorage (OrthoEasy® Pin / Pal) or a fixed Class II telescopic (SARA®) without changing bracket houses
  • Practices treating nickel-sensitive patients who need a published nickel-low / nickel-free map (Sprint MIM family, Comfort Line tubes, Betaflex / BT3, FORESTANIT Ni-Free, titanium screws)—then verify the current material list and IFU
  • Doctors who use Roth, MBT / McLaughlin–Bennett 5.0, Trevisi, FACE, Roncone PDS, Ricketts, or standard edgewise prescriptions listed on Forestadent’s philosophies page
  • U.S. offices that will order through FORESTADENT USA (St. Louis) and Catalogue 9 US, not a European web cart
  • Groups mixing FAS® aligners or FAS® Kids with Forestadent fixed appliances / OrthoEasy hybrid setups—and who will treat FAS planning claims as vendor positioning until they see cases

Notable products

Official names below match the live English Products & Solutions hub and linked product pages as researched in August 2026. Availability varies by country. Slot sizes, prescriptions, and kit contents belong on the current catalog or with your Forestadent / USA rep—not on this page. “Flattest in the world,” “smallest twin,” friction cuts, treatment-time cuts, “no AI,” and extraction-avoidance language are manufacturer positioning.

Self-ligating metal

  • BioQuick® — current active self-ligating metal line. Official page: fifth generation of the Quick® family, available since 2023; redesigned clip with a large vestibular opening (vendor: open with scaler/explorer, no special instrument); occlusal force-application point on the clip; .015″ auxiliary slot; molar BioQuick SKUs for first and second molars. Trade press in 2010 already called BioQuick the third generation of Quick—so “BioQuick” on an old invoice is not automatically the 2023 clip.
  • BioPassive® — passive companion to BioQuick. Deeper slot / more archwire clearance positioned for leveling; vertical mark on the mesial ligature wing to tell it apart from BioQuick; .016″ × .016″ auxiliary slot.

Ceramic / aesthetic

  • QuicKlear® III — third-generation self-ligating ceramic (translucent body, rectangular clip).
  • TruKlear® — self-ligating ceramic that Forestadent positions as fully metal-free (body and locking mechanism both ceramic) for aesthetic and nickel-allergy use.
  • GLAM® — one-piece polycrystalline ceramic conventionally ligated twin; matt/translucent positioning; vendor pairs it with 2D Lingual for hybrid/deep-bite setups and with the Pauls debonding tool.

Conventional metal twins (Sprint family)

  • Sprint® II — full-size conventionally ligated twin.
  • Mini Sprint® II — second-generation mini twin; Forestadent course copy also ties Mini Sprint II to the McLaughlin–Bennett 5.0 appliance. Confirm that pairing on the current kit, not a course flyer alone.
  • Micro Sprint® — smallest Sprint twin; vendor slogan “smallest twin bracket in the world”; site credits a Red Dot Design Award and a German Design Award.
  • Nickel-allergy page: Sprint-family metal brackets and Comfort Line buccal tubes are MIM with stated nickel ≤ 0.1%. That is a material claim—read the current material list.

Lingual

  • 2D® Lingual — self-ligating lingual line Forestadent markets as “the flattest lingual brackets in the world,” with stated height 1.3–1.65 mm, occlusal wire insertion via a vertical slot, direct bond without lab setup, and twelve variants. BioLingual® / Titanol® Lingual wires sit next door on the archwire hub.

Archwires and springs (named families on the live wire hub)

  • BioStarter® and BioTorque®
  • BioLingual® and Titanol® Lingual
  • DuoForce® — thermoactive copper-nickel-titanium with two programmed force zones (lighter anterior, heavier posterior); oral-temperature activation ~32 °C per the product page
  • Titanol® Low Force
  • Nanda archwires, including the Nanda BT3 Intrusion Arch (beta titanium, preactivated 30° V-bends)
  • Nickel-free chairside wires named Betaflex Titanium and BT3
  • Auxiliaries: Memory Titanol® molar-uprighting springs, leveling springs
  • Lab: FORESTANIT® Super and FORESTANIT® Ni-Free laboratory wires
  • Older GLAM flyers mention BioCosmetic® tooth-colored wires. There was no live BioCosmetic product page on the English hub at research time—treat that name as historic until the current catalog shows it.

Skeletal anchorage and Class II / III

  • OrthoEasy® — titanium mini-screws. Pin (bracket-style head, .022″ × .025″ cross slot, Ø 1.7 mm, lengths 6/8/10/12 mm) and Pal / Pal Long Neck 1.7 and 2.3 (internal-thread head for abutments). Color-coded lengths; ScanBuddy for capturing Pal position on an intraoral scan. Vendor “16 stars” line is marketing for the variant set.
  • SARA® (Sabbagh Advanced Repositioning Appliance) — fixed telescopic Class II appliance (collaboration with Dr. Sabbagh); mesial insertion in a headgear tube; interchangeable 3 N / 4 N outer spring; positioned as Herbst-plus-Jasper-Jumper mechanics without lab work. “Avoid extractions, surgery, or headgear” is vendor language, not a guarantee.
  • Also named on the Class II / III hub: Easy-Fit® Jumper, Functional Mandibular Advancer (FMA), mandibular protrusion device, bite-jumping screw, Sander II (VDP) and Class III double-plate (RDP) expansion screws.
  • Frog II molar distalizer (with Dr. Kevin Walde), anchored on OrthoEasy screws—on the molar-distalization page.

Expansion, thermoforming, TMD

  • Expansion screws — the 1974 ortho origin product; still a homepage category, including a titanium screw on the nickel-allergy page.
  • TRACK® thermoforming foils (and TRACK units) for retainers, aligners, IDB trays, nightguards, bleaching trays; some sheets peel an insulating layer after forming.
  • AquaSplint® Thermo and AquaSplint® Mini — prefabricated hydrostatic TMD / CMD splints (self-adjusting; no lab, per vendor).

Digital / aligners

  • FAS® Aligner System — Forestadent’s clinician-submitted aligner brand. Vendor differentiation: planning on the Arc of Closure (hinge axis) for vertical control; cases planned by orthodontists “without any artificial intelligence”; STOP and GO® checkpoint; hybrid with OrthoEasy. “Significantly fewer aligners,” “minimal refinements,” and TMJ-harmony claims are manufacturer positioning. Packages are described only as moderate / complex / very complex on the public page—confirm live package names in the FAS portal.
  • FAS® Kids — early / mixed-dentition splint system with STOP and WAIT around eruption; optional Smart Hooks, Digital Tracks, lingual stops, eruption splints, occlusal blocks. Prevention and habit-control language is vendor indication copy, not an outcome promise.
  • 3D-Printing-World — UNIZ printers, LuxCreo 4D Aligner™ / ActiveMemory™, and SIMPLEX filament model printing. These read as partner systems Forestadent resells, not in-house hardware on the same footing as BioQuick. Confirm who invoices and who supports the printer.

Adhesives (not Forestadent-made)

The adhesives category is Reliance Orthodontic Products sold through Forestadent: GoTo, Assure Plus, T.K.O., LightBond, Flow Tain, Ultra Band-Lok. Same Reliance house already covered on this site’s Reliance page. Do not write POs as if Forestadent compounded the paste.

Not assumed current from older catalogs

Mini-Mono® and earlier Quick® / BioQuick® generations show up in leftover PDFs and 2010–2021 catalogs. They are not tiles on the live English brackets hub (BioQuick, TruKlear, QuicKlear III, GLAM, Sprint II, Mini Sprint II, Micro Sprint, 2D Lingual, BioPassive). Confirm before restocking a historic SKU.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Real specialty breadth: vestibular SL and twin, ceramic SL and twin, a serious lingual line, wires, TADs, expansion, Class II telescopics, and a Forestadent-branded aligner—without pretending to be Align or DS.
  • Clear current bracket map on the English hub; BioQuick 5 vs BioPassive marking is documented; ceramic split (QuicKlear III vs metal-free TruKlear vs ligated GLAM) is easier to shop than a single “aesthetic” dump bin.
  • Nickel-allergy page is unusually specific (MIM ≤ 0.1% Sprint/Comfort Line, Betaflex/BT3, FORESTANIT Ni-Free, titanium expansion screw). Useful as a starting map if you still verify lots and IFUs.
  • U.S. path is a named subsidiary with a published catalog (Catalogue 9 US, created Dec 2024 / updated Feb 2025 on the download page) rather than “ask a European dealer.”
  • Legal name, Pforzheim street address, and 1907 / 1974 timeline are on Forestadent’s own About and History pages.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Naming leftovers will trip purchasing. Foresta (watches), Quick vs BioQuick 3 vs BioQuick 5, Mini-Mono, BioCosmetic, forestadentusa.com, and three different St. Louis-area street addresses all still exist in PDFs. The live hub is forestadent.com/en.
  • FAS® is a planning philosophy plus a portal, not a box of trays you grab like TRACK sheets. “No AI,” Arc of Closure, and fewer-aligner claims are vendor positioning; country availability of FAS vs FAS Kids is not spelled out on the public U.S. catalog landing page—ask USA before promising it to patients.
  • Adhesives and several 3D-print SKUs are other companies’ products in a Forestadent catalog. Support, IFU, and recall responsibility may not sit in Pforzheim.
  • Deep SKU surface (prescriptions × slot × Sprint vs BioQuick vs ceramic × auxiliaries) is a formulary project for a startup office. This is not a three-SKU house.
  • Superlatives (“flattest lingual,” “smallest twin,” “highest precision,” “uncompromising”) are campaign copy. Micro Sprint’s design awards are cited by the manufacturer; they are not clinical outcome data.
  • SARA / FAS / OrthoEasy “shorten treatment,” “no cooperation,” and “avoid surgery” lines are the same class of claim every appliance vendor prints. Use IFUs and your own mechanics.

Buying notes

  • Start at forestadent.com/en and the Products & Solutions index. U.S. offices: Contact → FORESTADENT USA and Catalogue 9 US (PDF). Do not email the brand from this site; this page is informational only.
  • U.S. phones on the live Contact page: 1-314-878-5985 and 1-800-721-4940; orders@forestadentusa.com. Canada is dealer-routed (Swiss NF Metals, Orthodontic Supply of Canada) on that same page.
  • Legal manufacturer for European paper: FORESTADENT Bernhard Förster GmbH, Pforzheim. U.S. POs go to the subsidiary, not “Foresta” or a historic Weldon Parkway remit.
  • Confirm slot (.018 / .022), prescription (MB 5.0 vs classic MBT vs Roth vs Trevisi vs FACE vs Roncone PDS vs Ricketts vs edgewise), and whether you want BioQuick 5 or an older Quick remainder before you hit reorder on a favorite SKU.
  • FAS and FAS Kids need portal registration. Hybrid with OrthoEasy is a Forestadent-described option, not automatic.
  • Product availability varies by market. Returns, reprocessing, and material lists live under the site’s QM Documents menu.

Bottom line

Forestadent is a Pforzheim specialty manufacturer: 1907 as Förster, 1974 as Forestadent, still family-run, still making brackets and screws in the Black Forest, with a St. Louis subsidiary for North America. Shop the live hub names (BioQuick 5, BioPassive, Sprint II / Mini / Micro, QuicKlear III, TruKlear, GLAM, 2D Lingual, OrthoEasy, SARA, FAS)—not Foresta watches, not 2010 BioQuick, not Mini-Mono leftovers. Treat FAS as an optional digital lane on top of a real fixed-appliance catalog.

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.
