Leone

Italian specialty manufacturer (Leone S.p.A.) of expansion screws, RPE/Leaf Expander appliances, brackets, TADs, and lab products—plus a separate implant line—based in Sesto Fiorentino (Florence) since 1934.

HQ: Sesto Fiorentino (Florence), Italy · Founded 1934 · Researched 2026-09-15 · Official site

Overview

Leone is the public brand of Leone S.p.A., an Italian dental manufacturer headquartered at Via P. a Quaracchi, 50, 50019 Sesto Fiorentino, Firenze (Florence), Italy. The live English hub is leone.it/english. Site chrome and footers also use www.leone.it; both resolve to the same Leone S.p.A. property. Export contact on product PDFs: export@leone.it.

How the names stack (verified, not a rebrand story):

  • Leone S.p.A. — current legal manufacturer on the company page, download footers, and VAT/registry block (P. IVA 01686960483).
  • Leone / Leone Orthodontics and Implantology — how the English home titles the business. Orthodontics and implantology are parallel divisions on the same corporate site—not a rename away from “Leone Orthodontics.”
  • 1934 — Official Company page: Mario Pozzi begins manufacturing dental products in Florence and adopts the Florentine Marzocco lion-head trademark. Alessandro Pozzi takes over after Mario’s death in 1961; current CEO named on the same page is Elena (daughter), active from the late 1990s.
  • 1993 — Company page: member of the O.M.A. (Orthodontic Manufacturers Association).
  • 2001 — Company page: Leone expands into the Leone Implant System (implantology line; separate from the ortho formulary core).
  • 2017 — Company page: acquisition of U.S. distributor LeoneAmerica and strengthening of French associate Odontec. LeoneAmerica’s own About page says it has sold the Leone line since 1971, with the newer LeoneAmerica Dental Products, Inc. focused on USA / Canada / Puerto Rico.

Official Company copy: manufacturing facilities west of Florence (~10,000 m², ~125 employees), ISO 9001 / 13485, in-house Istituto Studi Odontoiatrici (ISO) training center, and Marco Pozzi biotechnology research center. Treat headcount and square-meter figures as company statements.

U.S. / Canada / Puerto Rico path: LeoneAmerica Dental Products, Inc., 1400 Graves Ave. Unit A, Oxnard, CA 93030; phone 805-487-9860; info@leoneamerica.com. Leone’s North America distributors page lists LeoneAmerica as the ortho distributor and a separate Implant Logistics Inc. for implantology only. Order through LeoneAmerica / local dealers; country availability varies.

This is an Italian specialty house for expanders, appliances, brackets, and lab—with a real implant division and sleep/MAD kits on the same corporate site. For Ortho Index, the chairside and lab core is expansion (RPE / Leaf / POP), brackets, bands/tubes, wires, TADs, and removable-appliance hardware.

Who it’s for

  • Specialists and labs that want a deep Italian expander and appliance catalog—classic RPE, Leaf Expander® / Leaf Self Expander®, POP® screws, CAD-CAM / TAD-assisted expanders—not only a bracket box
  • Offices that still build removable appliances and care that standard, special-purpose, and POP expansion screws, acrylic workflow accessories, and chairside brackets come from one manufacturer
  • Teams evaluating NiTi leaf-spring expansion (Leaf series) or MARPE / hybrid TAD expanders without switching houses for the screw body
  • Doctors shopping metal and ceramic self-ligating (Interactive SL, F1000Plus, AquaSL), Idea-L lingual 2D setups, or conventionally ligated metal twins from the same catalog
  • Groups that will order North American ortho through LeoneAmerica (Oxnard, CA) and European / rest-of-world through Leone S.p.A. or local distributors listed on leone.it
  • Practices that may also use Leone MAD / anti-snoring kits or the implant line—and who will keep those invoices separate from the ortho formulary

Notable products

Expansion screws and RPE (lab / appliance core)

  • Standard expansion screws — broad biomedical stainless line for removable appliances (upper, universal, micro/sectional, etc.); catalog positions Leone screws as a long-running Italian production story.
  • POP® (Perfect Orthodontic Performance) — stainless + biomedical techno-polymer expansion screws; male screw isolated from acrylic; self-centering rectangular guides; multiple colors; Universal / Upper / Micro sectional variants on LeoneAmerica and catalog Sez. A.
  • Rapid palatal expanders (RPE) — classic A0620 “Rapid Expander” family (laser-welded arms, laser-etched limit/arrow/lot), pre-bent-arm versions, telescopic-guide RPEs, micro / Easy Access, orthogonal-arm anatomical expanders, Ragno fan-type / mini RPE, Baby RPE, lower-arch expanders.
  • Palatal expander for TADs / MARPE path — strengthened RPE with adaptable TAD attachments for hybrid (teeth + TADs) or bone-borne designs; catalog also lists CAD-CAM rapid / anatomical expanders with squared slots for laser-melted frameworks designed in 3D Leone Designer.
  • Leaf Expander® Series — NiTi MEMORIA® leaf-spring maxillary expanders (collaboration credited to Dr. Claudio Lanteri and Mr. Filippo Francolini). Leaf Expander® (office re-activation when springs separate); Leaf Self Expander® (no home or in-office re-activation); Anatomical / Mini CAD-CAM variants; typical force models 450 g / 900 g (and 600 g on some Anatomical / Mini Self models) with 6 mm / 9 mm expansion capacities depending on SKU. Dedicated site: leafexpander.com. “No home activation,” visit-reduction, and comfort claims are vendor positioning—follow the current IFU/protocol.
  • Hybrid Mode Expander — named on the Italian orthodontics novelty list alongside Mini Hybrid / Leaf Anatomical updates; confirm live English SKU naming and indication with the current catalog before stocking.
  • Special-purpose screws still in Sez. A: three-directional / Bertoni-style, fan-type / Pagani, Twin Wing Class II kit, First Class and Fast Back molar distalizers, micro spring screws, and related keys/instruments.

Brackets and tubes

  • F1000Plus — passive self-ligating metal D.B. brackets; live news page lists ROTH / MBT / DAMON .022 assortments. Leone’s Interactive SL brochure disclaimer applies in spirit across prescription-named kits: Leone does not claim doctor/school endorsement or identical copies of other systems—treat “DAMON System” as a Leone assortment label, not Ormco affiliation.
  • Interactive SL (also styled InterActive) — interactive self-ligating metal D.B. line; MIM body, spring clip, Roth and MBT-style .022 kits on the LeoneAmerica PDF.
  • AquaSL — interactive self-ligating ceramic (CIM polycrystalline, rhodium-plated clip surface); Roth and MBT-style .022 kits; NiTi clip with passive / interactive / active phases described on the AquaSL PDF.
  • Ghiaccio — aesthetic bracket name still listed on Leone’s download index (“Orthodontic brackets Ghiaccio”) and as a demonstration model code in the 2024 lab/diagnostic PDF; distributor and third-party listings describe monocrystalline sapphire kits. Confirm whether your market’s current purchase SKU is Ghiaccio vs Aqua / AquaSL before reordering from an old flyer.
  • Idea-L — D.B. lingual brackets (~1.4 mm thickness) for 2D round-wire biomechanics with Slide™ ligatures; jigs for positioning; no full lab setup required per vendor. Companion Memoria® / Beta Memoria® lingual arches on the same flyer.
  • Conventionally ligated metal twins, weldable brackets, MIM® buccal tubes (incl. convertible), CALIBRA® / WEB bands with free prewelding/solder-brazing services called out in the tubes-and-brackets catalog section.

Wires, elastics, instruments, TADs

  • Orthodontics hub catalogue chapters: wire and wire forms, elastics and ligatures, intra-/extraoral, TADs and orthodontic mini implants, pliers and instruments, office/lab supplies, diagnostic and lab equipment.
  • Confirm named TAD SKUs (e.g. Italian novelty TAD PA Bifasico) and current mini-implant diameters/lengths on the live TAD section or with your rep—do not invent a single “Leone TAD” SKU from marketing alone.

Digital / lab software

  • 3D Leone Designer — Leone’s driven CAD workflow for custom expander frameworks coupled to CAD-CAM expander bodies (library of Leone CAD-CAM expanders; STL → selective laser melting / laser weld to screw). Featured on LeoneAmerica home and expansion catalog.

Beyond the brackets formulary (company breadth)

  • Leone Implant System (since 2001) — implantology; North America implant distribution is not LeoneAmerica on the official distributors page (Implant Logistics listed separately).
  • MAD / anti-snoring kits (Forward!, Telescopic Advancer / Dual, etc.) on Sez. A and LeoneAmerica sleep pages.
  • OK, Orthodontics for Kids — LeoneAmerica pedodontic / early-ortho positioning lane.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Genuine Italian specialty depth on expanders and appliances: standard + POP removable screws, a very wide RPE map, Leaf NiTi leaf-spring series, TAD/MARPE and CAD-CAM expanders, distalizers, and MAD kits—stronger on this lane than many bracket-first mega-vendors.
  • Bracket lane is real, not a token SKU: passive metal F1000Plus, interactive metal Interactive SL, ceramic AquaSL, lingual Idea-L, plus conventional metal and MIM tubes/bands with documented prewelding services.
  • Clear legal identity (Leone S.p.A.), street address in Sesto Fiorentino, founding year 1934, and OMA membership are on Leone’s own Company page—no invented rename required.
  • Documented North America ortho path via LeoneAmerica (Oxnard) with published phone and sole-distributor claim for USA / Canada / Puerto Rico ortho products.
  • Leaf Expander and RPE CAD-CAM / 3D Leone Designer give practices a concrete digital-lab story for expansion without abandoning fixed appliances.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Naming leftovers will trip purchasing. Leaf Expander vs Leaf Self Expander vs Anatomical Mini vs CAD-CAM codes; Interactive SL vs F1000Plus (interactive vs passive); Aqua vs AquaSL vs Ghiaccio demo/SKU confusion; “DAMON System” assortment labels that are not Ormco products; LeoneAmerica since-1971 heritage vs 2017 acquisition wording. Shop the live download index and current catalogue—not a 2015 PDF alone.
  • Catalog surface area is huge (expansion SKUs × brackets × wires × TADs × MAD × implants). Startup offices need formulary discipline or they will overstock lab hardware they never use.
  • Implantology and sleep/MAD are real Leone businesses—do not confuse implant mini-implants with ortho TADs, or assume LeoneAmerica ships every European implant SKU.
  • Leaf “no activation,” visit cuts, “painless,” F1000Plus chair-time reduction, and “best-selling RPE” lines are manufacturer positioning. Force values (450/900 g, etc.) are labeled approximate on product tables—verify IFU and your own activation protocol.
  • Country availability of Leaf CAD-CAM, 3D Leone Designer, specific TAD expanders, and aesthetic bracket generations is not uniform; U.S. offices should confirm with LeoneAmerica before promising a European novelty SKU to patients.
