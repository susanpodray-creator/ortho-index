Vatech

Publicly listed Korean dental X-ray maker (Vatech Co., Ltd., Hwaseong; KOSDAQ 043150) behind the Green X CBCT family, PaX-i pan/ceph units, A9 and Smart Plus entry CBCTs, HD intraoral sensors, EzRay Air generators, and Ez3D-i / EzDent-i / Clever One software. U.S. arm: Vatech America (Teaneck, NJ).

HQ: Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea · Founded 1992 · Researched 2026-10-13 · Official site

Overview

Vatech is a South Korean dental X-ray imaging company. The legal entity is Vatech Co., Ltd. (Korean filings also use the English name Value Added Technology Co., Ltd.), headquartered at 13 Samsung 1-ro 2-gil, Hwaseong-si, Gyeonggi-do, Korea. The global site is vatech.com. In the United States, the company sells and supports through its subsidiary Vatech America Inc., based in Teaneck, New Jersey (vatechamerica.com).

Ownership and structure, from official sources:

  • Vatech is publicly traded on KOSDAQ (ticker 043150). Its own history page dates the listing to September 2006.
  • Vatech belongs to Vatech Networks, a group of medical-systems affiliates. The group describes Vatech Ewoo Holdings as its holding company. Affiliates include Rayence (X-ray detectors), Ewoosoft (dental imaging software such as EzDent-i, Ez3D-i, and EzOrtho), and Vatech ENG.
  • A 2023 article Vatech republished on its own site says the detectors come from Rayence and the column modules from Vatech ENG. Vatech Networks says it builds core components (generators, detectors, software) in-house or with affiliates.

Founding: Vatech’s history page says the company was set up in April 1992 as Vatech System Corporation and took the Vatech name in March 2002, when it entered dental digital X-ray. Vatech’s own retelling says it started in industrial inspection equipment, and that the dental business model really dates from around 2002. This page uses 1992 for founded, the year Vatech itself lists.

Milestones Vatech highlights:

  • Its first digital panoramic unit in Korea (PaX-300, 2003).
  • What it calls the world’s first “3-in-1” CT + panoramic + cephalometric system (Picasso-Trio, 2005).
  • The low-dose “Green” CT line, starting with PaX-i3D Green in 2013.
  • A carbon-nanotube portable intraoral X-ray (EzRay Air P, 2020).
  • In January 2026, Vatech reported its 100,000th dental digital X-ray system produced (a Green X 21).

Scale, in Vatech’s words:

  • Vatech reported Q2 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 121.4 billion, with 3D products at 58.3% of product revenue.
  • It lists 18 overseas corporations and more than 200 overseas agencies.
  • Its About page claims several “No. 1” market positions, including 3D X-ray market share in the U.S. These are Vatech’s own claims, based on its 2022 business report. We have not verified them independently.
  • In June 2026, Vatech said its whole dental imaging portfolio was certified under EU MDR. That matters for Europe, not for U.S. clearance.

What this page covers: the parts of Vatech an orthodontic office is most likely to evaluate: CBCT and pan/ceph units, intraoral sensors and generators, and imaging software, as listed on Vatech America’s current U.S. product pages. It is informational, not a purchasing recommendation. Every product page says specifications are subject to change without notice.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic offices that want pan + ceph in one unit, with a choice of scanning ceph (PaX-i) or Vatech’s faster Rapid Ceph (PaX-i Plus RC, A9 RC, Smart Plus RC).
  • Practices that want CBCT + pan + ceph in one footprint. Vatech has built its brand on this “3-in-1” idea since 2005. Most current Green X models list ceph as an option.
  • Offices weighing a large-FOV CBCT for full-skull or orthognathic work. Green X 21 lists volumes up to 21 × 19 cm, and Green X (18) up to 18 × 15 cm.
  • Practices that want an entry-level 3D path (A9 with an 8 × 8 cm volume, or Smart Plus) and may plan to trade up later. Vatech America runs buyback and trade-in programs aimed at that.
  • Offices standardizing on one vendor for extraoral and intraoral X-ray (HD Sensor, EzRay Air W wall-mounted or EzRay Air P portable), all viewed in EzDent-i, Ez3D-i, or Clever One.
  • Windows-based offices. Vatech’s published workstation requirements for Ez3D-i and Clever One are Windows-only and call for NVIDIA graphics for 3D.

Notable products

Names and specs below come from Vatech America product pages, the 2026 U.S. brochure, and vatech.com, checked October 13, 2026. FDA 510(k) references come from FDA’s public 510(k) data (via openFDA) and show that a device was cleared, not how well it performs. Speed, dose, and image-quality language is Vatech’s marketing, not independent testing. Confirm exact model numbers, options, and software licenses on any written quote.

CBCT / 3D (most include pan; ceph optional)

  • Green X 21 (PHT-90CHO): Vatech’s current flagship. It offers CT + pano + ceph + face scan + model scan. The FOV options run from 4 × 4 up to 21 × 19 cm, with an Endo mode at 0.05 mm voxel size.
    • Vatech says one scan can give a CT plus an auto-generated pano and ceph (“One Scan, Three Images”). It lists a 0.9 s One Shot ceph.
    • FDA 510(k) K243081 (decision Feb 14, 2025). Vatech says the U.S. launch came in 2025.
    • Published footprint with ceph is about 75.3” L × 52.9” W × 91.2” H, at roughly 419 lb without the base.
  • Green X (Green X 18) (PHT-75CHS): CT + pano + optional ceph + model scan, with FOVs from 4 × 4 up to 18 × 15 cm. Listed ceph scan times are 1.9 s or 4.9 s. FDA 510(k) K210329 (Feb 2021).
  • Green X 12 (PHT-75CHS): same platform with FOVs up to 12 × 8.5 cm, auto pano from the CT scan, and an optional ceph. FDA 510(k) K231796 (Jul 2023). The FDA records also list a Green X 12 SE (K243088, Feb 2025) and a Green X 12 VE / Green X VE (K260093, Mar 2026). Vatech’s June 2026 EU MDR release names a Green X 12 Plus and Green X 12 SE as 2026 models. Check which variant you are quoted.
  • Green X Plus (PHT-70CHS): FOVs from 4 × 4 up to 18 × 10 cm, auto pano, model scan, and an optional ceph (16-bit ceph).
  • Smart Plus (PHT-35LHS): compact CBCT with an “arch-shaped” volume (up to 10 × 8.5 cm, which Vatech calls anatomically 12 × 9). It includes auto pano and model scan. The Smart Plus RC adds ceph.
  • A9 (PHT-30CSS): entry 3D unit with an 8 × 8 cm FOV, front entry, a small footprint, and “Magic Pan” multi-layer panoramic. The A9 RC adds Rapid Ceph. FDA 510(k) K203797 (Jan 2021). It is the lightest Vatech CBCT listed, at about 239 lb without the base and without ceph.
  • Every listed unit needs about 3 extra inches behind it for the wall-mount bracket, unless it is installed on a base.

Panoramic / cephalometric (2D)

  • PaX-i (PCH-2500): pan + scan ceph, with separate dedicated pano and ceph sensors. Ceph image sizes are lateral 21 × 23 cm (12.9 s) and full lateral 27 × 23 cm (16.9 s). Ceph views include LAT, PA, SMV, Waters, and carpus. It works standing or with a wheelchair. FDA 510(k) records for PCH-2500 / PaX-i date to 2012–2013.
  • PaX-i Plus RC (PCH-30CS): pan + Rapid Ceph, listed at 1.9 s or 3.9 s. Pano runs 10.4 s normal or 14 s HD. Footprint with ceph is about 76” L × 47” W × 90.5” H, at 264.5 lb without the base. FDA 510(k) K170731 covers PaX-i Plus and PaX-i Insight.

Intraoral imaging

  • HD Sensor: CMOS sensor in sizes 1.0, 1.5, and 2.0. Vatech lists a 14.8 µm pixel, a theoretical 33.78 lp/mm, a 4.8 mm thickness, a direct USB 3.0 connection with no control box, and seven contrast filters.
  • EzRay Air W: wall-mounted carbon-nanotube intraoral X-ray. Listed at 65 kVp and 3.0 mA, 0.4 mm focal spot, with arm lengths of 62”, 68”, or 80”.
  • EzRay Air P: portable carbon-nanotube intraoral X-ray, 4.7 lb, with internal and external backscatter shielding. FDA 510(k) K200182 (Feb 2020). Rules for handheld X-ray use vary by state, so confirm registration and operator rules before buying.
  • EzCam: USB intraoral camera with autofocus.
  • No intraoral scanner appears in Vatech America’s current product menu. A 2023 article Vatech republished on its own site also noted that Vatech is hard to find in scanners, printers, and milling.

Software

  • EzDent-i (Ewoosoft): 2D imaging and consultation hub. It includes Vatech’s Virtual Consultation Tool, with more than 200 patient-education animations. The FDA 510(k) record for EzDent-i / E2 / Prora View / Smart M Viewer v3.5 is K241114 (Jul 2024).
  • Ez3D-i (Ewoosoft): 3D viewer with VR rendering modes, 3D panoramic navigation, implant simulation, and 2-click airway analysis (airway volume and minimum axial area). It needs Windows 10 Pro 64-bit or later and an NVIDIA GTX 1660 / RTX 2060-class GPU or better. Vatech says AMD, Intel, and integrated graphics are not supported.
  • Clever One (Ewoosoft): launched in the U.S. in August 2025. It puts pano, CBCT, intraoral, facial, and STL scan data in one viewer. Vatech says it integrates Pearl’s AI lesion detection on panoramic images, one-click 3D tooth and bone segmentation, image alignment of CBCT, intraoral scans, and face scans, and EzCloud sync. FDA 510(k) K250005 (May 2025). It runs on Windows 11 clients, with a separate server PC.
  • Ortho-specific analysis: Ewoosoft’s EzOrtho has FDA 510(k) records (most recently v1.3, K220003, Feb 2022). Vatech’s global site also lists OrthoVision (2D ceph digitizing and analysis) and DAVIS AI auto-tracing for ortho planning; the Korean regulator certified DAVIS. None of these appears in Vatech America’s current product menu, so confirm U.S. availability and licensing before you count on them.
  • Integration: Vatech America’s FAQ includes a hardware and software integration SDK and a TWAIN guide. Confirm the bridge to your practice-management, ceph, or aligner software before purchase.

Service and purchase programs (U.S.)

  • Vatech America technical service is reachable by phone and email, Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–8 p.m. ET. Remote support runs through TeamViewer, and there is a self-help FAQ and an education portal. Vatech says its service team does not handle how-to questions, which go through education and training.
  • Vatech Assurance programs, in Vatech’s words:
    • Buyback: within five years of installation, Vatech and the supporting dealer apply 100% of the original price of a 2D unit toward an eligible new 3D Green model.
    • Trade-in/trade-up: a trade-in credit for 3D units.
    • Freedom Program: a 3-year path to a new device, with a buyout or return option.
  • Read the actual terms in writing. The buyback language names a “supporting dealer,” which tells you sales run through dealers.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Imaging is the whole business. Vatech is an X-ray company first. CBCT, pan/ceph, sensors, generators, and their software come from one group, and the detectors and software come from its own affiliates (Rayence, Ewoosoft).
  • Real ceph choices. Scan ceph (PaX-i), Rapid Ceph (PaX-i Plus RC, A9 RC, Smart Plus RC), optional ceph on Green X models, and a 0.9 s One Shot ceph on Green X 21 give ortho offices several price and speed tiers.
  • 3-in-1 footprint. One unit for CBCT + pan + ceph can save a second X-ray room. Vatech has built that design since 2005.
  • Wide FOV ladder. Volumes run from 8 × 8 cm (A9) up to 21 × 19 cm (Green X 21), so an office can match volume size to its case mix.
  • Clear regulatory trail. FDA 510(k) records exist for the main current CBCTs (Green X 21, Green X 12, Green X 18, A9), the PaX-i and PaX-i Plus pan/ceph units, EzRay Air, and Ewoosoft’s EzDent-i, Ez3D-i, Clever One, and EzOrtho.
  • Public company with published results. KOSDAQ listing and quarterly results offer more transparency than a private vendor.
  • Upgrade paths are published. Buyback, trade-up, and 3-year Freedom programs are spelled out on the U.S. site. They still need contract-level checking.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Dose and image-quality claims are Vatech’s. The “Green low dose,” “No. 1” market-share, and image-quality statements come from Vatech. Ask for the dose data for the exact protocols you would use (for example, a ceph or a full-skull FOV on a teen) and compare against your current unit.
  • Many similar model names. Green X, Green X 12, 12 SE, 12 VE, 12 Plus, Green X Plus, Green X 21, plus RC and SC variants. Match the quote to the exact model number (PHT-/PCH-) and confirm whether ceph is included.
  • Big, heavy units. Green X-family units with ceph are about 75” long, about 91” tall, and roughly 375–540 lb depending on the model and base. Confirm room size, ceiling height, wall reinforcement or base mount, and shielding with your installer.
  • Windows and NVIDIA required for 3D. Ez3D-i and Clever One list Windows-only requirements and NVIDIA GPUs. A Mac-centered office will need dedicated Windows workstations.
  • No intraoral scanner in the U.S. lineup. If digital impressions matter, you will be pairing Vatech imaging with another vendor’s scanner. Test the STL and DICOM handoffs.
  • Ortho analysis software is unclear in the U.S. OrthoVision, EzOrtho, and DAVIS auto-tracing are on the global or Ewoosoft side, but not on Vatech America’s product menu. Many ortho offices will still run a separate ceph and planning program, so verify export and integration.
  • Dealer-driven sales and service terms. Pricing is not public, and the U.S. programs involve a “supporting dealer.” Get installation, training, warranty, and service response terms in writing from both the dealer and Vatech America.
  • Handheld X-ray rules. EzRay Air P is portable, but state rules on handheld units differ. Confirm before purchase.

Evaluation notes

  • Decide the record set first: pan + ceph only, entry CBCT (A9 or Smart Plus), mid-FOV (Green X 12 or Green X Plus), or large-FOV (Green X 18 or Green X 21).
  • For ceph, compare scan ceph vs Rapid Ceph vs One Shot: ask for exposure time, image size (lateral vs full lateral), and the dose for each.
  • Confirm the exact model number and that the FDA 510(k) on the quote matches it, along with the FOV, ceph, face-scan, and model-scan options.
  • Ask which software ships: EzDent-i, Ez3D-i, or Clever One. Ask whether the AI features (Pearl lesion detection, segmentation) and EzCloud are included or licensed separately, and what renewals cost.
  • Test DICOM export into your ceph or planning software, and the bridge to your practice-management system (SDK or TWAIN), before go-live.
  • Plan the workstations: Windows 11 clients, a server PC for Clever One, and NVIDIA GPUs for 3D.
  • Get the Vatech Assurance terms (buyback, trade-up, Freedom) and the service response commitments in writing.

Bottom line

Vatech is a KOSDAQ-listed Korean company focused on dental X-ray. Its U.S. lineup covers pan/ceph (PaX-i, PaX-i Plus RC), CBCT from an entry 8 × 8 cm A9 up to the 21 × 19 cm Green X 21, HD sensors, carbon-nanotube intraoral generators, and Ewoosoft-built software (EzDent-i, Ez3D-i, Clever One). For orthodontics, the draws are the ceph options and the 3-in-1 footprint. The things to check are the exact model and option set, dose data for your own protocols, Windows/NVIDIA workstation needs, integration with your ceph and practice software, and dealer service terms.
