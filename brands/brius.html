Brius Technologies

Carrollton, Texas maker of the Brava / Brava+ Independent Movers system — custom lingual NiTi arms on a shared anchorage base that program simultaneous, per-tooth movement without a continuous archwire.

HQ: Carrollton, Texas, USA · Founded 2017 · Researched 2026-09-28 · Official site

Overview

Brius Technologies (often styled BRIUS®) builds Brava® — marketed as Independent Movers®, a custom lingual orthodontic appliance that is neither a conventional continuous-wire braces system nor a clear aligner. Public materials describe a rigid anchorage base that sits near the palate or lingual vestibule, with flexible nickel-titanium (NiTi) arms that connect independently to each tooth through lingual brackets. Forces are programmed per tooth from a digital plan rather than transmitted along a shared archwire between neighbors.

The live consumer and provider brand home is bravabraces.com (company site name Brius; press and doctor tools also cite brius.com and the Brius Planner at planner.brius.com). Legal footer language uses Brius Technologies Inc, 2611 Westgrove Dr Ste 109, Carrollton, TX 75006. Series B company copy (May 2025) says the firm was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in the Dallas area; the mailing address and About page place operations in Carrollton. Leadership listed on the About page (reviewed September 28, 2026) includes Lea Ellermeier (CEO) and Ruedger Rubbert (CTO). Trade press and company-provided Orthodontic Practice profiles credit orthodontist-physicist Mehdi Peikar, DDS, PhD as inventor / cofounder and (in 2022 Orthodontic Products coverage) chief medical officer.

Commercial milestones on the company’s own newsroom: Brava market launch materials dated July 6, 2022; Brava+ announced February 13, 2024 (self-engaging brackets and soft-coated anchorage base; cases submitted on or after March 4, 2024 fabricated exclusively on Brava+ per that release); $19.5 million initial Series B close announced May 6, 2025 (led by View Ventures, with Sonder Capital, Invus, SUM Ventures, and Tech Wildcatters named).

This brief is for practices comparing aesthetic fixed alternatives, lingual workflows, and custom digital appliances. It is not patient marketing, a protocol, or medical advice. Case selection, bonding, and finishing sit with the treating doctor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists evaluating a compliance-free aesthetic option for patients who want appliances hidden behind the teeth but are a poor fit for aligner wear schedules
  • Offices already comfortable with intraoral scan → cloud plan → approve → custom ship → IDB workflows who want that model for a lingual independent-mover appliance rather than buccal braces or clear trays
  • Practices interested in fewer planned adjustment visits and non-sliding, pre-programmed mechanics (vendor positioning — confirm visit cadence in your own protocols)
  • Doctors open to a certified-provider / training relationship (2022 Orthodontic Products interview described in-office clinical training for early bondings, webinars, and study clubs)

It is a weaker shortlist if you only want stock buccal twin/SL inventory with same-day start, if you refuse lab-dependent custom appliances, if lingual speech/adaptation coaching is not in your chairside culture, or if your case mix and state rules make a new certified system impractical. Confirm eligibility, territory, pricing, and training with Brius — not from this page.

Notable products

Names below match Brius’s public home, About, provider, how-it-works, launch, and Brava+ pages plus 2022–2025 trade coverage reviewed September 28, 2026. Hardware, software, turnaround, and training change; verify current doctor materials before you brief patients or sign a contract.

Brava / Brava+ Independent Movers

  • Brava® Independent Movers® — custom lingual appliance: anchorage base + pre-programmed flexible NiTi arms engaging each tooth. Company framing: simultaneous, independent tooth movement in six degrees of freedom; no continuous wire between adjacent teeth (easier flossing in patient copy). Arms and base described as U.S.-made shape-memory NiTi in 2022 launch materials.
  • Brava+ System (announced February 13, 2024) — next-generation stack with what the company calls the world’s first self-engaging Brava brackets (spring-lock to resist disengagement), rounded bracket contours, and a soft-coated anchorage base for tongue comfort. From the release date forward, new cases were to be designed and fabricated exclusively on Brava+.
  • Anchorage base geometry — provider page places the base about 1 mm above soft tissue; earlier Orthodontic Practice technical profile described the stabilizing base designed to lie about 0.5 mm from palate or lingual vestibule. Treat those as vendor/technical descriptions that may evolve — confirm IFU language with Brius.

Planning, bonding, and practice tools

  • Brius Planner — digital treatment-planning software (linked from the provider nav as planner.brius.com). Orthodontic Products (2022) described scan upload, a virtual plan in about 1–2 days, optional CBCT for root/bone context, doctor approval, then manufacture. Orthodontic Practice / company profile materials also note ethnicity, age, gender, and root morphology as planning inputs in product-profile copy — confirm live planner features with Brius.
  • Indirect bonding (IDB) trays — provider page: trays come pre-loaded with self-engaging brackets; trays can be modified for mid-treatment rebonds. Bonding appointment framed as about one hour on patient how-it-works copy.
  • Clinical support kit — provider page cites a printed treatment plan, clinical/treatment guides, and patient care instructions with each case; training and webinars called out in 2022 trade coverage.
  • Retainers — early Orthodontic Practice technical write-up noted final-scan retainer fabrication after treatment; confirm current retainer SKUs with Brius.

Public workflow (scan → plan → fabricate → bond → monitor)

  1. Provider consult and digital scan (photos / CBCT as requested)
  2. Setup in Brius Planner; doctor reviews and approves
  3. Brius fabricates Independent Movers, brackets, and IDB trays
  4. Bond brackets via IDB; engage arms into self-engaging brackets
  5. Progress with vendor-positioned lower visit frequency; complex cases may need a second appliance per 2022 Orthodontic Products interview

Shipping language from that 2022 interview: 95% of devices within 5 business days of plan approval, all within 7 — treat as then-current ops claims, not a live SLA. Always verify territory, remakes, backup brackets, and current SKU naming with Brius directly. Ortho Index does not publish prices.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Distinct category — lingual, fixed, per-tooth programmed NiTi movers on a shared base; useful shortlist item when patients reject visible braces and aligner compliance
  • Aesthetic + fixed — hidden from the facial view while remaining bonded (company “compliance-free vs aligners” framing)
  • Digital custom workflow with planner, IDB, and case-level support materials in one vendor relationship
  • Brava+ usability story — self-engaging brackets and comfort redesign aimed at classic lingual pain points (chairside engagement, tongue comfort); vendor messaging from the Feb 2024 release
  • Active commercial runway — public Series B close ($19.5M, May 2025) and Japan consumer site (bravabraces.jp linked from the U.S. nav) signal ongoing expansion intent

Cons / watch-outs

  • Lab dependency and learning curve — every start waits on scan quality, plan approval, fabrication, and certified-provider technique; stock buccal braces still bond from the drawer same day
  • Treatment-time and visit claims are vendor / interview figures — patient home page comparison tables cite roughly 6–12 months and 4–6 visits vs longer braces/aligner ranges; 2022 launch copy says “as little as six months”; Orthodontic Products quotes mild-to-moderate finishes with few visits. These are not Ortho Index tests and are not guarantees for your office or any patient
  • Early-system lineage matters for diligence — Orthodontic Practice’s technical profile described early BRIUS setups using Forestadent 2D® lingual brackets; Brava+ later introduced Brius’s own self-engaging brackets. Confirm which hardware your contract and IFUs specify
  • Founding-year and HQ wording vary slightly across sources — company Series B About says founded 2017 / Dallas; some third-party profiles list 2016 / Carrollton. Use current contracts and the Carrollton mailing address for operations, not scrapers
  • Cost and case economics differ from catalog braces or aligner labs — custom NiTi appliance + planner + training is a different P&L; Ortho Index does not publish fees — get a live quote
  • Speech and adaptation — company and trade coverage note an adjustment period (often framed around the first weeks); set expectations the way you would for any lingual fixed appliance

Warm and direct: Brius/Brava is a custom lingual independent-mover manufacturing and planning relationship, not a promise. Ortho Index does not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.

How it compares briefly

Against buccal twin / self-ligating catalogs (American Orthodontics, Ormco, RMO, Forestadent, etc.), Brava trades same-day stock inventory and familiar wire progressions for a hidden, lab-made, non-sliding appliance with fewer planned wire changes. Against classic lingual systems (historical Incognito-style fully customized lingual braces and other lingual bracket/wire setups), Brius’s public differentiator is Independent Movers — arms anchored to a shared base rather than a continuous archwire sliding on lingual anatomy (provider page explicitly contrasts Brava+ with “lingual orthodontic systems of the past”). Against clear aligners (Invisalign, Spark, ClearCorrect, SureSmile Aligners), it stays fixed and compliance-light while keeping an aesthetic story — digital planning is the shared language, not the appliance. Against other custom fixed digital platforms (e.g. LightForce generative buccal braces, SureSmile wires/IDB), Brava sits in the lingual independent-mover niche rather than 3D-printed buccal brackets or robotically bent wires. None of that ranks clinical superiority — match case mix, lab fees, scanner stack, lingual comfort coaching, and staff readiness to your office.

How practices use it

Typical public story: scan and records → Brius Planner setup → doctor approval → receive Brava+/brackets/IDB → bond and engage → monitor with wider visit intervals if hygiene and tracking look good; some doctors still see patients monthly for relationship and hygiene even when little chairside mechanics work is planned (per 2022 Orthodontic Products interview). Mild-to-moderate cases are often framed as one appliance; more complex cases may need a second Brava. Remote monitoring is described as compatible because the appliance is not adjusted like a conventional wire at each visit — still a practice-protocol choice, not a requirement from Ortho Index.

Confirm live products, territory, contracts, IFUs, and clinical protocols with Brius before you buy or brief patients.
