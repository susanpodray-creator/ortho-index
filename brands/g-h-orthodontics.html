G&H Orthodontics

U.S. manufacturer (G&H Wire Company, commercial name G&H Orthodontics) of archwires, brackets, bands, buccal tubes, and elastomerics—G4, M5, TitanMoly, miniPrevail, Agility, Auralis—designed and made in Franklin, Indiana, and registered for sale in 93 countries.

HQ: Franklin, Indiana, USA · Founded 1975 · Researched 2026-08-31 · Official site

Overview

G&H Orthodontics (styled G&H® Orthodontics) is the commercial brand of G&H Wire Company. The live site is ghorthodontics.com. Site footers and trademark lines still list G&H, OrthoForce, OrthoClub, ODP and the G&H logo as registered trademarks of G&H Wire Company. An official EU MDR blog note states plainly: “G&H Orthodontics is the commercial name for G&H Wire Company.” That is a commercial/legal-name stack, not a rename or rebrand story—do not write “formerly G&H Wire” as if the ortho brand died.

How the names stack (verified, not a rebrand story):

  • G&H Wire Company — legal manufacturer. BSI ISO 13485 certificate MD 635659 (PDF hosted on ghorthodontics.com) registers G&H Wire Company, Inc. at 40 Linville Way, Franklin, Indiana 46131, with dba names including Highland Metals Inc., Flexmedics, OrthoClub, G&H Orthodontics, and Orthodontic Design & Production (ODP). Scope: design, manufacture, and distribution of archwires, brackets, bands, tubes, hooks, springs, elastics, chain, ligatures, appliances, and accessories.
  • G&H Orthodontics / G&H® — public commercial brand on the marketing site, shop, and catalog. This is the name to use on Ortho Index and on POs aimed at the brand.
  • OrthoClub — loyalty / program trademark (and a dba on the ISO cert), not a separate product house.
  • ODP (Orthodontic Design & Production) — trademark and regulatory dba on quality docs, not a competing brand.
  • OrthoForce — registered trademark listed in the footer; treat as a G&H mark, not a third-party house.

HQ and plant paper: Contact and site footer mailing address: 40 Linville Way, Franklin, IN 46131 USA. Phones: 1-800-526-1026 (US & Canada) and +1 317-346-6655 (international); orders@ghortho.com. Aspire Johnson County (Aug 2021) covered the move into the Linville Way shell building in Franklin Business Park after the company outgrew an older Graham Road site. Older shop footers and directories still show 2165 Earlywood Drive—use Contact / current footer for remit paper, not a leftover Earlywood line.

Founding year: Aspire’s 2021 expansion piece and the LinkedIn company profile both state founded in 1975. The live About page says only “For over 40 years”; homepage copy says “45 years of expertise”; the brackets hub also says “50 years of expertise.” Those are marketing decade phrases, not a printed calendar year on About. 1975 is included here from those secondary public sources, not from a dated founding paragraph on ghorthodontics.com/about. About (researched Aug 2026) names Lou Azzara as CEO and Frank Vrettos as COO; older MDR blog quotes then-CEO John Voskuil—use the live About page for current officers.

About: design and manufacture in the USA; registered to sell in 93 countries (company figure). Homepage product pillars: brackets, bands & attachments, buccal tubes, wires, elastomerics. This is a fixed-appliance and wire specialty house, not an aligner-platform company. tune® Clear Aligners accounts transitioned to ARCAD Labs effective July 1, 2025 per G&H’s own transition page—do not list Tune as a current G&H-fulfilled aligner SKU.

Who it’s for

  • Specialists who want a U.S.-made wire house with a full named alloy map (NiTi, thermal CuNiTi, SS, heat-treated SS, TMA, NiCo, plus Wilcock Australian) rather than a mega-vendor leftover wire bin
  • Offices shopping metal twin and self-ligating (miniPrevail®, Agility®) plus ceramic (Auralis™, Agility® Ceramic) from one Franklin catalog
  • Teams that still buy bands, buccal tubes, and elastomerics (ties, chain, intraoral elastics, separators) from the same manufacturer as the archwires
  • Practices that care about EU MDR continuity on a full fixed portfolio (G&H’s own MDR blog positions that as a reason they stayed in brackets/wires while some peers narrowed to aligners)
  • Doctors who will order through ghorthodontics.com / OrthoClub (or a distributor) and treat “Pain-Free Guarantee / Smile Guarantee” language as vendor service positioning, not a clinical outcome claim
  • Groups that used Tune aligners and need to know support/planning/billing moved to ARCAD as of mid-2025—not practices hunting a G&H-branded aligner tray line today

Notable products

Official names below match the live Products hubs (Wires, Brackets, Buccal Tubes, Bands, Elastomerics / Elastic Ligatures) as researched in August 2026. Availability varies by country and channel. Slot sizes, prescriptions, force curves, and kit contents belong on the current catalog or with your G&H / distributor rep—not on this page. “Moves teeth farther and faster,” “perfect archwire,” “nearly invisible,” and satisfaction percentages are manufacturer positioning.

Archwires (live wires hub)

  • G4™ Nickel Titanium — proprietary NiTi formula; vendor: lower initial load forces and residual force as full-shape recovery progresses; formulated to perform at body temperature.
  • M5™ Thermal Copper Nickel Titanium — softer at room temperature for easier engagement; transformation completed by ~35 °C per product copy; proprietary polish for sliding.
  • S3™ Stainless Steel — medical-grade SS; positioned for multiple step bends without breakage.
  • XR1™ Heat Treated Stainless Steel — stress-relieved / heat-treated SS for higher force and resiliency.
  • TitanMoly™ Titanium Molybdenum — TMA-class wire; nickel-free positioning for sensitive patients; vendor “NiTi feel + SS force” language.
  • Colboloy™ Blue Nickel Cobalt — bendable NiCo alloy; heat-treatable after forming per product page.
  • A.J. Wilcock® Australian Wire — imported exclusively to North America through G&H; hardness/resilience positioning for special applications—not a G&H-melted alloy.

Older product sheets and shop PDFs also name B3® Beta Titanium, G4orce™ Progressive Force, and Ultraesthetic tooth-colored finishes. Prefer the live wires hub names above when writing formulary notes; confirm any historic SKU against the current catalog before restocking.

Metal brackets (live brackets hub)

  • miniPrevail® TWIN — low-profile miniature twin; MIM 17-4 surgical-grade stainless; anatomical 80-gauge mesh base; chamfered slot / center scribe.
  • miniPrevail® SL — interactive self-ligating; NiTi spring clip; passive with round wires up to .020″, active with rectangular wires from .016″×.022″; clip opens with explorer.
  • Agility® Twin — cast 17-4 stainless twin; torque-in-base; Anchor-Lock™ pad language on the product page.
  • Agility® SL — passive self-ligating; single-piece MIM 17-4; convertible NiTi clip; dual locking mechanism per vendor copy.

Ceramic / aesthetic brackets

  • Auralis™ Ceramic — ceramic injection molded; high-translucency positioning; multi-contoured base with mechanical undercuts; controlled debond at adhesive interface (vendor).
  • Agility® Ceramic — CIM translucent ceramic; integrated portal base for mechanical retention; torque-in-base.

Volume 16 catalog still lists Prism™ Composite; it is not a tile on the live brackets hub (Auralis, miniPrevail SL/TWIN, Agility SL/Twin/Ceramic). Confirm before assuming Prism is current web inventory.

Buccal tubes (live tubes hub)

  • miniPrevail® TUBE — low-profile nonconvertible MIM 17-4; crosshair / bullseye placement marks; funneled entrance.
  • VIPER™ — low-profile MIM 17-4; convertible and nonconvertible; torque-in-base; malleable ball hook.
  • MAGNUM™ — cast 316 SS; convertible / nonconvertible options.
  • Agility® Buccal Tubes — cast 316 SS; single/double, hooks, convertible / nonconvertible options.

Bands

  • Saturn™ Molar Bands — stainless; 36 full-size increments; micro-etched interiors; electrochemical size ID.
  • Titan™ Molar Bands — stainless; 30 half-size increments; same etching / festooned-edge family language. Free welding of attachments is vendor service copy on the bands page.

Elastomerics

  • Categories on the elastomerics hub: ligatures, intraoral elastics, power chains, power threads, protective archwire tubing, separators.
  • Ligature styles on the elastic-ligature page: Bambino–Ties™, Versa–Ties™, Select–Ties™, Mini–Ties™ (latex-free stick packs; 33 colors claimed), plus DesignerTies® (shaped novelty ties) and cane / bulk clear and grey.
  • Intraoral elastics: surgical-grade natural latex with continuous force testing (vendor); non-latex options; personalized practice packaging offered.
  • Shop/catalog materials also use Dyna-Link™ for chain color-coordination with Versa-Ties—confirm the live chain SKU name on the current shop before writing a PO as “Power Chain” alone.

Not current G&H-fulfilled (important)

  • tune® Clear Aligners — G&H’s transition page: effective July 1, 2025, Tune accounts move to ARCAD Labs for case planning, manufacturing, support, and billing. Historic MDR blog copy still mentions “aligners” in a portfolio sentence; that does not override the 2025 transition page. Do not stock or promise Tune as a G&H-manufactured aligner line today.

Adhesives / third-party chairside

Catalog tables of contents show Reliance bonding products and other clinical supplies sold through G&H channels. Same pattern as other specialty houses: confirm who manufactures the paste before treating it as a G&H compound.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Real wire depth with current, named alloys on a public hub (G4 / M5 / S3 / XR1 / TitanMoly / Colboloy / Wilcock)—easier to formulary than an anonymous “NiTi assortment.”
  • Bracket map on the live hub is clear: miniPrevail twin vs interactive SL, Agility twin vs passive SL, plus two ceramic lanes (Auralis vs Agility Ceramic portals).
  • Same manufacturer lane for wires + brackets + tubes + bands + elastomerics, which matters if you want one Franklin QC story instead of three distributors’ private labels.
  • Legal name, Linville Way address, ISO scope, and “commercial name = G&H Orthodontics” are on company-hosted pages/PDFs—not guesswork.
  • EU MDR blog and dual certificates are concrete regulatory paper if your EU or MDR-sensitive supply chain cares who stayed certified on fixed appliances.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Founding-year marketing is sloppy. About says “over 40 years,” home says 45, brackets say 50; Aspire/LinkedIn say 1975. Do not treat decade slogans as audited chronology.
  • Address leftovers. Earlywood Drive still appears on older shop chrome; Graham Road shows up in 2021 local press. Remit and shipping: 40 Linville Way.
  • Aligner story changed in 2025. Anyone still quoting G&H as an aligner company from pre-transition decks will mis-order. Tune → ARCAD.
  • CEO / officer names drift across blog vs About. Use the live About page, not a 2022 MDR quote.
  • Catalog leftovers (Prism, older B3 / G4orce sheet names, Vapor-as-ceramic in old PDFs vs live Auralis / Agility Ceramic) will trip purchasing if you shop from a stale PDF.
  • “Pain-Free Guarantee,” “99.9% satisfaction,” “farther and faster,” and “perfect archwire” are campaign and service copy, not clinical evidence. Force graphs and ANSI/ADA wire-spec claims belong in the current data sheet you actually ordered against.
  • Deep SKU surface (prescription × slot × twin vs SL × tube convertible options × 33 elastomeric colors) is a formulary project for a new office.

Buying notes

  • Start at ghorthodontics.com → Products hubs and the shop / Volume catalog. Do not email the brand from this site; this page is informational only.
  • US & Canada: 1-800-526-1026; international +1 317-346-6655; orders@ghortho.com. Mailing: 40 Linville Way, Franklin, IN 46131.
  • Legal manufacturer on EU/ISO paper: G&H Wire Company, Inc. Commercial brand: G&H Orthodontics. OrthoClub is the loyalty lane, not a separate factory.
  • Confirm slot (.018 / .022), prescription (Roth / MBT / edgewise as offered on the SKU), twin vs SL (interactive miniPrevail SL vs passive Agility SL), and whether ceramic means Auralis or Agility Ceramic before reorder.
  • If you still have active Tune cases, follow the ARCAD transition contacts on G&H’s Tune page—not a G&H aligner manufacturing assumption.
  • Product availability varies by market. Returns, IFUs, and material lists live under Resources / quality & regulatory documents on the site.

Bottom line

G&H Orthodontics is the commercial face of G&H Wire Company in Franklin, Indiana: a U.S. specialty manufacturer whose live catalog is still centered on wires, brackets, tubes, bands, and elastomerics—not on running an aligner factory (Tune moved to ARCAD in mid-2025). Shop the live hub names (G4, M5, S3, XR1, TitanMoly, Colboloy, Wilcock; miniPrevail Twin/SL; Agility Twin/SL/Ceramic; Auralis; VIPER / MAGNUM / miniPrevail TUBE; Saturn / Titan bands; Versa-Ties / Bambino-Ties / DesignerTies)—not Earlywood letterhead, not Prism leftovers, not “G&H the aligner company.” Treat decade slogans and satisfaction stats as vendor voice; verify force and material claims on the current IFU and data sheet.
