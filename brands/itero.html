iTero

Align Technology’s intraoral scanner and imaging line—currently iTero Lumina and the iTero Element Plus Series—for digital impressions, chairside visualization, and native Invisalign case submission. Distinct from Align’s Invisalign aligner brand.

HQ: Tempe, Arizona, USA · Researched 2026-09-02 · Official site

Overview

iTero is Align Technology’s intraoral scanner and imaging brand, not the aligner. Clear aligners live on Align’s Invisalign line (see that seed page). The live doctor hub is itero.com (www.itero.com 308s there). Align’s corporate site also hosts an iTero scanner hub. Align still describes itself as the company behind the Invisalign System, iTero scanners and services, and exocad CAD/CAM software.

How the names stack (verified, not a rename):

  • Cadent commercially launched the iTero digital impression system in the mid-2000s (Cadent’s own 2006 launch copy; Orthodontic Products covered a “next-generation” iTero at ADA in October 2007, noting introduction “last year”). Cadent’s 2006 PR called the company founded in 1999. That is Cadent’s founding year, not a calendar founding year printed for the iTero brand itself, so this page omits founded rather than using Align’s 1997 date or inventing an iTero year.
  • Align acquired Cadent Holdings (makers of iTero and OrthoCAD iOC) and announced completion on May 2, 2011. iTero did not become Invisalign, and Invisalign did not become iTero.
  • iTero Element is the confocal-imaging generation. The live U.S. comparison page still lists iTero Element Plus Series (“iTero Gold Standard”) and iTero Element 5D imaging system (“Smart Value,” including Certified Pre-Owned / PC-only positioning).
  • iTero Lumina is the current flagship generation (Align launch PR January 31, 2024; ortho-capable commercial availability from February 2024). It uses iTero Multi-Direct Capture (MDC) rather than the Element confocal stack. Align did not rename Element to Lumina; the site still sells/supports both families and offers Element Plus wand trade-up to a Lumina wand on the same cart/mobile platform where available.

HQ: Align’s global corporate headquarters is Tempe, Arizona, USA (Align announced the move effective January 1, 2021; current investor/company materials still list Tempe). San Jose remains Align’s stated innovation/product campus. iTero launch PRs are still datelined San Jose, Tempe, and Petach Tikva, Israel—useful for R&D geography, not a second “iTero HQ” on this page.

This page covers the scanner/imaging platform for practices. It is not patient marketing, not an Invisalign package list, and not a substitute for Align IFUs, service-plan terms, or your own diagnostic protocol.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and qualified dentists who want native Invisalign scan-to-submit (auto-upload, Outcome Simulator / Outcome Simulator Pro, Progress Assessment) without an STL hop—while knowing Align also accepts scans from certain third-party scanners on its interoperability list
  • GP and ortho-restorative offices shopping a chairside visualization stack (Align Oral Health Suite, TimeLapse, Occlusogram, integrated intraoral camera) rather than a scan-only wand
  • Practices evaluating NIRI (Near Infra-Red Imaging) as an aid in detecting interproximal caries above the gingiva—on SKUs that actually include it (currently documented on iTero Lumina Pro and iTero Element 5D Plus, not on Lumina without NIRI or Element 5D Plus Lite)
  • Offices already on Element Plus that Align says can upgrade the wand to Lumina and keep cart/mobile accessories, vs. first-time buyers choosing Lumina cart, mobile, or PC
  • Labs and restorative workflows that need open STL/PLY export, MyiTero lab routing, and (where licensed) iTero Design Suite / exocad-powered in-practice design—not just ortho records
  • Groups that will budget the scanner plus an ongoing iTero service plan (cloud storage, software, hardware replacement). Several consultation tools are gated by model, software, and plan—not by owning a wand alone

Notable products

Names below match the live U.S./Canada iTero hub and Align investor PRs as researched September 2, 2026. Country availability, NIRI clearance, PC configuration, and Certified Pre-Owned stock change; confirm with Align before you write a PO. Speed, accuracy, “replace intraoral photos,” revenue-lift, and treatment-acceptance figures are manufacturer claims (mostly Align “data on file” bench tests or small surveys)—not independent outcome guarantees.

Current flagship — iTero Lumina™

  • iTero Lumina intraoral scanner — MDC imaging; Align positions a 3× larger field of view, ~2× faster scanning, 50% smaller / 45% lighter wand, and 25 mm capture distance, all versus the iTero Element 5D wand/system (not versus every competitor). Live product page lists cart, mobile, and PC configurations. PC units need an Align-specified computer; the Lumina page names Contec as the authorized computer vendor.
  • iTero Lumina Pro imaging system — same Lumina platform with iTero NIRI. Align’s March 6, 2025 PR also calls it the iTero Lumina Pro dental imaging system and tied restorative/diagnostic software to the Lumina family (existing non-Pro Lumina units getting restorative/diagnostic software without NIRI). Use the live product page name on a quote; the PR name is the same device, not a fourth SKU.
  • iTero Lumina intraoral scanner (without NIRI) — explicitly listed next to Pro on the Lumina solutions tiles. Do not assume every Lumina wand has NIRI.

Still listed — iTero Element™ family (confocal)

  • iTero Element Plus Series — live product page: iTero Element 5D Plus (NIRI) and iTero Element 5D Plus Lite (excludes NIRI), in cart and mobile. Comparison hub also mentions PC. Align launched Plus on February 3, 2021. This is the generation many Invisalign offices still run; it is not Lumina.
  • iTero Element 5D imaging system — still a column on itero.com/our-solutions as “Smart Value,” PC only, including Certified Pre-Owned (laptop not provided; extra-cost training note on that config). Treat it as the older 5D unit still in the catalog/CPO lane, not as a silent rename of 5D Plus.

Software and connected tools (not extra wands)

  • Align Oral Health Suite — consultation UI on Lumina and Element Plus (not claimed for every legacy Element). Vendor revenue and acceptance stats are Align surveys / practice-production analyses.
  • iTero NIRI — near-infrared imaging aid for interproximal caries above the gingiva; Align compares sensitivity to bitewings in its own study language. It is not a radiograph replacement and is SKU-locked.
  • iTero TimeLapse, iTero Occlusogram, iTero scan report, integrated HD intraoral camera
  • Invisalign Outcome Simulator and Invisalign Outcome Simulator Pro (in-face). Align’s iTero hub currently names Pro on Element Plus and Lumina; some older footnotes said Plus only—confirm on the live scanner and service plan.
  • Invisalign Case Assessment, auto-upload, Progress Assessment
  • MyiTero portal — cloud case management and lab communication
  • iTero Design Suite — in-practice design (models, splints, restorations, mock-ups), powered with exocad; licensed software, not a scanner model
  • iTero service plan — Align’s published bundle for cloud backup, unlimited scans, support, replacement, and software updates. Several “Pro” features require an active plan.

Not this brand

  • Invisalign aligners, ClinCheck treatment planning as an aligner product, and Vivera retainers — Align, but not iTero hardware.
  • OrthoCAD iOC / iCast / iQ / iRecord — Cadent-era names from the 2011 acquisition PR. Do not treat them as current iTero SKUs unless they reappear on the live hub.
  • exocad — Align-owned CAD/CAM, sold as its own line.

Always verify eligibility, territory, CPO vs new, and current SKU naming with Align. Public pages do not list street prices.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Tightest Invisalign chairside loop Align sells: scan, visualize, auto-upload, simulate, track. If the office lives on Invisalign volume, that workflow is the actual product, not the wand slogan.
  • Two live generations with a documented upgrade path (Element Plus wand → Lumina wand on existing cart/mobile where offered), plus cart / mobile / PC—and a CPO Element 5D lane if you want cheaper confocal hardware.
  • Consultation tools (Oral Health Suite, TimeLapse, in-face simulator, scan reports) are mature sales-and-records software, which is why so many ortho offices standardized here even when a cheaper scanner can export STL.
  • Open export (STL/PLY) and a large iTero-enabled lab list, so restorative/lab work is not Invisalign-only.
  • NIRI, when you buy the SKU that has it, is a real extra modality—not a rename of the intraoral camera.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Capital cost + subscription. The wand is not the whole bill. Service plan, software packs, Design Suite, financing, and trade-in terms sit in the contract. Align’s public site will not quote a number.
  • SKU confusion is a purchasing hazard. Lumina vs Lumina Pro vs Lumina without NIRI; Element 5D vs 5D Plus vs 5D Plus Lite; new vs Certified Pre-Owned; cart vs mobile vs PC (Contec laptop). NIRI and several visualization tools do not follow the word “iTero” onto every box.
  • Not the only Invisalign-capable scanner. Align publishes a third-party interoperability list. Buying iTero for “Invisalign compatibility” alone is a weak reason if a scanner you already own is on that list.
  • Claims are vendor-weighted. Full-jaw accuracy vs Trios 5 / CS3800 / Medit i700 / Alliedstar is Align bench testing (ADA/ANSI 132, data on file). “Replace intraoral photos” is for Invisalign record-taking, from a n=22 limited-market survey. Photogrammetry-threshold language is market-specific (Align notes it is not EU at this stage in the cited footnote). Treat campaign math as homework, not IFU.
  • NIRI is an aid, above the gingiva, on specific models. Align’s own comparison-to-bitewing copy does not retire your radiographic protocol.
  • Ecosystem gravity is real: portals, storage, simulators, and Align commercial terms. Open STL export helps; it does not make the chairside software stack interchangeable with 3Shape or Medit overnight.
  • Regional pages differ (U.S./Canada copy vs GB/AU). Feature and config availability is not global just because itero.com shows a tile.

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.
