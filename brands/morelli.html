Morelli

Brazilian orthodontic manufacturer (Dental Morelli Ltda., trading as Morelli Ortodontia) in Sorocaba, São Paulo, making MIM metal and ceramic brackets, tubes, bands, archwires, elastics, mini-screws, expansion screws, instruments, and orthodontic oral-hygiene products since 1980.

HQ: Sorocaba, São Paulo, Brazil · Founded 1980 · Researched 2026-10-21 · Official site

Overview

Morelli is a Brazilian orthodontic manufacturer based in Sorocaba, in São Paulo state. The legal entity is Dental Morelli Ltda. (CNPJ 65.441.255/0001-35), and it trades as Morelli Ortodontia, the name you’ll see on its social channels and catalogs. The company dates its founding to 1980 in Sorocaba. The current CNPJ registration shows an activity start date of 1991, which likely reflects a later registration of the entity rather than a different founding story. It is privately held, and we found no parent company.

Morelli calls itself the pioneer and sales leader for orthodontic products in Brazil. It runs a network of more than 200 distributors there and sells abroad in the Americas, the Middle East, Asia, and Europe. Its own pages put that at “more than 50” or “more than 70” countries, so treat the exact number loosely. The catalog lists more than 2,000 items. Brackets, tubes, and accessories are made in-house by metal injection molding (MIM), and ceramic parts by ceramic injection molding (CIM).

In the U.S.: FDA’s public database lists Dental Morelli Ltda. as a registered foreign establishment in Brazil, with a U.S. agent and 25 device listings. Those listings include metal and ceramic brackets, tubes, orthodontic wire, elastic bands, expansion screws, orthodontic implants (mini-screws), and pliers. Two 510(k) clearances are on file: ceramic brackets (K131197, 2013) and an orthodontic miniscrew (K130476, 2014). We found no U.S. subsidiary and no authorized U.S. distributor named on Morelli’s own site. Some independent U.S. resellers list Morelli items, but confirm U.S. availability and support with a distributor before you plan around it.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and clinics in Brazil and across Latin America looking for a broad local catalog with deep bracket-prescription coverage
  • Practices that want brackets, tubes, bands, wires, elastics, mini-screws, and expansion screws from a single manufacturer
  • Buyers who care about long-term availability of replacement brackets for a given prescription
  • Distributors and international buyers looking for a value-oriented MIM bracket source

Notable products

  • Metal brackets: the Max line, single-piece MIM stainless steel in the main prescriptions (Roth, MBT, Edgewise and others), plus self-ligating options
  • Esthetic brackets: ceramic brackets (FDA-cleared under K131197) and polycrystalline/sapphire-style esthetic lines
  • Tubes, bands, and accessories: bonding and welding tubes, molar bands, buttons, and bite turbos
  • Archwires and wire: NiTi, stainless steel, and TMA preformed arches, plus straight lengths and rolls
  • Elastics: intraoral elastics, ligatures, and chain
  • Mini-screws (TADs): orthodontic anchorage mini-screws and their drivers and kits
  • Functional orthopedics: Hyrax-type expansion screws, headgear and facebows, and traction devices
  • Instruments and oral hygiene: orthodontic pliers and hand instruments, interdental brushes, and tongue scrapers

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • An established manufacturer with in-house MIM/CIM production and four decades of history
  • A very wide catalog, with many bracket prescriptions and replacement parts available by the unit
  • Publicly verifiable regulatory footprint: ANVISA GMP and ISO 13485 certificates shown on the official site, plus an FDA establishment registration with device listings and two 510(k) clearances
  • A strong distributor network and local support in Brazil

Cons

  • The official site and much of its documentation are mainly in Portuguese; English material is limited
  • No authorized U.S. distributor or U.S. office is clearly named, so U.S. support, pricing, and lead times are uncertain
  • Some company claims (country counts, performance figures in catalogs) are the company’s own and vary between pages; we found no independent comparisons
  • FDA clearances on file date to 2013–2014; newer product lines may be covered only by listings or exemptions, so check each item’s status

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

In Brazil and much of Latin America, Morelli is a mainstream everyday supplier. Practices buy bracket kits, tubes, wires, and elastics through local dental distributors, and replacement brackets for a given prescription are easy to get. For a practice elsewhere, including in the U.S., Morelli is more of a specialty or value option. Before you switch a bracket system, confirm which distributor serves your region, whether the exact items you need are FDA-listed or cleared, how returns and lead times work, and whether the prescriptions match your current setups.
