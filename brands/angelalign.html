Angelalign (Angel Aligner)

Global clear-aligner platform from Angelalign Technology Inc. (HKEX 6699), with doctor-facing Angel Aligner products, iOrtho planning software, and growing North American availability.

HQ: Shanghai, China · Founded 2003 · Researched 2026-09-22 · Official site

Overview

Angelalign Technology Inc. (Hong Kong stock code 6699) is a clear-aligner manufacturer and digital orthodontics company. The doctor-facing product brand is Angel Aligner™. Investor materials say the business was established in 2003, is headquartered in Shanghai, China (Yangpu District / KIC campus), and lists products and services in over 60 countries. The company IPO’d on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2021 and has been expanding outside China, including North America.

Public U.S. materials point doctors to www.angelaligner.com and the iOrtho™ web portal. A July 2026 company release (San Clemente, Calif.) described Angel Aligner as a challenger-scale global aligner vendor (#2 by company claim) with a growing U.S. footprint and a new U.S. manufacturing facility. Treat “#2 globally” and smile-count marketing as company claims, not independent rankings measured on this page.

This brief is for practices comparing clear-aligner brands and portals. It is not patient marketing, a protocol, or medical advice. Case selection, compliance, and finishing still sit with the treating doctor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and qualified dentists evaluating a full-service clear-aligner lab + digital planning portal as an alternative or complement to Invisalign and other national brands
  • Offices that want tiered product lines (adult/complex, selective, early/mixed dentition, retainers) under one manufacturer account
  • Practices interested in manufacturer features such as built-in aligner buttons for elastics, mandibular-advancement protocols, and CBCT-aware planning tools inside iOrtho (confirm what is live in your territory)
  • Multi-location groups comparing contract terms, turnaround, and clinical support from a large Asia-headquartered vendor with expanding U.S. ops

It is a weaker shortlist if you only need in-office print/CAD software with no lab relationship, or if your territory/credentialing does not yet support Angel Aligner submissions. Confirm U.S. (or other) eligibility, scanner requirements, and pricing with Angel Aligner sales — not from this page.

Notable products

Names below match Angel Aligner’s public U.S. portfolio and July 2026 trade/PR coverage reviewed September 22, 2026. SKUs, wear schedules, and software releases change; verify current doctor materials before you brief patients or sign a contract.

Aligner lines (U.S. portfolio pages)

  • Angel Aligner Pro™ — marketed for more demanding / complex cases. Public Pro copy describes a dual-aligner “7+3” stage (softer masterControl S™ wear followed by firmer masterControl™), plus features such as angelButton™ (integrated aligner button for elastics), A6 Mandibular Advancement™, and Intelligent Root System (IRS) planning aids. Wear-day and force-sequence language are manufacturer design claims.
  • Angel Aligner Select — portfolio tier for a broader/selective case mix (confirm indications vs Pro with current doctor guides).
  • Angel Aligner KiD — early / mixed-dentition focused line in the published portfolio; investor commentary has emphasized early-intervention volume in China. Pediatric and mixed-dentition use still depends on clinical judgment and local labeling.
  • Angel Aligner Retainer — retention product in the same portfolio family.

Software and clinical features

  • iOrtho™ — Angel Aligner’s digital treatment-planning / case portal (web access cited as iortho.angelalign.com in 2026 releases). iOrtho Release 6.2 went live around July 1, 2026 per Orthodontic Products and PR Newswire: IRS Gold CBCT-related 3D views (bone/sinus/airway visualization claims), variable trimline options (standard scalloped, full-arch high, posterior-only high), redesigned primary/refinement Rx forms, and UI tool consolidation. Doctor quotes in those releases are marketing testimonials, not Ortho Index endorsements.
  • angelButton™ — built-in aligner button for elastic mechanics (2024 “Best New Product” mention on Angel’s site from The Digital Orthodontist; still a vendor award callout).
  • A6 Mandibular Advancement™ — Class II / mandibular-advancement oriented feature set described on Pro materials.

Always verify eligibility, territory rules, scanner/upload paths, refinement policy, and current SKU naming with Angel Aligner directly.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Real global manufacturer scale (listed company, multi-country distribution) rather than a thin white-label reseller story
  • Clear doctor-facing U.S. brand site with a named portfolio (Pro / Select / KiD / Retainer) and an actively updated planning portal (iOrtho 6.2 in mid-2026)
  • Differentiating feature pitch vs commodity trays: integrated buttons, mandibular-advancement protocols, dual-material stage design, CBCT-oriented visualization in software — useful to evaluate even if you later reject the claims
  • Potential competitive pricing / contract leverage versus the market leader for offices willing to run a second (or primary) aligner brand — get a current quote; Ortho Index does not publish fee tables

Cons / watch-outs

  • Consumer recognition in many U.S. markets still trails Invisalign; case-acceptance conversations may need more doctor explanation
  • Corporate HQ and manufacturing roots are China-based; U.S. support, lead times, and the new U.S. plant story should be diligence items on any contract (confirm live production and shipping SLAs with sales)
  • Marketing language (“redefining excellence,” “#2 globally,” “predictable,” specific wear-day protocols, CBCT safety implications) is vendor messaging. Software views do not replace radiographic interpretation or clinical judgment
  • KiD / early-intervention positioning is commercially important to the company; pediatric case selection remains the doctor’s responsibility and is not expanded here
  • Contract terms, volume commitments, refinement fees, and scanner compatibility need the same careful review you would give any national aligner vendor

Warm and direct: Angel Aligner is a clear-aligner toolset and lab relationship, not a promise. Ortho Index does not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.
