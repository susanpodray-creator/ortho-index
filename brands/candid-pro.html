CandidPro (Candid)

Doctor-directed clear aligner system for general dentists, orthodontists and DSOs, from Candid (Candid Care Co.). Candid closed its direct-to-consumer business and studios in January 2022 and now sells only through dental practices.

HQ: New York, NY, USA (per company press materials; FDA registration lists Columbus, Ohio) · Founded 2017 · Researched 2026-11-04 · Official site

Get a quote Claim this brand

Overview

CandidPro is the clear aligner system sold by Candid (registered with FDA as Candid Care Co.). Candid launched in 2017 as a direct-to-consumer (DTC) aligner brand with retail “Candid Studios.” In January 2022 it announced it was closing its DTC business and all 45 studios to focus entirely on CandidPro, a model in which a patient’s own dentist or orthodontist directs treatment, supported by Candid’s orthodontist reviewers and remote monitoring.

Ownership and status: Candid describes itself in press materials as New York-based. Its FDA establishment registration lists an address in Columbus, Ohio, and third-party profiles list other locations, so the HQ shown here is the company’s own description. Candid appears to be privately held and venture-backed; we found no public announcement of an acquisition, merger or shutdown as of this page’s research date. Funding totals and recent rounds reported by third-party databases vary and could not be confirmed from primary sources, so they are not stated here. The CandidPro site and newsroom were active in 2026 (product updates, provider events), which indicates the platform is still operating.

FDA status (openFDA): Candid Care Co. holds a device registration with FDA listings for products including “Candid Tray,” a cheek retractor and the ScanBox Pro monitoring accessory. We found no 510(k) clearance under a Candid applicant name in openFDA. Sequential aligners are normally reviewed under product code NXC; aligner companies sometimes rely on a contract manufacturer’s or partner’s clearance, but we could not confirm which clearance, if any, covers CandidPro aligners. Practices should ask the company for the regulatory basis of the product.

Who it’s for

General dentists who want to offer clear aligners with orthodontist support, plus orthodontists and DSOs. Candid’s provider marketing is aimed mainly at general practices. Patients cannot buy CandidPro directly; treatment goes through a participating provider.

Notable products

  • CandidPro Comprehensive (CandidPro Clear Aligners): full aligner treatment directed by the provider, with optional review by a Candid orthodontist (“CandidConsult” in company materials).
  • CandidPro Elements: a limited aligner product for simple cases with per-tray pricing. Per the company, it excludes CandidConsult and the ProMonitoring platform unless added for a fee.
  • ProMonitoring and ScanBox Pro: a patient smartphone app plus a ScanBox accessory for at-home progress photos reviewed remotely. Some phone models need an LED adapter, which the company ships to eligible patients.
  • PermaForm retainers and Zoom whitening (Philips brand) are also listed on the CandidPro site.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Provider-directed model since 2022, unlike Candid’s earlier DTC approach.
  • Built-in remote monitoring may reduce in-person check-ins for some cases.
  • Optional orthodontist review can support general dentists with less aligner experience.
  • A limited-case product (Elements) and loyalty pricing tiers give some pricing flexibility.

Cons

  • No 510(k) clearance found under Candid’s own name; the regulatory basis for the aligners is unconfirmed.
  • Pricing is not published publicly.
  • Company history includes the DTC model and its 2022 closure; some clinicians may weigh that history.
  • Remote monitoring depends on patient compliance and compatible smartphones (some need an adapter).
  • Much of the company’s material is marketing (revenue and case-volume claims) and has not been independently verified.
  • HQ and corporate details differ between company materials and registration records.

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

CandidPro is an alternative to other provider-sold aligner systems. Practices submit scans and records, approve treatment plans in Candid’s platform, and can use ProMonitoring for remote check-ins between visits. Before adopting it, practices typically confirm the regulatory clearance basis, pricing, refinement and retainer policies, data-handling terms for the monitoring app, and how remote check-ins fit their state’s teledentistry rules.
