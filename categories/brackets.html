Adenta

Family-run German bracket and wire maker (Adenta GmbH, Gilching, near Munich) that dates its roots to 1949. Its live web shop lists CNC-milled metal twins (CROWN, ECONOLINE), aesthetic brackets (CLEAR sapphire, DISCREET, BREEZE SL self-ligating), and the TRIAMOND Wedgewise bracket. Older Evolution SLT and JOY lingual lines are not in the current shop, and the U.S. office site is a placeholder.

Dentaurum

German specialty manufacturer (DENTAURUM GmbH & Co. KG) of brackets, wires, expansion screws, acrylics, TADs, and lab products—discovery, equilibrium, rematitan, Orthocryl, tomas—plus implant and digital lines, based in Ispringen since 1886 roots.

Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics

Specialty orthodontics unit of Dentsply Sirona (Nasdaq: XRAY). After the 2020 exit from traditional GAC brackets, bands, tubes, and wires, the public catalog is SureSmile digital appliances plus Essix plastics and IPR—not a GAC or SureSmile rename.

Forestadent

German specialty manufacturer (FORESTADENT Bernhard Förster GmbH) of brackets, wires, expansion screws, TADs, and appliances—BioQuick, 2D Lingual, Sprint, OrthoEasy, SARA, plus FAS aligners—made in Pforzheim and sold through a U.S. subsidiary.

Henry Schein Orthodontics

Orthodontics business of Henry Schein, Inc.—Carriere Motion bite correctors and SLX 3D self-ligating brackets, Aria twins, Smilers aligners, and the historic Ortho Organizers / Ortho Technology catalogs—now ordered through Henry Schein Dental.

U.S. custom digital orthodontics company (KLOwen Braces, Inc.) in Richardson, Texas, offering fully custom metal and clear brackets, in-house treatment-planning software, a digital lab, and 3D-printed indirect bonding trays, sold only to orthodontists.

U.S. orders now go through Henry Schein Dental.

Former 3M Unitek / 3M Oral Care fixed-appliance and bonding catalog—Clarity ceramic brackets, Victory Series and SmartClip metal lines, APC precoat, plus Clarity Aligners—now marketed by Solventum after the 2024 3M Health Care spin.

Japanese orthodontic manufacturer (Tomy Inc., Tokyo, with a Fukushima factory) behind Clippy self-ligating brackets, Crystaline ceramics, OPA-K, and wires. Sold in Japan and Asia by Tomy International Inc.; in the U.S., Tomy-made brackets are marketed by GC Orthodontics America under GC brand names.
