Reliance Orthodontic Products

U.S. manufacturer specializing in orthodontic bonding adhesives, primers, band cements, and related chairside materials since 1981.

HQ: Itasca, Illinois, USA · Founded 1981 · Researched 2026-08-23 · Official site

Overview

Reliance Orthodontic Products is a U.S. specialty manufacturer focused on bonding adhesives and related chairside materials for orthodontic dental work. The company positions itself around adhesives for brackets, bands, retainers, and other appliances across a range of tooth and restorative surfaces—not as a full bracket, wire, or aligner systems brand.

According to its About page, Reliance has operated since 1981 and emphasizes clinical field testing before commercial release, plus technical support for bonding questions. Orthodontic teams most often encounter the brand through primers, light-cure pastes, band cements, and flowable composites used in daily bonding workflows. Educational content is offered through the RelianceU.tech portal (videos, literature, and related clinical support materials).

Pages like this treat Reliance as a supplies / adhesives vendor for specialty practices.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic practices standardizing bonding systems (primers + pastes)
  • Offices that bond to mixed enamel and restorative surfaces and want a dedicated all-surface primer workflow
  • Clinics comparing light-cure bracket adhesives, band cements, and retainer composites
  • Purchasing leads evaluating IFUs, delivery formats (syringe, tips, kits), and material handling
  • Teams that want manufacturer-side bonding education and live product support alongside the materials

Notable products

  • Assure Plus — All-surface light-cure bonding primer / adhesion enhancer intended for use on enamel and many restorative substrates; sold in bottle and unidose formats
  • GoTo — Light-cure bracket adhesive paste (push syringe and tip presentations); marketed for handling characteristics such as limited bracket drift and fluorescing flash cleanup under black light
  • Light Bond, Pad Lock, Phase II, Rely-a-Bond — Additional bracket adhesive lines and kits listed in the official bracket adhesives catalog (fluoride and non-fluoride options appear on several SKUs)
  • Ultra Band-Lok — Light-cure compomer band cement; also described by the manufacturer for acrylic appliances and occlusal build-ups
  • Flow Tain / Flow Tain L.V. — Flowable light-cure composites commonly positioned for lingual retainers, splinting, and related attachment uses
  • Ortho FlexTech — Fixed lingual retainer chain product featured on the manufacturer homepage (retainer hardware, not an adhesive)
  • ReliaMolds — Disposable molds for forming composite auxiliaries chairside with compatible light-cure composites

Always verify the specific product’s intended use and IFU for orthodontic applications; catalog names, shades, and fluoride options change over time.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Clear specialty focus on orthodontic bonding adhesives rather than a broad general-dental catalog
  • Wide adhesive portfolio (primers, pastes, band cements, flowables) under one manufacturer
  • Official IFUs/SDS links and RelianceU.tech education for technique support
  • Direct technical support phone line promoted for bonding questions

Cons

  • Not an appliance company—brackets, archwires, and clear aligner systems come from other vendors
  • Online shop pricing often requires login, which can slow quick price comparison
  • Deep SKU variety (kits, viscosities, fluoride options) means teams must standardize carefully to avoid mixed protocols
  • Marketing claims on the site should be read as manufacturer positioning; confirm performance expectations against IFU and your own clinical criteria
