Greyfinch

Cloud, web-based orthodontic practice management software from Greyfinch, LLC of Little Rock, Arkansas, covering scheduling, billing, patient communication, KPI reporting, built-in ceph tracing, and multi-office (OSO/DSO) management.

HQ: Little Rock, Arkansas, USA · Researched 2026-10-30 · Official site

Get a quote Claim this brand

Overview

Greyfinch is cloud orthodontic practice management software. The site footer names the company Greyfinch, LLC, and its contact page gives headquarters at 610 President Clinton Ave, Little Rock, Arkansas. Greyfinch says it was formerly known as AXPM and installed its first beta users in 2017. Third-party databases give the founding year as 2011 or 2012. Because those don’t agree, this page doesn’t state one.

Ownership: Greyfinch appears to be privately held. Third-party databases report small venture rounds in 2016, but Greyfinch hasn’t confirmed them itself. We found no public report of an acquisition or new funding round through October 2026. Treat ownership details as unconfirmed.

Recent developments: Trade press (Orthodontic Products, May 2026) reported a new integration with Align Technology that brings Invisalign and iTero tools into the platform. It also listed partner integrations for phones, remote monitoring, lab Rx, and AI ceph tracing.

Who it’s for

Orthodontic offices of every size that want browser-based practice management: startups, single practices, group practices, and OSO/DSO organizations running several offices or brands from one account.

Notable products

  • Practice management platform: web-based scheduling, treatment planning, billing, and financials, with nothing to install and no per-seat license downloads, according to Greyfinch.
  • Patient experience tools: new patient onboarding, an online appointment widget, Patient Hub, eSignature, secure email, two-way SMS, and telehealth.
  • Growth and reporting: KPI dashboard, NPS and reputation management, and Case Closer for following up on case acceptance.
  • Clinical extras: built-in AI-assisted ceph tracing (landmark plotting with selectable or custom analyses) and records capture from a mobile device.
  • Multi-office support: a dedicated OSO/DSO setup for managing multiple locations and brands.
  • Integrations (per trade press): Align (Invisalign/iTero), DentalMonitoring, Grin, EasyRX, Mango Voice, CephX, and others.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Fully web-based; there are no local servers to maintain
  • Many features are built in that often cost extra elsewhere (SMS, eSignature, reputation, ceph tracing, telehealth)
  • Explicit support for multi-office groups
  • Active integration work, including Align/iTero in 2026

Cons

  • Pricing isn’t published on the official site; you’ll need a demo or quote
  • A smaller, privately held vendor with limited public company information; the founding year and investors aren’t confirmed by the company
  • Marketing copy makes broad “only” and “most advanced” claims that we couldn’t verify independently
  • Moving from an established system means planning a data migration; ask how imaging, ledgers, and history carry over
  • AI ceph tracing is a support tool; landmarks still need clinician review

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

Greyfinch would be the office’s system of record for appointments, patient accounts, billing, and communication. It would replace an on-premise or older cloud practice management system, and could also replace separate texting, review, and e-form tools. Imaging, aligner, and remote-monitoring vendors connect through integrations. When you evaluate it, ask for pricing in writing, the data-migration scope, the list of integrations relevant to your setup, security and backup details, and contract terms.
