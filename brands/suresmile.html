SureSmile (Dentsply Sirona)

Dentsply Sirona clear-aligner and digital ortho platform — browser-based planning, COMPLETE/SELECT full-service packages, robotically bent wires, IDB trays, and open scanner intake with Primescan / DS Core.

HQ: Sarasota, Florida, USA (Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics; OraMetrix roots in Richardson, Texas; parent Charlotte, North Carolina) · Founded 1998 · Researched 2026-09-24 · Official site

Overview

SureSmile® is Dentsply Sirona’s doctor-directed digital orthodontics brand — best known today for clear aligners, but built as a broader platform that also covers robotically bent archwires, indirect bonding (IDB) trays, retainers, and browser-based treatment planning. The technology started at OraMetrix (founded 1998). Dentsply Sirona announced a definitive agreement to acquire OraMetrix on March 26, 2018 (investor release; close planned for Q2 2018). Public DS materials still frame SureSmile as the clinical face of the company’s Orthodontics category after the 2020 exit from traditional GAC-style brackets, bands, tubes, and wires.

Doctor-facing entry points live on Dentsply Sirona’s brand and category pages (suresmile-aligners, orthodontics hub), with planning in SureSmile® Software, optional SureSmile® Simulator on DS Core®, and fabrication through the SureSmile Digital Lab. Legal manufacturing for the ortho unit is Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics Inc. in Sarasota, Florida; parent DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc. is in Charlotte, North Carolina. OraMetrix historically operated from Richardson / Dallas, Texas, with R&D in Berlin.

This brief is for practices comparing clear-aligner labs, digital wire/IDB workflows, and DS ecosystem fit. It is not patient marketing, a protocol, or medical advice. Case selection, compliance, and finishing sit with the treating doctor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and qualified dentists who want a full-service clear-aligner lab plus optional fixed-appliance digital tools (custom wires, IDB) on one software platform
  • Offices already using — or considering — Primescan® / DS Core® who want scan → simulation → SureSmile case flow without forcing a single closed scanner
  • Practices that like a simple two-package pricing story (flat-rate COMPLETE vs pay-per-aligner SELECT) rather than a long step-cap ladder
  • Clinics treating mixed and permanent dentition who want one aligner product line and software path (U.S./Canada expansion public mid-2026)

It is a weaker shortlist if you only need in-office print/CAD with no lab relationship, if you still buy traditional DS/GAC bracket catalogs (those exited), or if your region’s Clearance, VPro, whitening-kit, or DS Core availability does not match sales materials. Confirm eligibility, pricing, and refinement rules with Dentsply Sirona — not from this page.

Notable products

Names below match the live U.S. SureSmile brand page, the August 2025 Orthodontic Product Catalog, and June 2026 mixed-dentition coverage reviewed September 24, 2026. SKUs, wear schedules, package limits, and software features change by market; verify current doctor materials before you brief patients or sign a contract.

Clear aligners and packages

  • SureSmile® Full-Service Aligners — lab-fabricated sequential clear aligners from a Digital Lab proposal the doctor reviews and approves. Public brand copy positions the system for simple-to-complex malocclusion; 2026 materials add mixed and permanent dentition on one product/software platform (U.S. and Canada per trade coverage) with clinician-chosen 1- or 2-week wear intervals.
  • COMPLETE (Total Plan) — catalog flat-rate package: aligners with refinements throughout treatment for up to 3 years from aligner production, including Patient Kit, SureSmile® VPro™, and U.S. Whitening Kit (market availability varies). Ortho Index does not publish prices.
  • SELECT (À la carte) — pay for the number of aligners needed; includes Patient Kit and Whitening Kit per catalog; VPro optional for an additional fee. Framed for less complex cases.
  • SureSmile® In-Office Clear Aligner Design — same Ortho platform; doctor or Digital Lab stages, then print models in-office from STL export or order printed models from the lab.
  • SureSmile® Retainers — ordered by arch from a finished stage or a new scan; also listed for patients not treated with SureSmile. Catalog: designed to wear up to 12 months when cared for as directed; includes protective case; straight trimline.

Software, simulation, and digital workflow

  • SureSmile® Software — browser-based, clinician-controlled planning. Upload digital impressions, photos, and optional X-rays/CBCT; Digital Lab review on full-service cases. Open intake with Primescan and other commonly used scanners called out on the brand page.
  • SureSmile® Simulator (DS Core®) — 3D smile visualization before a prescribed plan. Requires DS Core subscription and compatible IOS (often Primescan; brand footnotes also mention 3Shape). Simulation is not a treatment plan; outcomes may differ; not in all markets.
  • Software UX updates — brand page highlights patient history overview, task list, search, and QR photo upload (e.g. release notes language around 7.6.26). Confirm live version with support.

Adjacent SureSmile appliances (same platform)

  • SureSmile® Robotically Bent Wires — custom Shape Memory Alloy wires from a 3D target setup; each wire inspected, tracked, shipped. This is DS’s remaining public wire SKU path after the traditional catalog exit — not a Sentalloy/BioForce shelf list.
  • SureSmile® IDB — digitally designed indirect-bonding trays (lab-printed or STL export). Placement toolset; doctor chooses a supported bracket set (including third-party “For IDB” libraries per doctor materials).
  • SureSmile® VPro™ — high-frequency vibration device, mouthpiece, and accessories. Bundled on COMPLETE; optional on SELECT. Comfort/acceleration language is vendor messaging.

Always verify territory rules, scanner/upload paths, package upgrade rules, refinement fees, mixed-dentition IFUs, and current SKU naming with Dentsply Sirona directly.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Dentsply Sirona backing — large global dental manufacturer with scanners (Primescan), imaging (Axeos), DS Core, Essix plastics, and education (DS Academy) adjacent to the aligner sale
  • One digital platform spanning aligners, custom wires, and IDB — useful if you want lab aligners and a digital fixed-appliance lane without bolting on a second vendor stack
  • Clear COMPLETE vs SELECT package split that is easier to staff-train than multi-tier step-cap ladders
  • Open scanner story plus deep Primescan / DS Core integration — not only a proprietary closed box
  • Long digital-ortho lineage since OraMetrix 1998 / SureSmile system (not a thin white-label reseller narrative); 2018 acquisition put it inside DS Orthodontics

Cons / watch-outs

  • Consumer brand recognition in many U.S. markets still trails Invisalign; case-acceptance conversations may need more doctor explanation
  • Marketing claims (“practically invisible,” refinement-rate percentages, predictable outcomes, VPro benefits) are vendor messaging. Brand-page “75% of cases required 1 or no refinements” cites an internal analysis of completed cases (Jan 2021–May 2025); catalog footnotes reference older data-on-file / JCO language — treat as manufacturer stats, not Ortho Index tests
  • Parent portfolio complexity — Byte DTC kits were suspended (2024) and not reinstated for new patients (2025 investor updates on the sister Orthodontics page). Keep SureSmile (doctor channel) and Byte (historic DTC) mentally separate when you diligence the company
  • Feature and kit availability (VPro, Whitening Kit, Simulator, mixed-dentition indication, wear-protocol software) varies by market; get the current quote sheet and IFU in writing
  • Traditional DS/GAC bracket catalogs are gone — if you expected a full twin/SL bracket line from this brand, that is the wrong shortlist

Warm and direct: SureSmile is a digital-ortho lab relationship and toolset, not a promise. Ortho Index does not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.

How it compares briefly

Against Invisalign (Align), SureSmile usually competes as the Dentsply Sirona / open-scanner alternative with a shorter public package ladder and optional custom-wire/IDB siblings. Against ClearCorrect (Straumann), both are corporate-backed full-service aligner labs; ClearCorrect leans Straumann/SIRIOS and a FLEX→unlimited package ladder, while SureSmile leans Primescan/DS Core and COMPLETE/SELECT plus wires/IDB. Against Spark (Ormco), SureSmile sits in a different corporate house (DS vs Envista/Ormco) with a stronger public “digital fixed + aligner” combo story. None of that ranks clinical superiority — match scanner stack, lab SLAs, refinements, and fee structure to your office.
