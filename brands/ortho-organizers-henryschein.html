Ortho Organizers

Historic full-line ortho manufacturer (Ortho Organizers, Inc.)—legal entity behind Henry Schein Orthodontics, still a catalog/account label for Maestro Mini-Twin and commodity SKUs ordered through Henry Schein Dental.

HQ: Savannah, Georgia, USA · Founded 1975 · Researched 2026-09-17 · Official site

Overview

Ortho Organizers is the historic specialty manufacturer and legal entity—not the current house marketing brand. The live public face of the business is Henry Schein Orthodontics (HSO) at henryscheinortho.com. On the 2026 FDA establishment registration, the firm is listed as Ortho Organizers, Inc. with business trade name Henry Schein Orthodontics, address 800 King George Blvd, Suite 100, Savannah, GA 31419, registration status Active.

Secondary deal databases (Mergr) date Ortho Organizers to 1975 and place the historic HQ at 1822 Aston Avenue, Carlsbad, California, with the old consumer site orthoorganizers.com. Henry Schein acquired the privately held company around the March 2009 close (deal coverage often cites a 2008 announcement window); 2008 sales were reported near $30 million, with roughly 200 employees. In March 2014, Orthodontic Products reported that Henry Schein unified Ortho Organizers, Masel, ClassOne, and Ortho Technology under the Henry Schein Orthodontics umbrella. Treat “founded 1975” as directory-sourced, not a live HSO About claim—HSO’s About page attributes 1932 to parent Henry Schein, Inc., not to the ortho unit.

Notable products

Ortho Organizers no longer markets itself as a separate website brand. What still carries the name—or still ships under the Ortho Organizers, Inc. manufacturer line—is mostly legacy catalog identity inside HSO / HSD. Confirm live SKUs, prescriptions, and HSD item numbers on henryscheindental.com or with your Schein ortho rep. Treatment-time and comfort claims below are vendor positioning where they appear on distributor copy.

Still visible under Ortho Organizers / OO manufacturer labels

  • Maestro® Mini-Twin® / Maestro low-profile twin brackets — metal twin line still sold on Henry Schein Dental with manufacturer shown as Ortho Organizers, Inc. (and sometimes Henry Schein Orthodontics) on item cards. Distributor copy describes MIM construction, low profile, contoured slot edges, 80-gauge mesh bases, and Roth / MBT-style prescription kits in .018 and .022. This is the clearest product line that still reads as Ortho Organizers on the cart—not Carriere SLX 3D and not Aria Expressions.
  • Ortho® Organizers branded storage / band cabinets and commodity accessories — HSD still lists Ortho Organizers–branded storage SKUs (example: band storage item cards). Treat these as catalog leftovers under the OO brand mark, not as a modern “innovation” franchise.
  • Broader twin / tubes / bands / elastics / instruments inventory — the historic Ortho Organizers full-line catalog was absorbed into HSO’s shop categories. HSO FAQs say the portfolio is expected to remain largely the same while redundant SKUs may be phased out over time. Do not assume every 2000s OO catalog name is still a homepage hero.

Historically associated, now told under HSO / Carriere

  • At acquisition, deal coverage highlighted Ortho Organizers’ Carriere LX self-ligating bracket as a growth product. Today’s Carriere story on henryscheinortho.com is Motion / Motion Pro, SLX 3D, and M-Series—marketed as the Carriere® System inside HSO, not as Ortho Organizers branding. See the Henry Schein Orthodontics page for those SKUs.
  • Smilers® aligners, Aria® twins, Froggymouth, and smartIPR are HSO / distribution portfolio items—not Ortho Organizers house brands.

Ordering path (2026)

  • U.S. direct ortho purchases—including Ortho Organizers–labeled parts—process through a Henry Schein Dental account after February 11, 2026. Search old HSO / Ortho Organizers part numbers on henryscheindental.com to map to HSD item numbers per HSO FAQs.
  • Specialty ortho support numbers on the FAQs still include Henry Schein Ortho 1-760-448-8600 / USASales@HenryScheinOrtho.com alongside Team Schein Dental routing.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Clear public paper trail: FDA listing (Ortho Organizers, Inc. / trade name HSO, Savannah), 2014 unification write-up, and 2009 acquisition coverage make the corporate story checkable
  • Still useful as a purchasing decoder—staff who grew up on OO catalogs can map Maestro and commodity SKUs without pretending Ortho Organizers is a separate competitor to HSO
  • Legal manufacturer continuity matters for registration, remittance, and “who made this bracket” questions even when the marketing site says Henry Schein Orthodontics
  • Maestro Mini-Twin remains a concrete twin option on HSD under the Ortho Organizers manufacturer line for offices that want a Schein-family twin without adopting Carriere SLX 3D

Cons / watch-outs

  • Not a current standalone brand. There is no Ortho Organizers homepage product grid competing with Ormco or American Orthodontics. Searching only “Ortho Organizers” will miss Carriere Motion, SLX 3D, Aria, and Smilers
  • Naming stack is easy to get wrong: Ortho Organizers ≠ Carriere ≠ Ortho Technology ≠ Henry Schein Dental. Invoices and boxes may mix all four
  • Founding year and Carlsbad HQ come from secondary directories / deal databases; live Contact/FDA point to Savannah. Do not hard-code Carlsbad as current HQ
  • Catalog churn risk: HSO has said redundant SKUs may be phased out. Legacy OO names can disappear from HSD even when the legal entity stays
  • This page is identity and catalog archaeology—not a clinical comparison of bracket philosophies. For Sagittal First / Motion / SLX workflows, read the HSO brand page

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

Bottom line / similar

Ortho Organizers is the roots and legal shell of Henry Schein’s ortho manufacturing business—still printed on FDA paper and some HSD item cards (notably Maestro Mini-Twin), no longer the face on the marketing homepage. If you need bite correctors, self-ligating heroes, or aligners from this family, you are shopping Henry Schein Orthodontics / Carriere / Smilers, not a separate Ortho Organizers storefront.
