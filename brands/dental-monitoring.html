DentalMonitoring

Paris-based AI remote-monitoring platform (Software as a Medical Device) for braces and aligners — ScanBox pro patient scans, doctor dashboard protocols, SmartSTL remote refinements, plus DM Engage and DM Insights practice tools.

HQ: Paris, France · Founded 2014 · Researched 2026-09-30 · Official site

Overview

DentalMonitoring (company legal name often Dental Monitoring SAS; product styled DentalMonitoring) is an AI-powered remote orthodontic monitoring platform. Patients capture guided intraoral photos at home with a smartphone and the company’s ScanBox pro cheek-retractor / scan accessory; the practice reviews AI-flagged observations and protocol-driven instructions on a web Dashboard. The live storefront is dentalmonitoring.com.

HQ and founding: Official About Us and FDA De Novo requester records place the company at 75 Rue de Tocqueville, 75017 Paris, France. About Us also lists a U.S. Americas office at 10801-1 North Mopac Expressway, Suite 320, Austin, TX 78759, plus offices in London, Sydney/Eveleigh, Hong Kong, and Tokyo. Public company materials consistently say founded in 2014 (About Us timeline describes founding as DentalMind before the DentalMonitoring platform launch). CEO named in company and trade materials is Philippe Salah. This brief sets hq to Paris, France and founded to 2014.

Regulatory framing (important): FDA granted De Novo classification DEN230035 on May 17, 2024, classifying DentalMonitoring as a Class II dental image analyzer under 21 CFR 872.1770, product code SBC. The classification order describes prescription software that analyzes optical camera-based intraoral images as an aid to diagnosis and/or monitoring of treatment progress — not for stand-alone clinical decision-making. Company home-page marketing says “FDA De Novo approved & MDR certified” and “Software as a Medical Device”; EU/other markets use CE / MDR language with the usual country-by-country availability disclaimer on every product footer. Ortho Index cites the FDA record; it does not restate every cleared parameter or claim that every 2026 UI module sits inside the 2024 order.

This page is a practice-facing software comparison brief for offices evaluating remote monitoring, not patient marketing, a clinical protocol, or medical advice. Case selection, scan review, and treatment changes stay with the treating doctor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists who want scheduled between-visit visibility on aligner fit, wire passivity, attachment/bracket status, and oral observations without bringing every patient in for a routine check
  • Practices running braces and/or clear aligners (vendor copy stresses appliance-agnostic monitoring across brands) and willing to train patients on ScanBox pro + patient app compliance
  • Offices that want protocol automation (custom or template protocols that trigger patient/team instructions when observations fire) and dashboard review from any device
  • Groups evaluating remote refinement / retainer STL workflows (SmartSTL) with partner aligner labs — public 2025 coverage highlights Ormco Spark Approver and Angel Aligner / iOrtho paths
  • Practices that also want adjacent tools for lead triage (DM Engage) and practice analytics (DM Insights), or PMS / imaging hooks called out in 2025 trade coverage (GreyFinch, Ortho2, Sensei Cloud, Dolphin, 3Shape, Medit — confirm live connectors for your stack)

It is a weaker shortlist if you will not invest in patient onboarding and staff dashboard time, if your case mix or payer/regulatory setting does not support remote photo monitoring, or if you only need an in-office CAD / print stack with no home-scan program. Confirm territory, license tiers, hardware kits, and cleared indications with DentalMonitoring sales — not from this page.

Core remote monitoring stack

  • DentalMonitoring (Dashboard + patient app + Data Analysis Platform) — HCP-facing web dashboard for patient cards, scan intervals, observation parameters, notifications, and team/patient instructions; patient mobile app for scan capture, messaging, and reminders. Product page markets detection/monitoring of 130+ oral observations, braces and aligners workflows (including Dynamic Aligner Change / go-to-next-aligner framing, archwire passivity alerts, activity graphs), multi-view photo comparison, and customizable protocols. Home page also cites weekly home scans tracking 60+ parameters, with a subset under FDA/MDR medical-device indications — verify the live cleared list in IFU / sales materials for your market.
  • ScanBox pro — proprietary lightweight scan accessory used with the patient’s smartphone to standardize intraoral photo capture from home (or office). About Us timeline credits an earlier DM ScanBox generation; current marketing centers on ScanBox pro.
  • ScanAssist — announced June 26, 2023 (PR Newswire): in-app AI-guided scan process with real-time guidance / feedback aimed at scan quality and patient engagement. Company framing called it the only AI-guided scan process available in orthodontics at launch — treat as vendor positioning.
  • DentalMonitoring+ — introduced at AAO 2025 (Orthodontic Products, Apr 24, 2025): next-generation AI tool adding dynamic 3D visualization, tooth-movement measurement, and real-time occlusion / movement views. Confirm what is shipping vs demo in your region.
  • AIDA Auto Replies — also debuted around AAO 2025: AI-suggested customizable automated replies inside the platform for staff/clinician communication efficiency (trade coverage).

SmartSTL and partner refine workflows

  • SmartSTL — optional platform feature: combine an initial intraoral scan (IOS or digitized impression), current stage/treatment file, and ongoing DentalMonitoring ScanBox scans to generate an updated, manufacture-oriented STL of current tooth positions so refinements or retainers can be requested without a new in-office scan (SmartSTL product page; Ormco Apr 25, 2025 PR for the Spark Approver path). Doctor still reviews and initiates lab requests in the partner software.
  • Ormco / Spark partnership (public 2025) — North America monitoring-fee discount framing for newly started Ormco cases (aligners and brackets, including Damon Ultima) when using DentalMonitoring, plus continued Spark Approver Smart STL refinement/retainer workflow (company PR). Discounts and eligibility are commercial terms — get the live partner sheet.
  • Angel Aligner / other integrations (trade 2025) — Orthodontic Products AAO coverage also named Angel Aligner SmartSTL / iOrtho remote-refinement paths, PMS links (GreyFinch, Ortho2, Sensei Cloud, Dolphin Imaging), and 3Shape / Medit direct scan upload. Confirm current certification matrix before you buy around an integration.

Adjacent platform modules

  • DM Engage — online assessment / lead module: prospective patients submit photos; AI-assisted reports (doctor-configurable) help gauge oral findings and treatment interest/readiness; optional HIPAA/GDPR-framed video consult path on the Engage page. Framed for conversion and triage, not as a substitute for in-person exam when clinically required.
  • DM Insights — practice analytics / performance statistics module (named on the home “Intelligent Platform” suite and in company terms summaries). Subscription feature — ask sales what metrics ship in your tier.
  • Vision / Loum (historical About Us timeline) — About Us mentions Vision (smile/appliance simulator using patient tooth images) and acquisition of Loum (“digital assistant”). Treat as company history; confirm whether each is still a live SKU on your quote.

FDA De Novo snapshot (U.S.)

  • DEN230035 — device name DentalMonitoring; requester Dental Monitoring SAS, Paris address matching About Us; decision granted 05/17/2024; regulation 872.1770; product code SBC; generic type dental image analyzer. Decision summary / order language: aid for HCPs monitoring orthodontic treatment progress; prescription device; indicated for patients over age 6 with results on permanent teeth; not OTC; not stand-alone clinical decision-making. Hardware elements referenced in review materials include DM App, cheek retractor / ScanBox-class capture hardware, Dashboard, and data analysis platform. A De Novo grant creates the classification pathway; it is not a blanket endorsement of every marketing claim or every later software module.

Always verify IFU scope, cleared parameters, pediatric limits, and country registration before clinical rollout.

Pros

  • Category-defining remote-monitoring story with a long public track record (2014 founding; ScanBox + app workflow widely discussed in trade press) and a real U.S. De Novo classification rather than marketing-only “AI” language
  • Appliance-agnostic positioning — braces and aligners, multi-brand — useful if your office is not locked to one lab’s proprietary monitor
  • Protocol + automation depth — configurable observations, patient/team instructions, Dynamic Aligner Change / wire-passivity style workflows give a concrete ops story beyond “patients text us photos”
  • SmartSTL + OEM partnerships (Spark Approver, Angel Aligner paths in 2025 coverage) can reduce refinement-scan chair time when the integration is live and staffed correctly
  • Broader platform (Engage for leads, Insights for analytics, PMS/scanner hooks) if you want one vendor across monitoring and growth tooling
  • Global footprint (Paris HQ, Austin U.S. hub, APAC/EU offices) matters for multi-location or relocating doctors comparing support SLAs

Cons / caveats

  • Patient compliance is the product — ScanBox kits, app coaching, and missed-scan chase still cost staff time; AI does not replace a non-compliant teenager
  • “First / only / leader” and outcome claims (FDA-only framing, observation counts, chair-time savings, faster finishes, conversion lifts) are vendor or partner marketing. FDA cleared a device type with special controls; it did not certify every dashboard widget or guarantee your KPIs
  • Aid, not autopilot — De Novo indications explicitly say results are an aid and not stand-alone for clinical decisions. Doctors remain responsible for review quality, false positives/negatives, and when to bring the patient in
  • Pediatric / dentition limits in U.S. order language (over age 6; permanent teeth reporting) may not match every early-treatment or mixed-dentition protocol you want to run remotely — read the live IFU
  • Cost and kit logistics — per-patient monitoring fees, ScanBox inventory, partner discount rules (e.g. Ormco 25% framing), and module add-ons (Engage, Insights, DentalMonitoring+, SmartSTL) need a current quote; Ortho Index does not list street prices
  • Integration matrix moves — PMS, scanner, and aligner-lab connectors in AAO booth coverage can lag or differ by region; pilot one workflow end-to-end before you promise remote refinements to every start
  • Crowded “remote care” field — aligner brands’ own portals, other teledentistry apps, and in-house photo protocols compete on price and simplicity; DentalMonitoring fit depends on your volume, staff bandwidth, and whether SmartSTL/partner discounts matter

Warm and direct: DentalMonitoring is a doctor-supervised remote scan and protocol platform, not a promise of fewer visits or better finishes for every case. Ortho Index does not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.

Bottom line for practices

Shortlist DentalMonitoring when you want a mature AI remote-monitoring SaMD with ScanBox-guided home scans, configurable protocols for braces and aligners, and optional SmartSTL refine paths into partner labs — especially if Ormco Spark, Angel Aligner, or named PMS/scanner integrations are already on your floor. Diligence the live quote (monitoring fees, kits, Engage/Insights/+ modules), IFU/cleared parameters for your country, staff dashboard workflow, and a real patient-compliance plan. Confirm products, contracts, and clinical protocols with DentalMonitoring before you buy or brief patients.
