orthocaps®

Doctor-prescribed clear aligner system from Ortho Caps GmbH (Hamm, Germany), built around the TwinAligner® approach of separate day and night aligners in elastic thermoplastics, plus hybrid aligner treatment (HAT), early-treatment appliances, and a cloud planning portal.

HQ: Hamm, Germany · Founded 2006 · Researched 2026-10-16 · Official site

Overview

orthocaps® is a clear aligner system made by Ortho Caps GmbH, a privately run company in Hamm, Germany. Its official site says the company was founded in 2006 by orthodontist Dr. Wajeeh Khan, who still leads it; the legal notice lists Dr. Wajeeh Khan and Jocelyne Khan as management. orthocaps.de (German) and orthocaps.com (English) are the same site in two languages, and orthocaps.net is the practitioner portal.

The signature idea is the TwinAligner® system. For each treatment step, the company makes two aligners from elastic thermoplastics: a thinner DAYcaps aligner and a firmer NIGHTcaps aligner. Ortho Caps says this lets it tune force levels by material thickness and calls itself the first aligner system made of soft plastics. Those are the manufacturer’s claims, not independent findings.

orthocaps is doctor-prescribed. Practitioners get certified through a course, submit cases through the portal, and receive a 3D plan to review and edit before aligners are made. We found no direct-to-consumer ordering on the official site. Pricing for practices isn’t published.

Regulatory status: The site links an EN ISO 13485:2016 certificate (the linked copy is dated valid until October 2025). In the U.S., the FDA database lists 510(k) K180241 for “Orthocaps Twinaligner,” cleared September 28, 2018, with Ortho Caps GmbH as applicant.

U.S. availability: In 2018, Rocky Mountain Orthodontics (RMO) of Denver announced it was the exclusive U.S. and Canadian distributor. The current orthocaps site doesn’t mention the U.S. or name a U.S. partner, and RMO’s site no longer has an orthocaps page we could find, so check current U.S. availability with Ortho Caps or RMO before planning on it.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and dentists who want an aligner lab with distinct product tiers, from minor anterior cases to complex ones
  • Practices interested in combining aligners with fixed appliances (HAT) or Herbst-type mechanics
  • Offices that treat children in mixed dentition and want night-wear aligner or myofunctional options
  • Clinicians who prefer reviewing and editing setups themselves in a cloud portal
  • Practices in Europe most of all, where the company runs its courses (Hamm, Paris, Zürich are on the current schedule)

Notable products

  • orthocaps® PRO: the main system for moderate to complex cases. The company says it can correct up to 90% of malalignments.
  • orthocaps® NOCTIS: worn at night (12 hours) on weekdays and also during the day on weekends, aimed at people who talk a lot for work.
  • orthocaps® KIDS: night-wear aligners for mixed dentition, listed for ages 9 to 11.
  • orthocaps® TEN and TEN+TEN: lower-cost options with up to 10 aligner sets per arch for minor anterior alignment.
  • orthocaps® HAT (Hybrid Aligner Treatment): aligners combined with indirect-bonded brackets, pre-bent wires, QuadHelix, and lingual arches, for cases the company says go beyond aligners alone.
  • orthocaps® Rainmaker: a patented Herbst-type appliance used together with aligners.
  • BiteMaintainer: a dental-silicone positioner for finishing or retention.
  • BiteGym®: a custom myofunctional appliance for early treatment, ages 5 to 11.
  • Retainers: Clear Retainers (three per arch), a gold-wire Lingual Retainer, and CERATAIN® Flex.
  • iSetup® and TER: a cloud-based 3D planning tool in the portal (with DICOM, face-scan, and X-ray integration and Bolton/space analyses), plus a 3D Treatment Evaluation Report at the end of each phase.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • A genuinely different design (day and night aligners per step) if you want an alternative to single-tray systems
  • Wide range under one roof: tiers for minor to complex cases, hybrid fixed/aligner treatment, Herbst-type and early-treatment appliances, and retainers
  • Clinician control: you review and edit the setup in iSetup® before anything is made
  • Accepts open STL scans as well as silicone impressions
  • Built-in phase checks (TER), which help catch wear problems before the next phase
  • FDA 510(k) clearance on record for the TwinAligner

Cons

  • U.S. support is unclear. The 2018 RMO distribution deal isn’t reflected on the current official site.
  • No public pricing for practices
  • Certification is required first, and courses are listed in Europe
  • Two aligners per step means more for patients to keep track of and switch between
  • Material and outcome claims (like “up to 90%” or “as effective as fixed braces”) are the company’s own, without independent data on the site
  • Some site copy is patient-facing and promotional, and the English is uneven in places

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

After certification, you get a portal account at orthocaps.net. Upload photos for a free case assessment (the company says within 48 hours), then send impressions or STL scans, a panoramic X-ray, and a lateral ceph if available. Ortho Caps builds a 3D plan, you adjust and approve it in iSetup®, and the first phase of aligners arrives in two to three weeks. Each phase is typically about ten numbered sets of DAYcaps and NIGHTcaps, followed by a TER review before the next phase. If you’re in the U.S., confirm who supplies and supports orthocaps locally before you sign up.
