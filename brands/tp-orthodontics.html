TP Orthodontics

U.S. specialty manufacturer (TP Orthodontics, Inc.) in La Porte, Indiana since 1942—known for Tip-Edge PLUS / PLUS SL brackets, InVu aesthetics with Personalized Color-Matching Technology, and the original Tooth Positioner.

HQ: La Porte, Indiana, USA · Founded 1942 · Researched 2026-09-16 · Official site

Overview

TP Orthodontics (often styled TPO®) is a specialty orthodontic manufacturer headquartered in La Porte, Indiana, USA. The live corporate site is tportho.com. Worldwide headquarters address on current product literature: 100 Center Plaza, La Porte, IN 46350-9672; U.S. phone 800-348-8856; info@tportho.com.

Official About copy dates the company to 1942 and positions TPO as a long-running manufacturer of brackets, aesthetic systems, finishing appliances, and related specialty products. Product pages credit founder Dr. H.D. Kesling with inventing the original Tooth Positioner. The company remains known in specialty circles for the Tip-Edge® differential-tooth-movement platform and for ceramic aesthetics marketed with Personalized Color-Matching Technology®.

About materials also describe a worldwide quality management system for medical devices and branch offices in the United States, Europe, Australia, South Africa, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and China, plus distributor networks elsewhere. Treat “most experienced,” “unmatched quality,” and similar lines as manufacturer positioning.

For Ortho Index, the chairside core is Tip-Edge PLUS / PLUS SL brackets, InVu and Tip-Edge aesthetic ceramics, and custom Tooth Positioner finishing—not a turnkey clear-aligner ecosystem brand.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists evaluating or already trained in Tip-Edge differential mechanics (light-force tipping/retraction with a dynamic slot story) rather than only twin straight-wire or conventional SL twins
  • Offices comparing self-ligating Tip-Edge (PLUS SL, cobalt-chromium per TPO literature) against stainless Tip-Edge PLUS and aesthetic Tip-Edge kits
  • Practices that want ceramic aesthetics with TPO’s color-matching positioning (InVu, Tip-Edge PLUS Aesthetic)
  • Teams that still use or want to evaluate a custom Tooth Positioner / Mini-Positioner for finishing and retention—including finishing after aligner stages, per TPO’s product page
  • Multi-country specialty offices that can order through TPO’s branch/distributor network rather than a single mega-vendor aligner stack

Notable products

Names below match live tportho.com product pages and current Tip-Edge / PLUS SL literature as researched September 2026. Slot sizes, prescriptions, kit codes, and country availability belong on the current catalog or with your TPO rep—not on this page. Force ranges, visit counts, and “proven results” language are vendor claims.

Tip-Edge bracket family

  • Tip-Edge® PLUS Stainless Steel — metal Tip-Edge PLUS brackets; product page describes a patented programmed-treatment design, a “deep tunnel” horizontal archwire slot for uprighting/torquing with a small round superelastic auxiliary, light forces, and low anchorage strain relative to conventional edgewise marketing. Available with oriented / non-oriented options and patient kits per metal-bracket literature.
  • PLUS SL® (Tip-Edge PLUS Self-Ligating) — self-ligating Tip-Edge platform; doctor brochure describes cobalt-chromium (CoCr) construction, low nickel content positioning for nickel-sensitive patients, passive self-ligation on Tip-Edge mechanics, and the same dynamic slot / deep-tunnel story as Tip-Edge PLUS. Confirm current SKU naming (PLUS SL vs Tip-Edge PLUS SL) on the live metal-bracket product page before stocking.
  • Tip-Edge® PLUS Aesthetic — ceramic Tip-Edge PLUS brackets; marketed with Personalized Color-Matching Technology and the same Tip-Edge programmed-treatment narrative as the stainless line. Catalog materials also show mixed ceramic-anterior / metal-posterior kits.
  • Tip-Edge PLUS with pre-applied adhesive — brochure listings describe brackets pre-coated with eXact® orthodontic adhesive (ready-to-place positioning); confirm live availability by market.

Other aesthetic / finishing

  • InVu® Aesthetic Brackets — ceramic aesthetic line separate from Tip-Edge; product page emphasizes strength/bonding/debonding design (molded polymer mesh base called out) and the same Personalized Color-Matching Technology story (brackets engineered to blend with surrounding enamel optically).
  • Tooth Positioner / Mini-Positioner — custom lab finishing appliances invented by Dr. H.D. Kesling; constructed over an individualized set-up. Product page lists Original Tooth Positioner and Mini-Positioner, optional precision seating springs, molded airways, and finishing/retention use cases (including aligner-adjacent finishing language). Custom lab path—order through TPO’s lab process, not as a shelf SKU alone.

Beyond the core formulary

  • TPO’s site and catalogs also cover broader specialty inventory (tubes, wires, auxiliaries, custom lab appliances). Confirm named SKUs on the live Product Catalog / Lab Catalog PDFs linked from tportho.com rather than assuming a full twin-bracket mega-catalog from Tip-Edge marketing alone.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Clear specialty identity around Tip-Edge—a differential-tooth-movement bracket philosophy with metal, aesthetic, and self-ligating (PLUS SL) variants documented on official product pages
  • Long, dated company story (1942 on About; Tooth Positioner credited to founder H.D. Kesling) without needing third-party rename archaeology
  • Aesthetic lane is real: InVu plus Tip-Edge PLUS Aesthetic, both tied to Personalized Color-Matching Technology on TPO pages
  • Tooth Positioner remains a distinctive custom finishing/retention offering many bracket-first vendors do not emphasize
  • Published HQ city (La Porte, IN), street address on literature, phone, and global branch footprint make the commercial path checkable

Cons / watch-outs

  • Tip-Edge is a technique system, not a drop-in twin swap. Offices without Tip-Edge education will underuse the deep tunnel / auxiliary-wire workflow or stock kits they cannot finish confidently
  • Marketing leans hard on fewer appointments, lighter forces, “proven results,” and ABO-grade finishing language—treat as vendor positioning; verify against IFUs and your own outcomes
  • Catalog naming leftovers (Tip-Edge vs Tip-Edge PLUS vs PLUS SL; InVu vs Tip-Edge Aesthetic; stainless vs CoCr; oriented vs non-oriented kits) can trip purchasing—shop the live product page and current PDF catalog
  • Not a full clear-aligner platform company; Positioner finishing can complement aligners, but TPO is not selling a Spark/Invisalign-class aligner ecosystem here
  • Country availability of PLUS SL, pre-applied adhesive kits, and custom lab turnaround varies—confirm with TPO or your regional branch before promising a U.S. flyer SKU abroad

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.
