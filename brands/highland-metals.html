Highland Metals

Value-oriented U.S. archwire brand (HMI / Highland Metals Inc.)—SuperElastic and Heat-Activated NiTi, stainless steel, nickel-free Beta Titanium Molybdenum, plus brackets, tubes, springs, and elastomerics—sold direct from Franklin, Indiana, as a live catalog brand that is also a dba of G&H Wire Company.

HQ: Franklin, Indiana, USA · Researched 2026-09-01 · Official site

Overview

Highland Metals (also Highland Metals Inc., HMI™, tagline Simply Great Wire!™) is a U.S. orthodontic brand best known for archwires sold in bulk packs at volume discounts. The live storefront is highlandmetals.com. Catalog and site footers put mailing/shipping at 40 Linville Way, Franklin, IN 46131 USA—the same plant address used by G&H Orthodontics.

Relationship to G&H (verified, not invented): Highland Metals is not a dead legacy label. It still has its own public site, shop cart, Quick Reorder tools, trade-show booth notes, and a 2nd Edition 2026 product catalog. At the same time it is not an independent legal manufacturer. BSI ISO 13485 certificate MD 635659 (and MDSAP 695170), hosted on ghorthodontics.com, registers:

G&H Wire Company, Inc. dba Highland Metals Inc., Flexmedics, OrthoClub, G&H Orthodontics, Orthodontic Design & Production at 40 Linville Way, Franklin, Indiana 46131.

Highland Metals’ own Regulatory Compliance page links the same two certificate numbers. Catalog trademark/copyright language states that HIGHLAND METALS™, HMI™, the Highland Metals Incorporated logo, SIMPLY GREAT WIRE!™, and BETA BLUE™ are property of G&H® Wire Company, with copyright lines reading © … G&H® Orthodontics. So the honest stack is:

  • Legal manufacturer / QMS holder: G&H Wire Company, Inc.
  • Public commercial brand A: G&H Orthodontics (full clinical portfolio on ghorthodontics.com)
  • Public commercial brand B: Highland Metals / HMI (value / bulk-wire–centered catalog on highlandmetals.com)

Treat Highland Metals as a live sister brand / dba channel under one Franklin manufacturer, not as a competitor factory and not as a retired OEM name with no storefront. Do not write “Highland Metals was acquired and shut down” from the ISO dba line alone—the site and 2026 catalog contradict that.

Founding year: About/press boilerplate uses decade marketing (“over 28 years” in 2016, “over 29 years” in 2017, “over 30 years” on the current catalog back page). That points to a late-1980s start if you back-calculate, but no calendar founding year is printed on the live About page. This directory page omits founded rather than inventing one. Older LinkedIn scrapers still say San Jose; current remit paper is Franklin. The 408 international phone on the Highland site is a leftover Bay Area area code, not proof of a second plant.

Made-in-USA positioning: About and catalog repeatedly claim U.S. manufacture and BSI ISO 13485:2016 certification. CE / customs language appears on About; confirm current EU labeling against the live regulatory PDFs before assuming MDR vs MDD status for a specific SKU.

Who it’s for

  • Offices that want a direct, bulk-discount wire lane (site advertises tiered savings at order thresholds such as $500 / $1,500 / $2,500—confirm live terms before budgeting)
  • Teams shopping SuperElastic NiTi, Heat-Activated NiTi, stainless steel, and nickel-free Beta Titanium Molybdenum under one Highland catalog rather than mixing anonymous private-label packs
  • Practices that already use G&H Orthodontics and need to know Highland is the same legal manufacturer’s second brand, with different phones, SKUs, pack sizes, and pricing story
  • Buyers who still want mini twins, cast buccal tubes, NiTi springs, S-Tie ligatures, chain, and intraoral elastics from the same HMI cart as the wires—without expecting G&H’s named alloy map (G4 / M5 / TitanMoly, etc.)
  • International accounts comfortable ordering FOB Franklin with Highland’s own international phone / payment terms (catalog: payment in advance common for international)

Notable products

Names below match the live highlandmetals.com navigation and the web/PDF catalog researched September 2026. Pack counts, sizes, archforms, and prices change—confirm on the current shop or catalog before writing a PO. Marketing phrases (“simply great,” “maximum patient comfort,” “superior…”) are vendor voice, not clinical evidence.

Archwires (core of the brand)

  • SuperElastic NiTi arches — best-seller positioning; light continuous-force copy; large multipacks (catalog shows 100 arches/pack for many sizes); natural and progressive archforms; reverse-curve and lingual options; NiTi also on spool
  • Heat-Activated NiTi arches — soft/pliable at room temperature per product copy; copper-free claim on catalog; reverse-curve options; 100 arches/pack for many listings
  • Stainless steel arches — 304V VAR stainless per catalog; regular SS, high-tensile SS (vendor: ~8–10% more force than regular SS), 8-strand braided SS for early leveling / finishing, lingual arches, and 14″ straight lengths
  • Beta Titanium Molybdenum — nickel-free beta TiMo line launched November 2016 to replace Beta Blue™; force positioning between NiTi and SS; arches (often 25/pack), lingual, and straight lengths
  • Tooth-colored (epoxy-coated) SuperElastic NiTi and stainless arches — still promoted on the live home page; 2016 launch copy noted coating adds about .002″ to labeled dimension (e.g. labeled .016″ coats a .014″ base)—verify current IFU before assuming coated size = base size

Archform notes on the catalog caution that Highland shapes are not endorsed copies of MBT / Roth / Damon systems (Damon® is an Ormco mark; Highland disclaims affiliation). Midlines: ink on NiTi; permanent electro-etch on SS and beta.

Brackets & tubes (secondary to wires, but on the live menu)

  • Highland Bracket System — mini twin family; catalog: 17-4 stainless, 80-gauge indented mesh pad, torque-in-base, center scribe, .018 / .022; kits of 200 and per-tooth packs; bondable / weldable options called out for tubes
  • Site nav also lists cast and MIM twins plus an MIM SL lane—confirm which constructions are still orderable on the live product pages before standardizing a bracket formulary on Highland alone
  • Cast buccal tubes — miniaturized cast 316 SS positioning; funneled entrance, malleable ball hook, torque-in-base, bondable or weldable

Springs & wire auxiliaries

  • NiTi open coil, stop-wound, and closed-coil eyelet springs (plus closed-coil on spool)
  • Ligature wire, pre-formed / pre-twist ligatures, Kobayashi hooks, round retainer wire, ball clasps and related auxiliaries listed under Wire Auxiliaries

Elastomerics

  • S-Tie elastomeric ligature ties — latex-free; catalog 25 colors; S-shaped sticks holding 100 ties/unit
  • Elastomeric chain — clear or gray; short / long / continuous spacing
  • Intraoral elastics — surgical-grade natural latex; amber; medium/heavy force bands in common diameters; patient multipacks

Highland is not an aligner platform. Do not list clear-aligner trays under this brand.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Still a real public catalog brand in 2026—not a certificate-only dba with a parked domain
  • Clear value / bulk story: large NiTi and SS pack sizes plus published percentage discounts on order totals
  • Alloy coverage that matters for fixed treatment: SE NiTi, heat-activated NiTi, SS (including high-tensile and braided), beta TiMo, coated aesthetic wires
  • Same Franklin ISO / MDSAP paper as G&H Wire Company, with certificates linked from both brands’ regulatory pages
  • Direct ordering (web, email orders@highlandmetals.com, US/Canada 800-368-6484) without forcing every wire PO through the G&H Orthodontics shop

Cons / watch-outs

  • Two brands, one manufacturer. Formulary confusion is real: Highland’s SE NiTi / Beta TiMo names are not the same SKU language as G&H’s G4™ / M5™ / TitanMoly™. Do not assume cross-compatibility of force curves or pack part numbers without checking both catalogs
  • Bracket/tube depth is thinner and less named than G&H’s miniPrevail / Agility / Auralis map; Highland’s strength is wires first
  • Founding-year and “over N years” copy drifts; San Jose / 408 phone leftovers can confuse AP and shipping clerks who expect only Indiana contacts
  • Catalog PDF copyright still shows © 2022 G&H Orthodontics on pages fetched in 2026 while the cover calls itself 2nd Edition 2026—treat prices and SKUs as needing a live-site confirm
  • Volume-discount math, restock fees (45-day return window per catalog terms), and international prepaid terms belong in purchasing policy—not assumed free
  • Coated-wire dimension vs force notes from the 2016 launch are easy to miss; train assistants before substituting coated for bare sizes
  • “Simply great,” comfort, and efficiency slogans are marketing. Force and material claims live on the IFU / data sheet for the lot you received

Buying notes

  • Start at highlandmetals.com → Wires hubs, View Catalog / PDF, Quick Reorder. This page is informational only; do not email the brand from Ortho Index.
  • US & Canada: 800-368-6484; international: +1-408-271-2955; fax 800-448-7971; orders@highlandmetals.com. Mailing: 40 Linville Way, Franklin, IN 46131.
  • Legal manufacturer on BSI paper: G&H Wire Company, Inc. Commercial brand for this cart: Highland Metals / HMI. Sister commercial brand for the fuller clinical portfolio: G&H Orthodontics at ghorthodontics.com (different phone: 1-800-526-1026).
  • If your office already buys G&H, decide deliberately whether Highland is a bulk wire second source or a duplicate spend lane—same plant story, different brand packaging and discount rules.
  • Confirm archform (natural / progressive / midsize / youth / lingual), alloy, round vs rectangular, pack count, and whether you need coated vs bare before reorder.
  • Regulatory PDFs: Highland’s quality page and G&H’s quality-regulatory hub both point at MD 635659 and MDSAP 695170. Prefer those live PDFs over screenshots in a vendor binder.
  • Product availability and CE / customs markings vary by market. Returns need an RA number per published terms.

Bottom line

Highland Metals is a still-active Franklin wire brand—own site, own 2026 catalog, own bulk-discount pitch—whose legal and quality home is G&H Wire Company, Inc. (dba Highland Metals Inc. on BSI ISO 13485 MD 635659). Shop it for affordable multipack archwires (SE NiTi, heat-activated NiTi, SS, beta TiMo, coated aesthetics) plus a lighter bracket/tube/elastomeric add-on set. Do not confuse it with a shuttered OEM name, and do not treat its SKUs as drop-in synonyms for G&H Orthodontics’ named G4 / M5 / TitanMoly lines without checking both catalogs.
