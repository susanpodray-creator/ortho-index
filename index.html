Adenta

Family-run German bracket and wire maker (Adenta GmbH, Gilching, near Munich) that dates its roots to 1949. Its live web shop lists CNC-milled metal twins (CROWN, ECONOLINE), aesthetic brackets (CLEAR sapphire, DISCREET, BREEZE SL self-ligating), and the TRIAMOND Wedgewise bracket. Older Evolution SLT and JOY lingual lines are not in the current shop, and the U.S. office site is a placeholder.
brackets USA

Allure Ortho

Orthodontic supply brand focused on brackets, wires, and practice-ready catalog items for specialty offices.
brackets Sheboygan, Wisconsin, USA

American Orthodontics

Specialty orthodontic manufacturer offering brackets, wires, instruments, and practice support for orthodontists worldwide.
aligners Shanghai, China

Angelalign (Angel Aligner)

Global clear-aligner platform from Angelalign Technology Inc. (HKEX 6699), with doctor-facing Angel Aligner products, iOrtho planning software, and growing North American availability.

Boyd Industries

Clearwater, Florida maker of specialty operatory equipment since 1957—orthodontic and pediatric treatment chairs, chairside and rear delivery units, LED exam lights, operator stools, monitor mounts, and custom clinical, sterilization, and 3D-printing cabinetry, built largely in-house in the USA.
other Carrollton, Texas, USA

Brius Technologies

Carrollton, Texas maker of the Brava / Brava+ Independent Movers system — custom lingual NiTi arms on a shared anchorage base that program simultaneous, per-tooth movement without a continuous archwire.
imaging Atlanta, Georgia, USA

Carestream Dental

Dental imaging hardware and connected software spanning CS 9600 and CS 8200 3D CBCT, CS 8100 Evo panoramic/cephalometric imaging, RVG sensors, intraoral X-ray systems, and CS Imaging 8—with Sensei handling the company’s practice-management portfolio.
aligners Round Rock, Texas, USA (Straumann Group; Basel, Switzerland)

ClearCorrect (Straumann)

Straumann Group clear-aligner system from Round Rock, Texas — ClearQuartz material, ClearPilot planning, Doctor Portal, and tiered case packages for GP and ortho practices.
other Newberg, Oregon, USA

DCI Equipment (DCI Edge)

Newberg, Oregon dental parts and operatory equipment maker founded in 1983, now part of NSK. It sells DCI Edge chairs, delivery systems, LED operatory lights, stools, and cabinetry, including a small orthodontic line of chairs and ortho carts, through authorized dealers. DCI-branded replacement parts are sold separately.
software Paris, France

DentalMonitoring

Paris-based AI remote-monitoring platform (Software as a Medical Device) for braces and aligners — ScanBox pro patient scans, doctor dashboard protocols, SmartSTL remote refinements, plus DM Engage and DM Insights practice tools.
brackets Ispringen, Germany

Dentaurum

German specialty manufacturer (DENTAURUM GmbH & Co. KG) of brackets, wires, expansion screws, acrylics, TADs, and lab products—discovery, equilibrium, rematitan, Orthocryl, tomas—plus implant and digital lines, based in Ispringen since 1886 roots.
brackets Sarasota, Florida, USA

Dentsply Sirona Orthodontics

Specialty orthodontics unit of Dentsply Sirona (Nasdaq: XRAY). After the 2020 exit from traditional GAC brackets, bands, tubes, and wires, the public catalog is SureSmile digital appliances plus Essix plastics and IPR—not a GAC or SureSmile rename.
imaging Quakertown, Pennsylvania, USA

DEXIS

Envista’s dental imaging brand (Dental Imaging Technologies Corporation, Quakertown, PA) covering OP 3D and i-CAT CBCT, DEXIS sensors, NOMAD and FOCUS intraoral X-ray, Imprevo/IS scanners, and DTX Studio Clinic software under one connected ecosystem.
software Chatsworth, California, USA

Dolphin Imaging & Management Solutions

Patterson-owned orthodontic software maker (Chatsworth, CA) behind Dolphin Imaging, Ceph Tracing, Dolphin 3D, Dolphin Management, and the browser-based Dolphin Blue suite.
supplies Lake St. Louis, Missouri, USA

DynaFlex

Privately held U.S. orthodontic supplier, full-service appliance laboratory, clear aligner manufacturer, and dental sleep device maker (Ortho Solutions, LC d/b/a DynaFlex) in Lake St. Louis, Missouri, founded in 1965.
