Dolphin Imaging & Management Solutions

Patterson-owned orthodontic software maker (Chatsworth, CA) behind Dolphin Imaging, Ceph Tracing, Dolphin 3D, Dolphin Management, and the browser-based Dolphin Blue suite.

HQ: Chatsworth, California, USA · Researched 2026-10-12 · Official site

Overview

Dolphin Imaging and Management Solutions makes software built specifically for orthodontic, oral and maxillofacial surgery, and pediatric dental practices. Its site lists a Chatsworth, California address (9200 Oakdale Ave.), and the site footer carries a Patterson Dental Holdings, Inc. copyright. In practice, Dolphin is a Patterson software brand, and Patterson branches handle sales and support in the U.S. and Canada.

Patterson Companies itself went private when Patient Square Capital completed its acquisition on April 17, 2025. The Dolphin name and product lines have not been renamed.

Patterson describes Dolphin as having more than 10,000 customers worldwide and over 30 years in the market. This page leaves out a founding year because no official page we checked gives an exact one.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic practices that want cephalometric tracing, superimpositions, and image-based case presentation in one place.
  • Practices and residency programs that work with CBCT and want 3D analysis tools. Patterson says Ceph Tracing is used in most North American orthodontic and oral surgery training programs.
  • Large or multi-location orthodontic groups looking at a dedicated ortho practice-management system.
  • Offices that would rather use a hosted, browser-based setup than an on-site server (Dolphin Blue).

Notable products

  • Dolphin Imaging / Imaging Plus: captures, organizes, and presents patient images, with optional modules.
  • Ceph Tracing: cephalometric analysis and progress superimpositions.
  • Treatment Simulation: visual treatment planning and case presentation.
  • Dolphin 3D: works with CBCT, medical CT, MRI, and 3D facial camera data, and measures in both 3D and 2D.
  • Dolphin Management: orthodontic practice management covering scheduling, treatment cards, billing, claims, and reports. It is aimed at high-volume and multi-location practices.
  • Dolphin Blue: a browser-based subscription that bundles Blue Practice, Blue Imaging, the Aquarium patient-education library, support, eClaims, text messaging, and AnywhereDolphin. It is hosted on Microsoft cloud infrastructure.
  • Dolphin Aquarium: a library of 3D patient-education animations.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Long track record in orthodontic and surgical imaging, and widely taught in training programs, so many clinicians and staff already know it.
  • Imaging, 3D, practice management, and patient education come from one vendor.
  • Dolphin 3D supports data from many CBCT and 3D camera brands, not just one hardware maker.
  • Dolphin Blue gives you a hosted option with automatic backups and updates and no on-site server.

Cons

  • No public pricing. Blue is quoted per practice as a monthly subscription, and the classic products are sold module by module.
  • The product lineup is easy to confuse: classic Imaging and Management, cloud-hosted classic, and Blue are separate offerings. Get the exact modules and terms in writing.
  • Much of the public website is thin or out of date, so you’ll probably need a sales conversation to confirm current features.
  • Blue depends on your internet connection. Dolphin recommends 50/5 Mbps or faster plus a backup connection.
  • The parent company changed ownership in 2025. Watch roadmap and support policies over time.
