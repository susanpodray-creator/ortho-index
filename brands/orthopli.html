OrthoPli

Philadelphia-based manufacturer of orthodontic pliers, cutters, Mathieus, and specialty instruments; family-owned since 1973.

HQ: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA · Founded 1973 · Researched 2026-08-23 · Official site

Overview

OrthoPli (Orthopli Corporation) is a U.S. manufacturer and distributor of orthodontic hand instruments. The company describes itself as family-owned and operated since 1973, with three generations of family members still involved. Public pages list headquarters contact details in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (10061 Sandmeyer Lane). Instruments are positioned around surgical-grade stainless steel forgings, hardened/tool-steel inserts, and skilled instrument making rather than as a bracket, wire-systems, or clear-aligner brand.

Orthodontic offices most often meet OrthoPli through everyday chairside tools—ligature and distal-end cutters, Mathieu ligating pliers, bird-beak and light-wire formers, Weingarts, and specialty loop/stop instruments—plus an in-house instrument repair pathway. The catalog also lists related chairside and lab accessories and some consumables (wires, elastics, brackets), but this page treats OrthoPli primarily as an instruments vendor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontic practices stocking pliers, cutters, and ligating instruments for daily chairside use
  • Offices that want multiple handle lengths and tip options on core tools (standard, small, medium, long/slim)
  • Teams comparing flush vs. shear distal-end cutters, carbide vs. non-inserted Mathieus, and bird-beak/light-wire formers
  • Labs and clinics that need forming, banding, debonding, lingual, or aligner/dimple instruments under one catalog
  • Purchasing leads evaluating manufacturer repair/retipping support alongside new instrument purchases

Notable products

Official shop categories and SKUs change over time; names below match current Orthopli collection and catalog listings:

  • Ligature cutters — Pin and ligature cutters in multiple handle and tip configurations (e.g., #020 family, angled #024, hard-wire variants); marketed with hardened inserted beaks and diamond-honed cutting edges
  • Distal end cutters — Flush cut-and-hold (#026 family) and shear cut-and-hold (#026-H family) styles; Orthopli states founder William Tippy Sr. invented and patented the flush distal-end cutter; multiple handle lengths and a non-holding flush option
  • Mathieus & ligating instruments — Mathieu ligating pliers (standard, thin, carbide-inserted, double-spring, Boynton, interlock), elastic placing hemostats, and related ligature-tying tools
  • Bird beaks & forming instruments — Light-wire formers and bird-beak bending pliers (with/without cutters or arch formers), Adams, Tweed loop formers, Jarabak, optical, and related wire-forming tools
  • Specialty instruments — Loop and stop formers such as the Four Step Automatic Loop Forming Plier (#053), Loop Tie Back Plier (#049), Nance loop forming/closing plier (#054), cinch-back and crimpable-hook placers (legacy specialty catalog)
  • Other instrument lines on the official category list — Weingart utility pliers, torquing, banding, debonding, Tweed, lingual, LAP joint, lab instruments, aligner & dimple instruments, tweezers, measuring devices, and instrument care/repair service

Always verify the specific instrument’s intended use, wire limits, and care instructions on the current Orthopli product page before purchase or clinical use.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Deep orthodontic-instruments focus with broad cutter, Mathieu, bird-beak, and specialty plier coverage
  • Long family ownership story (official site: since 1973) and U.S. manufacturing positioning around surgical stainless steel and inserted tips
  • Practical SKU variety—handle lengths and tip options that let doctors and assistants match grip preference
  • Manufacturer-facing repair/retipping pathway listed alongside new instruments
  • Distal-end cutter lineage called out on the official distal-end collection page (flush cutter attributed to the founder)

Cons

  • Not a full appliance systems company—brackets, archwire systems, and aligners are not OrthoPli’s primary identity even if some related SKUs appear in the shop
  • Website spans a modern Shopify storefront and older HTML catalog pages; cross-checking SKU names and specs is worthwhile
  • Large catalog depth means practices still need a short preferred list to avoid mixed tip/handle inventories
  • Independent clinical performance comparisons are limited on the manufacturer site; treat durability and cut-quality claims as vendor positioning and confirm against your own IFU/use criteria

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.
