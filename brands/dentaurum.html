Dentaurum

German specialty manufacturer (DENTAURUM GmbH & Co. KG) of brackets, wires, expansion screws, acrylics, TADs, and lab products—discovery, equilibrium, rematitan, Orthocryl, tomas—plus implant and digital lines, based in Ispringen since 1886 roots.

HQ: Ispringen, Germany · Founded 1886 · Researched 2026-09-14 · Official site

Overview

Dentaurum is the public brand of DENTAURUM GmbH & Co. KG, a family-owned German dental manufacturer headquartered at Turnstr. 31, 75228 Ispringen, Germany (gateway to the Black Forest). The live English hub is dentaurum.de/eng. Company materials and the contact footer also use info@dentaurum.com / sales@dentaurum.de. www.dentaurum.com redirects to the .de site—use the .de English hub as the canonical official URL, not a separate renamed domain.

How the names stack (verified, not a rebrand story):

  • 1886 — Swiss dental technician Arnold Biber founds a dental laboratory in Pforzheim. Official 140-year timeline and home copy: continuous dental company since day one; still family-owned; “oldest dental company worldwide that has existed continuously” is company positioning.
  • 1908 — After Biber’s death, Dr. Fritz Winkelstroeter takes over; the firm continues under the Arnold Biber name while expanding into practice equipment.
  • 1923 era — Precision engineering and chemical production operate under the name Dentaurum (previously Arnold Biber); first IDS Berlin participation is dated on the celebrate timeline. Do not invent a modern rename—Dentaurum remains the current brand and legal trade name.
  • 1968 / later Ispringen campus — Production, research, and administration move/expand to Ispringen (Turnstrasse). That Ispringen address is current HQ on the live site footer.
  • Today — CEO named on official celebrate copy: Mark Stephen Pace. Segments on the home page: orthodontics, implantology, prosthetics, and ceramics. Orthodontics is a full specialty lane (brackets through lab acrylics), not an afterthought SKU.

U.S. path: Dentaurum Inc., 2050 Cabot Boulevard West, Suite 100, Langhorne, PA 19047; phone 1-800-523-3946; eShop at shop.dentaurum.us. Order through USA / local reps; country availability varies (site disclaimer on product pages).

This is a fixed-appliance and lab specialty house that also sells prime4me® digital/aligner-adjacent products and a separate tioLogic® implant line. For Ortho Index, the chairside core is brackets, wires, tubes, expansion, acrylics, and TADs.

Who it’s for

  • Specialists who want a deep German catalog—metal and ceramic twins, self-ligating, lingual, wires, tubes, bands—from one manufacturer rather than a mega-vendor aligner stack
  • Offices that still run removable appliances (Orthocryl® acrylics + expansion screws) and care that lab and chairside SKUs come from the same house
  • Teams needing a published nickel-free / titanium map (equilibrium® ti, rematitan® arches and screws, titanium RETAIN3R, nickel-free product tiles on the orthodontics hub)—then verify lots and IFUs
  • Doctors stocking Roth or McLaughlin–Bennett–Trevisi-style kits as listed in current discovery / SL assortments (Dentaurum’s catalog notes that its MBT-style versions are not claimed as endorsed duplicates)
  • Practices adding skeletal anchorage (tomas®) or amda® molar distalization without changing bracket vendors
  • Groups evaluating prime4me® aligners / RETAIN3R / printed models as an optional digital lane beside fixed appliances
  • U.S. offices that will order through Dentaurum Inc. (Langhorne, PA) or the USA eShop, not assume every European SKU ships stateside

Notable products

Official names below match the live English orthodontics hub, product pages, and Edition 24 catalog PDFs as researched September 2026. Availability varies by country. Slot sizes, prescriptions, and kit contents belong on the current catalog or with your Dentaurum / USA rep—not on this page. “Smallest,” “almost invisible,” “significantly reduce chair time,” and similar lines are manufacturer positioning.

discovery® family (premium metal / ceramic / SL / lingual)

  • discovery® smart — current conventionally ligated metal premium twin (MIM); low In/Out called out at 0.7 mm on product materials; laser-structured base with FDI notation; blister packaging option described on the smart sl page (from 2018 for discovery smart).
  • discovery® smart sl — newest passively self-ligating addition to the discovery line (home + dedicated product page, 2026 messaging). Lid closes incisal→occlusal with an audible/tactile #CLICK & GO; special opening instrument; MIM at Ispringen; positioned as compatible In/Out with other discovery brackets (no compensation bends per vendor). Pairing product: Ortho-Cast sl self-ligating buccal tubes.
  • discovery® sl and discovery® sl 2.0 — earlier / parallel SL metal lines still listed in Edition 24 SL catalog materials (Roth vs MBT-style assortments). Confirm which generation you are reordering—smart sl is the current homepage hero, not a silent rename of sl 2.0.
  • discovery® pearl — all-ceramic twin via CIM (highly pure aluminum oxide, one-piece, no metal slot insert); laser-structured base; colored positioning guides; translucency / “natural aesthetics” are vendor claims.
  • discovery® delight — lingual system (catalog/flyer family); vertical anterior / horizontal posterior slot concept; rematitan® LITE and remanium® lingual arches listed as companions. Confirm live SKU set with your rep.

equilibrium® family (classic metal / titanium)

  • equilibrium® 2 and equilibrium® mini — one-piece milled stainless-steel twins (no solder per catalog); mini positioned as substantially smaller than equilibrium 2.
  • equilibrium® ti — pure titanium, nickel-free classic twin; catalog ties it to Dentaurum’s longer titanium experience from prosthetics/implantology.

Other SL names in current catalogs

  • dinamique® m and dinamique® c — appear in Edition 24 SL instrument tables alongside discovery sl lids. Treat as catalog-listed SL options; confirm whether they remain active purchase SKUs in your market before stocking.

Buccal tubes, bands, wires

  • Ortho-Cast / Ortho-Cast M-Series mini / Ortho-Cast sl — bonded buccal-tube families paired with discovery in demos and product pages.
  • dentaform® and related bands; welding service for brackets/tubes/auxiliaries onto bands is described on pearl and related pages.
  • Archwire families named on materials lists and hub copy include rematitan® (incl. rematitan® sl, rematitan® LITE, rematitan® SPECIAL), remanium®, Tensic®, Equire, dentaflex®, Noninium®, remaloy®—stainless, NiTi/thermoactive, titanium, and specialty options. Match wire metallurgy to allergy and prescription needs on the current wire list.

Lab appliances and acrylics

  • Orthocryl® and Orthocryl® LC — cold-cure / light-cure orthodontic acrylics for removable appliances; Orthocryl® Pink called out as a new color on the 2026 home page; celebrate timeline credits Orthocryl with opening the removable-appliance chapter (salt-and-pepper technique).
  • Expansion screws — broad stainless and rematitan® (pure titanium) lines; Mini / Medium / Maxi / Magnum and specialty / RPE options in expansion PDFs; designed for acrylic retention (holes in screw body) with Orthocryl workflows.
  • Bender II — retainer production equipment tile on the orthodontics hub.
  • Premium-Line pliers and practice/lab equipment — instruments hub on the same page.

Anchorage and Class II / distalization

  • tomas® — temporary orthodontic micro anchorage system (TAD) on the orthodontics hub; celebrate copy pairs tomas® & amda® as skeletal anchorage + distalization.
  • amda® — advanced molar distalization appliance.
  • Class II appliances category also listed on the orthodontics hub (confirm named SKUs in the current catalog).

Digital / aligner-adjacent (prime4me®)

  • prime4me® Aligner — Dentaurum’s splint/aligner concept on the orthodontics hub.
  • prime4me® RETAIN3R — customized 3D retainer; official news: titanium Grade 5, milled (not bent), positioned for nickel-preferring patients; country rollout expanding.
  • prime4me® model printing — base / dental-arch models.
  • Hub also names partner-style digital tiles (TRIOS scanning, OnyxCeph³™ software). Confirm who invoices and supports those tools—do not assume Dentaurum manufactures every named digital SKU.

Implantology (company breadth, not the ortho formulary core)

  • tioLogic® TWINFIT, tioLogic® DIGITAL, CITO mini® — implant systems on celebrate / implant pages. Relevant as proof Dentaurum is a multi-segment dental manufacturer; not required for a brackets-first formulary.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Genuine specialty depth: vestibular discovery / equilibrium brackets, ceramic pearl, SL (including current smart sl), lingual delight, wires, tubes, bands, expansion screws, Orthocryl lab acrylics, tomas TADs, and a Dentaurum-branded digital lane—without pretending to be Align.
  • Clear “Made in Germany / Ispringen” manufacturing story for core MIM/CIM brackets; laser-structured bases and FDI marking are consistently documented across discovery materials.
  • Nickel-allergy / titanium path is unusually concrete for a specialty catalog (equilibrium ti, rematitan arches and expansion screws, titanium RETAIN3R, dedicated nickel-free hub tile)—useful as a shopping map if you still verify IFUs and lot materials.
  • Lab + chairside under one roof: Orthocryl + expansion screws remain a real removable-appliance workflow, not a historical footnote.
  • U.S. subsidiary with published address, toll-free number, and eShop; legal German HQ street address is on every page footer.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Naming leftovers will trip purchasing. discovery sl vs sl 2.0 vs smart sl, dinamique vs discovery SL, equilibrium 2 vs mini vs ti, and historic Arnold Biber / Remanit→remanium timeline all still exist in PDFs. Shop the live hub and current Edition catalog—not a 2015 flyer.
  • Catalog surface area is huge (prescriptions × discovery vs equilibrium × SL generations × wire alloys × lab colors). Startup offices need a formulary discipline or they will overstock.
  • prime4me® and third-party digital tiles (TRIOS, OnyxCeph) are not “grab a box of brackets” products. Country availability and portal access need a yes from your market before promising aligners or RETAIN3R to patients.
  • Implantology (tioLogic) and ceramics (ceraMotion) are real company divisions—do not confuse implant SKUs with ortho TADs (tomas), or assume every Dentaurum invoice line is orthodontic.
  • Chair-time cuts, “gentle movement,” “almost invisible,” and “oldest continuous dental company” are vendor positioning. Use IFUs and your own mechanics.
