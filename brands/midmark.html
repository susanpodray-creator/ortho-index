Midmark

Clinical environmental design company for dental operatories—next-generation chairs, delivery systems, LED lights, Synthesis and Artizan Expressions cabinetry (including ortho delivery), M9/M11 sterilizers, QuickClean ultrasonics, mechanical-room air/vacuum, Preva intraoral X-ray, and Smart View connected equipment management.

HQ: Versailles, Ohio, USA · Founded 1915 · Researched 2026-09-08 · Official site

Overview

Midmark is a U.S. clinical environmental design company that builds medical, dental, and animal-health care environments. For orthodontic offices, the useful part of the portfolio is dental operatory equipment and infrastructure—patient chairs, instrument delivery, operatory lighting, clinician seating, clinical cabinetry (including ortho-specific delivery cabinets), instrument processing, mechanical-room air and vacuum, and intraoral radiography—not brackets, wires, or aligners.

The live dental site is midmark.com/dental. Midmark’s history page places the company’s start in 1915 as the Cummings Machine Company in Minster, Ohio; the Midmark name arrived with the move into healthcare manufacturing and the relocation to Versailles, Ohio, in 1969. Dental expansion is tied to the 1997 purchase of Knight Manufacturing. A July 2025 Midmark news release describes the headquarters and innovation hub as located in Versailles, Ohio, which is the hq used on this page. Older public language sometimes says “greater Dayton” or notes a prior Miamisburg/Austin Landing office move; treat Versailles as the current published headquarters/innovation hub, and confirm any remittance or legal address on the formal quote.

What Midmark is—and is not—for an ortho buyer:

  • Is: a single-source lane for operatory build-outs, sterilization centers, mechanical rooms, and chairside intraoral X-ray hardware, with design/layout support and connected-equipment tooling under Smart View™.
  • Is not: a CBCT / panoramic / cephalometric imaging vendor in the Carestream sense, and not an orthodontic appliance manufacturer. Extraoral imaging and aligner/bracket systems come from other brands.
  • Portfolio note: Midmark also sells medical and veterinary products. This page covers the dental stack that shows up in ortho operatories.

Names below match live Midmark Dental pages researched September 8, 2026. Availability, mounting options, warranties, Smart View plan features, and distributor terms vary by configuration and territory. Manufacturer positioning is not an outcome guarantee.

Who it fits

  • Orthodontic startups or remodels that need chairs, delivery, lights, stools, and cabinetry planned as one operatory platform rather than pieced from unrelated vendors
  • Practices that want ortho-oriented delivery cabinetry (Artizan Expressions Ortho Delivery) with integrated doctor/assistant delivery instead of a conventional GP treatment cart alone
  • Offices standardizing instrument processing around Midmark M9/M11 sterilizers, QuickClean ultrasonics, and Synthesis or Artizan sterilization-center cabinetry
  • Groups that care about ergonomics—vertical chair travel, left/right rotation, stool posture, and light positioning—because bonding and finishing days are long and repetitive
  • Multi-site or compliance-minded teams evaluating Smart View for connected sterilizer cycle records, device status, and software updates through the myMidmark portal
  • Buyers who also need mechanical-room compressors/vacuums and Preva wall/mounted intraoral X-ray, while sourcing CBCT/pan/ceph and scanners elsewhere

Notable products

Operatory chairs, delivery, lighting, and stools

  • Midmark® Dental Chair — current next-generation patient chair on a flexible platform Midmark describes as “premium features standard” rather than good/better/best tiers. Official specs highlight about 19” of vertical adjustment, under 6” of horizontal traverse, up to 30° left/right rotation, 500 lb maximum patient weight, optional heat/massage, double-articulating headrest, optional adjustable backrest, collision protection, and limited-seams/seamless upholstery options. Smart View connectivity is listed for software updates and performance monitoring.
  • UltraTrim®, UltraComfort®, and Elevance — still listed on the live Dental Chairs category page alongside the next-gen Midmark Dental Chair. Treat them as legacy/alternate chair families still visible for quote and installed-base conversations; confirm which family and upholstery package is actually on a current U.S. proposal rather than assuming every named chair is equally “next gen.”
  • Midmark® Dental Delivery System — next-gen delivery with chair-mounted left/right doctor or assistant units, 12 o’clock cabinet-mounted, and 12 o’clock column-mounted configurations. Midmark lists 4 or 6 handpiece positions (configuration dependent), independent coolant water per handpiece, a 2 L self-contained bottle plus facility-water option, waterline design positioned for biofilm reduction with ISO 16954 evaluation language, touchpad chair control when connected, cure-safe mode with the Midmark LED light, and optional integrated scaler / assistant evacuation. Smart View connectivity is listed.
  • Legacy delivery names — promotions and third-party distributor pages still reference Procenter and Asepsis 21®. The live next-gen focus is the Midmark Dental Delivery System; verify which delivery family appears on the quote.
  • Midmark® Dental LED Light — three-axis positioning, auto on/off, dual-side handle controls, high/medium/low intensities (listed >10k / >20k / >30k lux), cure-safe mode, CRI >90%, color temperature roughly 3600–6400 K, and mounting options including left/right chair, cabinet, ceiling, wall, and track (with optional monitor). Smart View connectivity is listed.
  • Midmark dental stools — operator and assistant stools positioned for posture and long procedures as part of the next-gen operatory set.

Cabinetry (including ortho)

Midmark publishes two clinical cabinetry collections, both described with a 10-year limited product warranty (plus a 1-year labor warranty on the operatory cabinetry overview):

  • Artizan® Expressions — wood-panel aesthetic collection with treatment stations, central stations, side cabinets, and sterilization centers. Customizable layouts/finishes; stainless-steel bases called out on treatment and ortho modules.
  • Synthesis® — Midmark positions this as the only full steel-frame operatory cabinetry made in the U.S., with modular treatment, central, side, cart/mobile, and sterilization-center configurations and seamless/spill-containing drawer language aimed at asepsis and durability.
  • Artizan Expressions Ortho Delivery Cabinets — explicitly marketed for orthodontic and pedodontic storage/workflow. Integrated delivery turns the cabinet into a treatment station; standard features include a five-drawer module, lift-up writing surface, outlet, soft-close hardware, and stainless-steel or mobile base options. Spec sheets reference Procenter specialty cabinet-mounted doctor/assistant delivery packages as a configuration path—confirm the exact delivery kit on the quote.
  • Synthesis mobile cabinets / carts — flexible point-of-care storage for multi-bay ortho layouts where fixed side cabinets are less useful.

Instrument processing

  • Next Generation M9® and M11® Steam Sterilizers — Midmark’s current dental autoclave family. Shared positioning: glove-friendly 5-inch touchscreen, multilingual UI, color LED status, automated cycle recordkeeping / user authentication / routine-care logging, and a re-engineered chamber rated for 25,000 cycles (Midmark states 2.5× prior models). The M11 detail page lists an 11” × 18” chamber. Optional accessories include data logger, thermal printer, VistaCool™ direct-to-drain, and cassette/pouch racks. Midmark warns that purchases outside authorized dental distributors void warranty/support eligibility.
  • QuickClean® ultrasonic cleaners — QC1, QC3/QC3R, and QC6/QC6R capacity ladder with frequency-leap cleaning language and digital controls; countertop and recessed (R) options on larger sizes.
  • Sterilization-center cabinetry — Artizan Expressions and Synthesis sterilization centers with dirty-to-clean visual cues (Artizan illuminated windows) and Corian® drainboard slide-outs rated to support sterilizers up to 200 lb.

Midmark’s sterilizer pages also note 2026 Dental Advisor Editors’ Choice / Top Product recognition and multi-year Townie Choice awards (including Ortho Townie language on the sterilizer category page). Those are third-party award citations, not clinical outcome guarantees.

Mechanical room

  • Smart Air Compressors — oil-less dental compressors with coalescing filtration and desiccant drying; published models P21 through P72 spanning roughly 1–10 users with listed CFM, tank, and electrical specs.
  • PowerVac® Dry Vacuums — oil-less/waterless dry vacuum family Midmark positions as quiet, up to 18” Hg, compact, upgradeable for larger user counts, and NFPA 99C Level 3 compliant, with multi-year pump/product warranty language on the product page.

Confirm headcount, simultaneous-use assumptions, utility requirements, and sound covers on the actual mechanical-room design—not from catalog max-user rows alone.

Intraoral imaging (boundary)

  • Preva™ DC and Preva™ 2.0 — current Midmark Dental intraoral X-ray systems on the live Intraoral Imaging category. Midmark emphasizes mounted, always-ready operation and drift-free arm positioning. Useful for periapicals and limited chairside films; not a substitute for panoramic/cephalometric or CBCT records common in orthodontics.
  • Intraoral digital sensors / Midmark Imaging software — Midmark has publicly discussed a Midmark Intraoral Digital Sensor System and Midmark Imaging (DICOM) software in support materials and earlier news. The September 2026 Intraoral Imaging discovery page led with Preva only. If sensors appear on a quote, verify current SKU, warranty/Sensor Safe terms, TWAIN/bridge behavior, and software lifecycle separately.
  • True Definition™ scanner — Midmark acquired 3M’s True Definition platform in 2019. Legacy manuals and some older digital-impression pages remain findable, and Midmark’s technical library groups True Definition with documentation that can look like end-of-support content. It should not be treated as Midmark’s current primary digital-impression offering without a current formal availability and support statement. Ortho digital scanning for aligners/records usually belongs in a dedicated scanner comparison (iTero and peers), not assumed from Midmark’s operatory stack.

Smart View™

Smart View is Midmark’s cloud-connected equipment layer via the myMidmark portal: device visibility, maintenance/status insight, software updates for connected operatory gear, and Sterilizer Manager cycle records/reporting (including biological-indicator data in the same record on the Smart View page). Midmark notes that some features require an active Smart View Plan, with trial language on the portal. Cybersecurity/IT checklists are published for practice IT teams. Connected value depends on what is actually enrolled and which plan features are purchased—not on owning Midmark hardware alone.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Broad operatory + infrastructure coverage: chairs, delivery, lights, stools, cabinetry, sterilization centers, sterilizers, ultrasonics, compressors, and vacuums can be designed as one clinical environment.
  • Ortho-specific cabinetry exists: Artizan Ortho Delivery cabinets are openly positioned for orthodontic/pedodontic workflows with integrated delivery—not only generic GP side cabinets.
  • Next-gen operatory platform is coherent: chair, delivery, and LED light share mounting/integration and Smart View language, which simplifies mixed-vendor finger-pointing during install.
  • Sterilizer depth: M9/M11 remain a widely recognized Midmark lane, with strong public documentation, authorized-distributor warranty rules, and connected recordkeeping options.
  • Design and layout support: Midmark Dental emphasizes human-centered design, sample layouts/AutoCAD resources, and in-house design assistance for new builds and remodels.
  • Clear warranty messaging on cabinetry: published 10-year limited cabinetry warranty is easy to compare on RFPs.

Cons / watch-outs

  • Not an ortho imaging or appliance ecosystem. Expect to pair Midmark operatories with separate CBCT/pan/ceph, scanners, PMS, and bracket/aligner vendors.
  • Naming spans next-gen and legacy lines. Midmark Dental Chair vs UltraTrim/UltraComfort/Elevance, Midmark Delivery vs Procenter/Asepsis 21, and Artizan vs Synthesis all change price, aesthetics, and service parts. Quote the exact SKUs.
  • Smart View is not free magic. Portal value and Sterilizer Manager features can depend on plan enrollment, network readiness, and IT approval.
  • Authorized-channel discipline matters. Midmark explicitly voids sterilizer warranty support for non-authorized purchases; similar channel rules can affect install quality elsewhere—ask before buying gray-market deals.
  • Capital and lead-time intensity. Full operatory + cabinetry + mechanical room packages are construction projects: utilities, flooring, infection-control flow, delivery access, and training matter as much as catalog photos.
  • Marketing claims need context. Ergonomic career-protection language, biofilm/waterline claims, drift-free imaging, award badges, and “better outcomes” copy are manufacturer or award positioning—not universal clinical results.
  • HQ/address wording has shifted over time. Use Versailles for hq based on current Midmark headquarters/innovation-hub language, but put the remittance/legal address from the contract on purchase orders.

Evaluation notes

  • Start from room count and workflow: open bay vs closed ops, bonding vs adjustment density, sterilization dirty-to-clean path, and whether ortho delivery cabinets or conventional chairs+carts fit the schedule.
  • Separate the operatory package (chair/delivery/light/stools), cabinetry package (Artizan vs Synthesis; ortho delivery vs mobile), instrument processing (ultrasonic + sterilizer + center), and mechanical room on the RFP so each can be compared.
  • For sterilizers, confirm chamber size (M9 vs M11), cassette strategy, printer/data logger/VistaCool options, Smart View Sterilizer Manager entitlement, and authorized distributor install/service.
  • If Preva is quoted, define which intraoral images the ortho office still needs in-house versus referral/CBCT pathways, and verify state registration and shielding rules for any new X-ray tube.
  • Ask which items are next-gen Midmark platform products versus legacy Ultra/Procenter/Asepsis configurations still being sold through transition promotions.
  • Validate service coverage, parts stocking, upholstery lead times, and cabinetry finish samples before locking finishes for a new build.

Bottom line

Midmark is a strong operatory and clinical-environment shortlist for orthodontic offices—especially when chairs, delivery, lighting, cabinetry, sterilization, and mechanical-room gear should come from one design-oriented vendor. The most relevant current lanes are the next-generation Midmark Dental Chair / Delivery / LED Light set, Artizan or Synthesis cabinetry (with Ortho Delivery options), M9/M11 sterilizers plus QuickClean, Smart Air / PowerVac mechanical-room products, and Preva for chairside intraoral X-ray.

It is not the brand to expect for panoramic/CBCT ecosystems or appliance systems. Keep those comparisons elsewhere, and make sure every Midmark quote spells out next-gen versus legacy model names, Smart View plan features, and authorized install/warranty terms.
