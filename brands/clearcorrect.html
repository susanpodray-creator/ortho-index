ClearCorrect (Straumann)

Straumann Group clear-aligner system from Round Rock, Texas — ClearQuartz material, ClearPilot planning, Doctor Portal, and tiered case packages for GP and ortho practices.

HQ: Round Rock, Texas, USA (Straumann Group; Basel, Switzerland) · Founded 2006 · Researched 2026-09-23 · Official site

Overview

ClearCorrect® is a clear-aligner brand under the Straumann Group. The company was founded in 2006 in Round Rock, Texas, and was acquired by Straumann in August 2017 for roughly USD 150 million (Straumann media release and contemporaneous GlobeNewswire coverage). Public Straumann materials still describe U.S. design and manufacturing around a Texas center of excellence; the parent group is headquartered in Basel, Switzerland.

Doctor-facing entry points today sit on Straumann’s ClearCorrect microsite (straumann.com/clearcorrect), with case work through the ClearCorrect Doctor Portal (dr.clearcorrect.com / Straumann eShop by region), planning in ClearPilot®, and support at support.clearcorrect.com. The consumer domain clearcorrect.com redirects into the Straumann ClearCorrect doctor pages.

This brief is for practices comparing clear-aligner labs, portals, and package structures. It is not patient marketing, a protocol, or medical advice. Case selection, compliance, and finishing sit with the treating doctor.

Who it’s for

  • Orthodontists and qualified dentists who want a full-service clear-aligner lab backed by a large implant/esthetics corporate parent (Straumann Group)
  • Offices that prefer tiered case packages (pay-per-step through multi-year unlimited windows) rather than a single flat pricing story
  • Practices already in — or considering — the Straumann digital ecosystem (e.g. SIRIOS™ scanners) who value direct ClearCorrect integrations alongside open STL upload
  • GPs and orthodontists evaluating an Invisalign alternative with U.S. manufacturing roots and global Straumann distribution

It is a weaker shortlist if you only need in-office print/CAD software with no lab relationship, or if your region’s package list, scanner path, or credentialing does not yet match what sales offers. Confirm eligibility, pricing, and refinement rules with ClearCorrect / Straumann — not from this page.

Notable products

Names below match ClearCorrect’s public 2026 doctor brochure, Straumann doctor pages, and support articles reviewed September 23, 2026. SKUs, package limits, wear schedules, and software features change by market; verify current doctor materials before you brief patients or sign a contract.

Aligner system

  • ClearCorrect® Premier Aligner — current public product framing for the clear aligner itself. Vendor materials highlight three pillars: ClearQuartz® tri-layer material, Performance Trimline, and ClearControl™ clinical features.
  • ClearQuartz® — proprietary tri-layer sheet (two resilient outer polymer layers + elastic inner layer) marketed for force retention, clarity, and stain/crack resistance. Material performance claims are manufacturer statements.
  • Performance Trimline — high / flat trimline described as extending beyond the gingival margin, with more than one height option in current brochure language, pitched for force application and control versus scalloped designs.
  • ClearControl™ clinical features — vendor umbrella for attachment/engager-style and related design features used in setups (confirm naming and availability in your territory).
  • ClearCorrect® Retainers — retention products in the same ecosystem (“Preserve” step in the 2026 digital-ecosystem diagram).

Case packages (public 2026 brochure)

Package names and step caps below are brochure summaries; regional T&Cs and fee tables differ. Ortho Index does not publish prices.

  • FLEX — pay for the exact number of steps planned; revisions, retainers, and replacements typically extra.
  • MINI — up to 12 steps; mild relapse / aesthetic-zone oriented; short treatment window (brochure: up to ~6 months) with limited revision inclusion.
  • ONE — up to 24 steps; milder Class I crowding/spacing style indications in brochure copy; ~1-year window with limited revision + retainer inclusion.
  • TWO — up to 48 steps; moderate discrepancies in brochure indications; ~2-year window with more revisions/retainers included.
  • UNLIMITED 3 / UNLIMITED 5 — as many steps as needed within 3- or 5-year treatment windows per brochure; unlimited revisions language, retainer allotments, and ClearCorrect RemoteCare called out on unlimited tiers. Older regional T&Cs may still list other package names (e.g. historic THREE / single UNLIMITED) — always check the live quote.

Software, portal, and digital workflow

  • ClearCorrect Doctor Portal — case submission, order management, replacements, practice filters/labels, and collaboration entry point (dr.clearcorrect.com; Straumann eShop path in some regions).
  • ClearPilot® — 3D treatment-planning / setup review tool (visualization, analysis, 3D edits). Brochure mentions features such as occlusal heat map, CBCT visualization, Bolton analysis, and multi-tooth edits — availability varies by market.
  • Planning Concierge / Collaborator Suite & TPS — optional treatment-planning support and collaboration with ClearCorrect / partner TPS resources described in the 2026 ecosystem diagram.
  • Capture & monitor tools — open scanner platform with direct integrations cited for Straumann SIRIOS™ / SIRIOS™ X3, 3Shape TRIOS®, and Alliedstar™, plus STL upload; ClearCorrect Sync App for photos/case management; RemoteCare and scan-compare style monitoring on higher packages.
  • Ortho Campus — Straumann/ClearCorrect online learning (vendor education, not Ortho Index content).

Always verify territory rules, scanner/upload paths, package upgrade rules, refinement fees, and current SKU naming with ClearCorrect / Straumann directly.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Straumann Group backing — large, long-running dental manufacturer with global distribution, education (Ortho Campus), and adjacent digital hardware (SIRIOS), useful if you already buy Straumann or want one-vendor relationship leverage
  • U.S. manufacturing story still emphasized (Round Rock / Texas center of excellence) after the 2017 acquisition — worth confirming live SLAs on any contract
  • Clear package ladder (FLEX → unlimited multi-year) that lets offices match fee structure to case complexity instead of forcing every case into one SKU
  • Mature doctor portal + ClearPilot planning stack with open scanner intake, not only a single proprietary scanner lock-in
  • Long market presence since 2006 as a named clear-aligner brand (not a thin white-label reseller narrative)

Cons / watch-outs

  • Consumer brand recognition in many U.S. markets still trails Invisalign; case-acceptance conversations may need more doctor explanation
  • Marketing language (“premier,” “proven results,” “scientifically-proven,” predictable outcomes) is vendor messaging. Material and trimline claims are not independent Ortho Index tests
  • Package complexity (step caps, revision windows, RemoteCare inclusions, regional T&C variants) means staff must learn the ladder carefully or risk surprise fees — get the current quote sheet in writing
  • ClearPilot / CBCT / editing features are not identical in every market; brochure footnotes say some tools are unavailable in all regions
  • Parent-company roadmap (Straumann orthodontics strategy, scanner push, eShop login quirks) can change portal UX and sales motion; diligence support responsiveness the same way you would for any national aligner vendor

Warm and direct: ClearCorrect is a clear-aligner lab relationship and digital toolset, not a promise. Ortho Index does not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.
