3Shape

TRIOS intraoral scanners (TRIOS 6, TRIOS 5, TRIOS 3, TRIOS Core), lab scanners, the free 3Shape Unite workflow platform, and orthodontic software (Ortho System with Ortho Analyzer, Clear Aligner Studio, Orthodontic Planner) from 3Shape A/S, headquartered in Copenhagen, Denmark, and founded in 2000.

HQ: Copenhagen, Denmark · Founded 2000 · Researched 2026-10-29 · Official site

Overview

3Shape is a Danish digital dentistry company best known for the TRIOS intraoral scanner line. The legal entity is 3Shape A/S, Holmens Kanal 7, 1060 Copenhagen K, Denmark. Public announcements from 3Shape and EQT give 2000 as the founding year and describe a company serving clinics and labs in more than 100 countries.

Ownership: 3Shape is privately held. In December 2021 the private equity fund EQT IX agreed to buy a 20% minority stake from the founders, who remained majority shareholders; the deal was expected to close in Q1 2022.

U.S. presence and FDA: FDA records list U.S. establishments under 3Shape Inc. (New Providence, New Jersey, and Morrisville, North Carolina), alongside 3Shape A/S, 3Shape TRIOS A/S, and 3Shape Poland in Copenhagen and Szczecin. FDA 510(k) clearances in 3Shape names include several for Ortho System (e.g., K152086, K163677, K161884, K171634, K180941), the TRIOS 5 caries-aid fluorescence function (K221249), TRIOS Ready Tip (K242103), TRIOS Dx (K260082, April 2026), Splint Design (K191911), Abutment Design, and Implant Studio.

Who it’s for

General dentists, orthodontists, and dental labs that want a widely supported intraoral scanner, plus practices and labs that plan clear aligners or do digital model analysis in-house.

Notable products

  • TRIOS intraoral scanners (current lineup on 3shape.com): TRIOS 6 Wireless (newest; ships preconfigured, pairs with Dx Plus AI visualization software), TRIOS 5 Wireless (pen-grip, ScanAssist), TRIOS 3 Wired, and TRIOS Core Wired (lower-cost, send-to-lab focused).
  • Lab scanners: F8 and the E Series (E2 to E4).
  • 3Shape Unite: the workflow platform included free with TRIOS; Unite Cloud storage, lab connection, and a Unite Store of 100+ partners (aligner companies, printing, practice management). A 2-year support lifecycle applies to Unite versions.
  • TRIOS Care: service plan; first year includes onboarding, a 5-year warranty with drop coverage, express replacement, and 10 TB cloud storage (10 GB without it).
  • Ortho System (orthodontic software suite): includes Ortho Analyzer (model analysis and virtual setup) and, since its 2019 release, the Clear Aligner Studio workflow for setup, staging, and production prep. Clear Aligner Studio is now marketed as its own product page (the old ortho-system URL lands there). 3Shape’s site also shows a notice that Ortho System products are no longer sold in some regions; availability depends on country.
  • 3Shape Orthodontic Planner: diagnosis and model analysis (Bolton, overjet/overbite, spaces), merging TRIOS and CBCT data.
  • 3Shape Automate: pay-per-design AI cloud service for crowns, copings, models, and nightguards. It is a CAD service, not orthodontic planning software.

Pros / cons (honest)

Pros

  • Mature, widely integrated scanner line with several price tiers (TRIOS Core up to TRIOS 6)
  • Unite is included with TRIOS and connects to many aligner, lab, and printing partners
  • Deep ortho toolset (Ortho Analyzer, Clear Aligner Studio, Orthodontic Planner) with FDA 510(k) history for Ortho System
  • Long-established company with a U.S. subsidiary and FDA-registered U.S. sites

Cons

  • Pricing isn’t published; you’ll need a reseller quote, and TRIOS Care is a recurring cost after year one
  • Product naming overlaps (Ortho System, Ortho Analyzer, Clear Aligner Studio, Orthodontic Planner), which makes it harder to know which license you need
  • 3Shape notes that Ortho System products are not sold in every region; confirm U.S. availability before planning around them
  • Unite’s 2-year support window means you’ll need to keep upgrading

Informational only. No outcome guarantees; follow manufacturer IFUs and your clinical judgment.

How it fits in a practice

In an orthodontic office, a TRIOS scanner usually replaces impressions for records, retainers, and aligner or appliance submissions. Scans flow through Unite to a lab or aligner partner, or stay in-house for analysis in Ortho Analyzer / Orthodontic Planner and aligner setup in Clear Aligner Studio, then to a 3D printer. When comparing quotes, ask your reseller which ortho licenses are sold in your country, and what TRIOS Care costs after the first year.
